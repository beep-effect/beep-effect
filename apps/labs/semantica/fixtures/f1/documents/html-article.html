Drift-Aware Herbarium Linking

By Elian Quill and Sora Tern

Abstract

Elian Quill, affiliated with the fictional Alderwake University, proposed the Mossline Alignment Method. Sora Tern at the invented Lattice Herbarium curated the Glass Fern Dataset used to evaluate it.

Method

The Mossline Alignment Method links specimen labels to collection events while preserving the sentence that states each collector, institution, and dataset relation.

Result

Alderwake University adopted the method for the Glass Fern Dataset, and Lattice Herbarium published the resulting fictional Mossline Relation Ledger.

Quill and Tern, Synthetic Botanical Informatics 7 (2025)
