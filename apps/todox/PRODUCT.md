# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Senior wealth-management buyers at established firms evaluating a public
category site. The narrative must work across five lenses without becoming
five sites:

- producing advisor: current client context and useful meeting preparation;
- operations leader: continuity, ownership, and handoffs;
- compliance and privacy: evidence, review, policy, and action boundaries;
- innovation or technology: fit around the installed stack and approved models;
- executive: a narrow proof of differentiated operating leverage, not another
  platform-transformation claim.

They already have CRMs, planning tools, document systems, compliance
processes, meeting technology, and an AI roadmap.

## Product Purpose

`apps/todox` is the public marketing site for Todox (site: todox.ai), a
vision-only concept for a firm-controlled agent runtime around
wealth-management work. The site makes the concept vivid — a living,
source-linked client index becoming a reviewable meeting-preparation packet —
without claiming deployment, and converts interest into one action:
**Request a private walkthrough**. Success: a qualified buyer understands the
source-to-review mechanism and requests a walkthrough.

## Positioning

Control, evidence, and ledgers made tangible:

- firm-controlled data and action authority;
- policy-approved model calls with bounded context;
- versioned source spans and supersession;
- named, scoped action authorization;
- an activity record that stays attached to the work;
- existing systems of record remain authoritative — Todox is infrastructure
  around the firm's stack, not another capture bot.

These are intended mechanisms and hypotheses, never claimed as "only" or
"first." Not a meeting recorder, copilot, or "more than a notetaker" framing —
competitors already occupy those moves.

## Operating Context

Advisor work spans client relationships, households, entities, documents,
email, meetings, specialists, tasks, and systems of record. The expensive
failure is losing why a piece of work exists, which source supports it, what
changed, who reviewed it, and what may safely happen next. The flagship
scenario starts at a familiar moment: preparing for the next client meeting.

Repository truth: wealth management is a dormant synthetic proof fixture (law
is the active vertical). An executable, package-contract-tested synthetic
wealth fixture exists — an incoming cash-request email producing candidate
claims, tasks, a client-facing draft, a pending approval gate, and an
evidence-bounded context packet.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Vision-only concept: no deployment, integrations, customers, pilots,
  certifications, metrics, or outcomes may be claimed.
- Claim gate: every factual public assertion must resolve to a
  `publicEligible: true` record in
  `explorations/todox-wealth-management-site/research/CLAIMS.jsonl` and keep
  its caveat through the rewrite.
- Concept tense required where applicable: "designed to," "the proposed
  runtime," "the concept," or an explicit synthetic-demonstration label.
- Forbidden claims: deployed / generally available / pilot-ready; use,
  approval, or endorsement by a named firm or person; live CRM, custodian,
  email, calendar, document, portfolio, or MCP integrations; autonomous
  advice, tax conclusions, trading, money movement, or unsupervised client
  communication; SOC 2 or regulator approval; "no hallucinations," "every
  fact is true," "nothing leaves the device," full-explainability absolutes;
  any time-saving, AUM, adoption, ROI, or customer-result metric.
- All demonstrations must be visibly synthetic wherever a reasonable visitor
  could mistake them for client data, a live system, or a measured result.
- The public site never names or implies the researched account family or the
  supplied individuals.
- First slice: one responsive public narrative. No backend, connectors,
  login or session handling, CRM/custodian work, or autonomous-action features.
- Primary CTA fulfillment: email contact (confirmed); the exact address is
  still to be named — do not invent one.
- Dev server only through the portless-wrapped script (`bun run dev` →
  `http://todox.beep.localhost:1355`).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Todox; public site identity todox.ai (confirmed 2026-08-27).
- No existing logo, wordmark, palette, or typography commitments — the visual
  world is established later through Impeccable new-work.
- Reference material is reference-only: no third-party logos, wordmarks,
  screenshots, photography, illustrations, faces, proprietary fonts, exact
  palettes, copy, or compositions; no Mariner or AdvicePeriod trade dress.

  fields, AI brains, orbit imagery, glass command centers, dominant chat

## Product Principles

1. Evidence before assertion — show the mechanism (source span → candidate →
   review → receipt) instead of naming abstractions like "explainable" or
   "agentic."
2. Synthetic and labeled — demonstrations are proud concept proofs, never
   faux-live product.
3. The claim ledger outranks the copywriter — no sentence ships that cannot
   cite its claim record.
4. Existing systems stay authoritative — infrastructure around the firm's
   stack, never a rip-and-replace story.
5. One story, one action — client index to meeting-preparation packet;
   request a private walkthrough.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Responsive, keyboard navigable, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion
respected. Real-browser evidence (recorded browser QA loop) is required
before any later build is called complete.
