# The real mission is a compliance approval, not a colleague pitch

The workspace was created for Benjamin pitching agent adoption generally; he then
clarified the concrete goal: his friend (a wealth manager) needs her compliance officer
to approve Claude Code on her Claude Enterprise account, where Cowork is the assumed-safe
default. This reframes the material from "capability pitch" to "governance case":
capability beats stay, but the decisive arguments are data locality (files stay on the
firm-managed endpoint vs Cowork's Anthropic-hosted sandboxes) and administrability
(managed settings, allowlists, permission prompts), ending in a concrete scoped-pilot ask.

**Implications:** future lessons should target the friend's delivery (she is the one in
the room), and any demo lesson should be a wealth-management routine (quarterly packet
assembly) run under a restrictive permission policy — the demo doubles as evidence for
the pilot's audit story.
