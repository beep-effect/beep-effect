Look docket intake matters up in the practice knowledge graph and cross-check
entry dates against a CSV export of the attorney's docket sheet. Adds the
`MatterSuggested` lookup case, the `DocketTrackedDates` port, and the
`matter-suggested`, `tracked-date-differs` and `tracked-dates-unavailable` flags.
