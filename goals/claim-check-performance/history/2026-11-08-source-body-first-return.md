# Source-body rejection contracts: bounded observational trace

The source-body trace replayed two original exposed families, all 22 complete
records, through the production lexical runtime and supplied-input inspection
service. Both complete canonical report payloads stayed byte-identical to the
retained 48/538 implementation. There were two inspection calls and zero
acquisition calls. This is diagnostic evidence, with no new cohort score or
usefulness-gate credit.

The trace joined all 14 historical source-plan rejections, retained two successful
plans without false failure events, and retained six unattempted inputs without
inventing a SourcePlanner invocation. The first rejecting calls were nine
function-ownership checks, two nominal-gap checks, and one each of nominal
attachments, predicate attachments and auxiliary-PP admission.

The selected independent witnesses rejected at function ownership with 234,974
work remaining and at nominal attachments with 225,916 remaining. The mandatory
passing fallback control also rejected at function ownership, with 254,269
remaining. These examples support investigating the ownership and attachment
contracts with ample remaining allowance. They do not support admitting every
unowned verb, treating a source-plan rejection as an extraction error, or
assuming a rejected provisional frame is already proved.

The recorded guard identifies the actual rejecting call. In particular,
`function-unowned` does not distinguish the internal branches of `functionOwned`,
and `leaf-attachments` does not identify the internal refusal within `attachments`.
Those limitations remain explicit before any repair proposal.

Instrumentation retains the original helper calls, evaluation order and work
charges. Speculative screens, successful productions and non-applicable
alternatives restore their diagnostic state; a genuinely rejected recursive
child retains its failure. This prevents an abandoned trial from becoming the
reported cause of a later rejection. The preparation passed 53 synthetic
attribution assertions, one positive and 17 negative validator controls, and
one accepted and eight rejected exact-observer controls. Independent reviews
qualified the source, static closure, controls and actual observations.

The closure retained 1,845 original runtime pins with exactly two diagnostic
source substitutions, unchanged 94 lexical asset pins and Bun identity. Original
preservation and runtime verification passed before and after execution.
Production source, labels, scorer and previous results were not modified by
this experiment. The separate citation CI repair is a later change, not part
of this observation's runtime.
