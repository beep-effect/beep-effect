# Source-admission diagnosis after the 538-claim census

The retained product measurement is **48/538 strict, 537 parsed, 538 abstained**.
The diagnostics below do not add a passing claim or remove an abstention. All
48 observed passing identities remain mandatory for subsequent comparisons.

Two pinned, disposable observers replayed the original 60 complete families
(1,219 context records, 538 selected claims), using the production service and
tool routes. Each replay made 122 invocations. All 121 product output files were
byte-identical to the retained result. The second observer qualified 26,048
events without changing assets, work bounds, source selections or references.

All 60 global indexes built. No independent claim had an accepted source plan:
97 of 137 independent context claims declined role compilation, and the other
40 declined source planning. All 141 admitted dependent plans consequently
failed complete ancestor-context acceptance. Of the selected claims, 347 had a
role plan and 61 had a projected source plan; none acquired accepted complete
context. This locates the universal abstention in admission and ancestor
context, rather than a universal global-index construction failure.

The largest selected stop phases were reduced-clause role reading (118), source
body parsing (212), and source-token ownership (57). These counts are observed
stop phases, not evidence that the corresponding grammatical guards are wrong.
Only three selected role failures had negative remaining work; no selected
source-planner failure did. Increasing every work allowance is not supported by
these observations.

A separate five-family first-return probe retained all 87 complete context
records and the real membership and work accounting. Its five canonical report
payloads remained byte-identical. One witness reached an ordinary verbal
participle after a direct object; four other witnesses provided contrasting
failures, including one mandatory passing claim. A general adjacency rule was
rejected because selected object complements and reduced relatives can share
the same local pattern.

A further private certificate probe reused that same bounded five-family route.
It performed zero acquisition calls and again retained all five canonical
report payloads byte-for-byte. Independent metadata qualification checked all
24 private studies against their actual governor-failure events, shared work
accounting and unchanged parent registries. Exactly one study certified three
local heads and one finite governor, spending 69,193 units from the existing
allowance. Attachment alternatives remained unknown and public admission was
withheld. This is evidence for testing a narrowly source-certified lexical
boundary repair, not evidence of a uniquely determined attachment or improved
product score.

The candidate protocol is recorded separately in the prospective
[source-certified fallback decision](../decisions/2026-11-08-source-certified-fallback-term-refinement.md).
Any production change still requires the unchanged full-538 evaluation, all 48
passing identities, preserved input ledgers and original artifacts, independent
source review, and the original usefulness gates. The five-family probe cannot
substitute for that comparison.
