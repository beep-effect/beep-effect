# Source eligibility vocabulary

This records the existing semantic subgroup meanings for source reviewers.
It changes no gate, minimum, source selection, reference or numerical result.
The original `LABEL-PROTOCOL.md` remains authoritative for annotation; the
October31 nested-feature correction supplies the existing nesting definition.

- Nested: explicit containment below a top-level limitation, represented by
  a `part-of` edge whose parent is not the claim root. Punctuation/markup
  alone is insufficient; clear subordinate component/operation containment
  can occur without enumeration markers.
- Functional: a source-explicit functional relationship with identifiable
  participants, boundaries and ordered anchored parts, as in protocol item5.
  Do not infer unstated function or perform claim construction.
- Dependent: the source incorporates an earlier claim as claim context.
- Alternative multiple-dependent: the source incorporates one of at least two
  earlier claims as alternative branches. Conjunctive references, merely
  displaying other claims, and ambiguous ranges do not establish this stratum.

Prescreens record positive source witnesses and uncertainty only. They are
not final membership or reference trees. Final subgroup membership needs
independent source-grounded reference annotation and critic adjudication
before predictions. Unknowns remain conservative and cannot fill a minimum.
Canceled source nodes remain in full source context and are explicitly
identified before any substantive cohort selection.

Sources: `goals/claim-element-antecedent-check/research/LABEL-PROTOCOL.md`
and `goals/claim-element-antecedent-check/history/2026-10-31-nested-feature-correction.md`.
