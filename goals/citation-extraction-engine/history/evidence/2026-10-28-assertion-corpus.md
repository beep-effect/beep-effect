## Loop accounting and tracing correction

The capture retains 8,488 iterations. The largest loops are 6,805 tokenizer
extractor-construction entries, 703 attribute comparisons, 580 tokenizer choices,
194 citation-test rows and 77 resolution rows. All 23 source loops are observed.
There are 241 runs over identified literal parameters. Each run's item indices
must be exactly `1..itemCount`, using the independent AST collection size.

An initial trace counted a `with` body line more than once and produced
self-parent iteration chains. That intermediate capture was rejected. The
exporter now arms an iteration at its loop header and consumes it once at the
body entry. Parents must be strict source-enclosing loops. The validator checks
parent ordering, function/invocation ownership, unique IDs, consecutive ordinals,
producer references and complete literal-loop item sequences. The corrected
580 tokenizer iterations replace the inflated 1,740 intermediate count.

## Remaining scope

Source literals include unused arguments and helper data, not just semantic
engine cases. Literal-loop coverage proves observed parameter runs, not that all
source-defined capabilities have cases. The final canonical ledger still needs
semantic case IDs linking operations, assertions, literal rows and supplemental
probes. Required public/model/helper behavior outside these suite observations
must be identified and exercised. Donor dispositions, vocabulary-version
mapping, regex strategy, resource limits, implementation, raw-source anchoring,
transformation laws and the one-PR hosted closeout remain required.
