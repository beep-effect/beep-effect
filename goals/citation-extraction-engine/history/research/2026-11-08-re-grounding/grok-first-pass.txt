# Research result

**Status: Partial**

The extraction contract is still exhaustive observable behavioral parity with Free Law Project eyecite 2.7.6 at commit 04d82c032ad5fd0f9ab72a61c87110c46ee8f52e for `clean_text`, `get_citations`, `resolve_citations`, and `annotate_citations`, including every upstream fixture, by porting behavior and coverage rather than donor layout or classes, with eyecite-ts and eyecite-js only as differential or extension references; algorithms may differ from Python, but the engine may not require Python, eyecite-ts, eyecite-js, a hosted parser, native Hyperscan, or privileged off-box text at runtime. [S1][S9] The smaller shape already on record is an engineering choice, not a license duty: vendor or port the BSD citation algorithm and reporter/court data with attribution, or reimplement them from spec, while only CourtListener AGPL application glue must be rebuilt from specification; skip native Hyperscan for a literal-substring prefilter plus per-extractor JavaScript RegExp; and treat statutory and journal forms as optional V1 scope for the hallucination guard. [S2] Keeping those capabilities separate cuts v1 to three lanes and excludes court-data ingestion or taxonomy, claim-admission vocabulary changes, matter-wall enforcement, general prompt-injection defense, citator/"good law" computation, rich-text annotation, and hosted enrichment, and the extraction non-goals likewise exclude hosted parsing/grounding truth, CourtListener enrichment, citator/good-law, claim lifecycle, matter-wall orchestration, editor UI, and MPEP patterns unless separately accepted, leaving those items gated follow-ons. [S14]

Upstream eyecite is the maintained line and is already past the pinned release. The JavaScript and TypeScript ports are not finished stable releases and do not close that parity gap. CiteURL is a different product from the four pinned operations. unitedstates/citation is the dormant outlier.

### Resolution drops ambiguity; lookup can return it

Pinned resolution drops a citation that cannot be resolved to exactly one resource and does not record an ambiguous or failed state; the short-case, supra, reference, and id resolvers return no resource unless the match is unique and valid. [S10] CourtListener citation lookup is a different surface: ambiguous case citations come back as status 300 with multiple normalized forms and clusters, as with `1 H. 150` returning three normalized reporters and two clusters, and that API does not look up statutes, law journals, id, or supra citations, which it leaves to eyecite. [S11]

### Throughput and what a green suite does not prove

The only published extraction-throughput figure inspected is a Hyperscan-era estimate of about 10 MB/second on typical legal text, with 99.9977% recognition of the citations measured, and with no worst-case guarantee because extraction and annotation may take exponentially long on worst-case inputs, so external time limits are recommended for potentially malicious input. [S12] Green runs of the pinned TypeScript implementations exercise their own suites only: the 2026-10-28 donor receipt records eyecite-ts at 4,844 passed, 10 skipped, and 8 todo, and eyecite-js at 423 passed, while all 5,674 disposition obligations remain unresolved, none is claimed as target parity, and the successful runtime record proves the donor case only. [S13]
