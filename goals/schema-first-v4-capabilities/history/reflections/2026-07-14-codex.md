# Reflection — schema-first-v4-capabilities (2026-07-14, codex)

## Summary

The retained deliverable is P0–P2 doctrine, agent guidance, and structured
schema-first enforcement, plus the evidence from completed P3/P4 pilots. The
portfolio closeout converted an open-ended remediation program into explicit,
triggered deferrals and dropped the unsupported SchemaRepresentation spike.

## Tooling experience

- **Worked:** Structured `[schema-first:issue]` output and Yeet issue parsing
  turned doctrine into routable findings with rule, file, and remediation data.
- **Didn't:** One broad arbitrary advisory inventory mixed genuine schema laws,
  incidental codec tests, runtime blockers, and flaky generators.
- **Frustrating:** A long pilot list made the packet appear unfinished even
  after its teaching and enforcement thesis had shipped.
- **Wished existed:** Blocker-specific advisory states for runtime-limited,
  curated-generator-needed, exception, and not-yet-triaged cases.

## Implementation improvement opportunities

- Keep the diagnostics helper internal until another package repeats the same
  redacted formatting need.
- Reopen remediation from live advisory evidence, not from the historical list
  of every conceivable v4 capability.

## Goal & prompt critique

The goal should have named P0–P2 as the minimum deliverable and made P3/P4 an
evidence-driven adoption program. That framing would have made completed pilots
valuable proof without turning every remaining repo site into a closeout gate.

## TODOs worth codifying

- Track the five deferred arbitrary cases with their distinct reopening
  conditions.
- Preserve fixtures for golden and regression evidence beside schema-derived
  properties.

## Lessons (confidence-tiered)

- **HIGH — Critical:** Ship enforcement before exhaustive remediation — the
  structured Yeet path is the durable retained capability.
- **MEDIUM — Best practice:** Extract helpers after repetition — direct upstream
  APIs remain clearer when only one internal caller exists.
- **LOW — Consideration:** Drop speculative codegen spikes cleanly — unsupported
  SchemaRepresentation work should not linger as implied scope.
