# Reflection — oppold-corpus-refresh (2026-07-03, claude)

## Summary

The refresh closed its operational scope: four redacted sources were walked,
8,336 provenance records were accounted for, 12 new artifacts were copied,
7,284 cross-run duplicate sets were cataloged without restorage, and the
verified originals were archive-moved without deletion. The packet stayed inside
its SPEC boundary: extraction is deferred to a follow-up run.

## Tooling experience

- **Worked:** The dedupe-first design collapsed 99.86% of refresh records into
  provenance-only rows while still preserving every walked origin in the
  manifest. The run-union catalog gave a clean copied-count to distinct-digest
  reconciliation: 12 copied artifacts and 12 new distinct digests.
- **Didn't:** The manifest writer treated append as a whole-buffer write and
  could exit successfully after leaving record 1 as a NUL block. That failure
  mode should have had a regression test from day one.
- **Frustrating:** `fs.realPath` rejected valid POSIX filename forms with
  semicolons and backslashes in both salvage and archive-move flows, which made
  path safety feel less deterministic than the hash/copy logic.
- **Wished existed:** A verify mode aimed directly at a run manifest: row-count
  reconciliation, JSONL decode, copied-file rehash, provenance-only sampling,
  and first-record corruption detection before catalog union.

## Implementation improvement opportunities

- Promote the fixed scoped append handle into the shared manifest-writing path,
  with ordered writes, sync, and first-record corruption coverage.
- Add a packet-standard run-manifest verification command so agents do not have
  to assemble copied-artifact and provenance-only checks manually.
- Keep archive-move preflight fail-fast: prove full coverage over the walked
  source inventory before any mutation happens.

## Goal & prompt critique

- The SPEC did the important thing well: it explicitly superseded the retained
  packet's immutable-raw and never-move-source assumptions for this refresh.
- Future refresh prompts should require a run-manifest verification target by
  name, because catalog summaries are not enough to prove manifest integrity.
- The extraction deferral should remain explicit anywhere copied PST counts are
  reported, so recovered archive bytes do not imply extraction happened.

## TODOs worth codifying

- JSONL manifest writer corruption tests, including record 1 and sparse-hole
  behavior.
- Run-manifest verification command for `beep corpus`.
- Weird-filename regression fixtures for semicolons and backslashes across
  salvage and archive-move.

## Lessons (confidence-tiered)

- **HIGH — Critical:** Verify the manifest as its own artifact before deriving
  catalog state — a successful process exit did not prove record 1 was valid.
- **HIGH — Critical:** Dedupe-first salvage is the right default for refreshes
  over known corpora — it stored only 12 new artifacts while preserving all
  8,336 source observations.
- **MEDIUM — Best practice:** Fail-fast archive coverage before movement pays
  for itself — filename-resolution bugs surfaced before source mutation.
- **LOW — Consideration:** Keep the closeout language boring and aggregate-only
  for private corpus packets; counts and redacted labels are enough to prove the
  run without recording source names.
