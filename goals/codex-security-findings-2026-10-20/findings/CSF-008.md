## Implemented Remediation

Keep an 8192-unit budget shared across dependency traversal, path construction/copying and finding accumulation, with recursive depth capped at 128. Give indexed term evaluation a separate invocation-wide allowance of `min(262144, 8192 + 512 * locatedTermCount)`. Reserve three full input passes before constructing claim and global-introduction-head indexes; an incomplete index produces abstention. Query only chain-local buckets and the charged matching-head fallback, preserving the first global outside-chain candidate. Charge chain ordering, copying and matching before work; the fixed ceiling is unchanged while ordinary larger claim sets avoid repeated whole-input scans. Charge both budgets before work and return explicit `pattern-not-covered` findings under `chain-work-limit` on exhaustion. Preserve the global term set and `antecedent-outside-chain` lookup.

The ten-claim ordinary-input control, dense 25-claim DAG, wide-parent copy bounds and 512-claim scan-ceiling control pass.
