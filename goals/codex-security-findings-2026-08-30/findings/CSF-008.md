# CSF-008: Applied reap can race with admission and kill live work

- Severity: Informational
- Codex ID: `8c5243aa119881919c825d3a0f142ecd`
- Source commit: `48358da0367bda8c1fd865f63c9531883b00a97a`
- Owner: repo-cli quality admission scheduler
- Status: remediated; focused regression proof green

## Public Summary

Applied scheduler reaping treated a loaded run scope absent from one lease
snapshot as abandoned and stopped it after an uncoordinated systemd scan.

## Current-HEAD Validation

Confirmed on the current head before remediation. A new admission could
publish between the lease scan and unit scan, making live work look unowned.

## Remediation

Only scopes derived from a verified dead lease are now eligible for stop. The
nonce uses the full UUID, the recorded unit must match it, and any unit also
represented by a live lease is retained rather than stopped. Disabled or
unsupported records reap without a stop, while an already-collected systemd
unit is confirmed through its machine-readable `LoadState`. Absence from a
snapshot is never stop authority, the loaded-scope census capability was
removed, and repo law documents the same invariant.
