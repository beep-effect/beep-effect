# CSF-013: Process identifiers survive public corpus redaction

- Severity at capture: Low
- Codex ID: `200befcd89048191b24d8f64114b58e9`

- Lane: `L9-corpus-redaction`

## Public Summary

The corpus generators copied `attachedPid` and `ownerProcStart` from typed
scheduler records because their process-member filters omitted those names.
The residue scanners also accepted them. Stage A contained 178 such members;
Stage B contained 220. These are process identity values, not ordinary event IDs.

## Current-HEAD Validation

Disposition: remediate. Confidence: high. `RunScopeRecord.attachedPid` is populated
from the current process ID. `AttemptTerminationJournal` stores `ownerProcStart`.
A regression with nested records reproduced both omissions in all four generators
before the fix. The separate `failedStepId` event field must remain intact.

## Remediation

All four standalone generators drop these members recursively, including case,
underscore, and hyphen variants. Byte scanning rejects their residual keys in raw
JSON, escaped JSON text, and properties output without echoing the values.
The legacy generator's manifest now describes process-member removal explicitly.

The staged repair regenerated 26 Stage A and 32 Stage B raw files and their
projections, removing 398 members. Run-2, checkout identity, and synthetic payloads
needed no changes. All five manifests pin the updated generator and retain their
original CSF-012 repair receipts, with CSF-013 updates appended. Capture times,
record counts, custody references, and population-specific projections survive.

- Four `etl_*` generators and `test_run3_generators.py` in the corpus directory.
- Five corpus pins and their generated manifests.
- `research/scripts/resanitize-corpora.py` adds an explicit finding argument.
- Current integrity tables in the Stage A and Stage B reports.
