# Reflection — ci-fleet-residue (2026-08-13, claude)

## Summary

All four residue items shipped their codeable halves as scoped Yeet PRs in one
session: #686 (P3 closeout writer Option-encoding fix + nanoid advisory bump),
#692 (P1 dated re-defer, review-hardened P0/P2 design briefs, friction
receipts), #702 (P0 `beep runners bake` tooling, review-hardened and
coverage-ratcheted), #708 (P2 per-job IMDS hook wiring + lock cleanup). The
packet stays active: P0's live bake/activation, P2's Gate E + red-team re-run,
and P1's revert remain operator- or calendar-gated.

## Tooling experience

- **Worked:** the yeet loop end to end (publish/monitor/closeout/reply/sweep);
  the reply-drafts flow resolved 26 review threads across three PRs without a
  single manual GitHub round-trip; JsonStringCodec as the one-boundary pattern;
  Codex delegation for mechanical volume (implementation, tests, JSDoc).
- **Didn't:** package-scoped verification as a proxy for the global gates (two
  delegation rounds bounced); `bun update` for transitive bumps.
- **Frustrating:** monitor-phase exits after successful pushes (startup race,
  rate-limited externals); advisory-feed and base-range (commitlint #705)
  failures landing mid-arc and reading as regressions of the diff.
- **Wished existed:** the locked-transitive bump helper; a sealed-worktree
  signal during proofs.

## Implementation improvement opportunities

- The bake report/plan/check codecs and the closeout report now share the same
  JsonStringCodec discipline; Status.ts's other artifact readers could migrate
  to the same single-declaration boundary.
- The IMDS hook's fail-closed provision checks (iptables-nft + OWNER match
  verification) came from the concurrent session's hardening pass; fold that
  posture back into the P2 brief when the Gate E results land.

## Goal & prompt critique

GOAL.md was accurate and the hard rails did real work (the subshell-scoped
retest rail shaped the hook design directly). One improvement: state explicitly
which halves of P0/P2 are operator-gated so an autonomous session scopes to the
codeable halves from the start instead of discovering the boundary mid-arc.

## Lessons (confidence-tiered)

- **HIGH — Critical:** attribute before repairing — the bun-audit/security reds
  (fresh advisory), the commitlint red (base-range inheritance from #705), and
  the Vercel reds (rate limits) were all environment-class; none were fixable
  by editing the diff, and two were fixed by merging main or waiting.
- **HIGH — Critical:** delegated jobs verify against the gates that will
  actually run — name the global check, the law lints, and the ratchets in the
  brief, or budget the bounce cycles.
- **MEDIUM — Best practice:** batch review fixes and coverage work into one
  proof cycle; stopping the solo coverage publish to fold in 15 thread fixes
  saved a full proof and a Greptile round.
- **LOW — Consideration:** park side-work files outside the repo during any
  running proof; the worktree is effectively sealed until push.
