## Embedding identity and replay

The dimension is frozen at 1,536 from C1 onward. The selected identity is:

- provider: `openai`;
- model: `text-embedding-3-small`;
- revision: `text-embedding-3-small@2024-01-25`;
- artifact hash:
  `ef6cde7cc67e079a9a698268a31556afc576be71e79d97a12c57424ed0dbbd67`.

Every live result is schema-decoded as an `EmbeddingVector` before it can be
stored. Empty, missing, or dimension-mismatched results become the only
declared degraded state, `DegradedEmbedding`; they cannot be stored or replayed
as successful vectors. Offline replay does not acquire the OpenAI Layer and
fails typed on a cache miss.
