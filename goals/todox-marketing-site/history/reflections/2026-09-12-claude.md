check that runs each `apps/*/vercel.json` buildCommand in a fresh-clone
simulation (no `dist`, cached nested `node_modules`) would have caught both
before the first deploy. Recorded in memory as
`vercel-build-cache-stale-tsbuildinfo-skips-emit` and
`vercel-next-app-workspace-deps-need-turbo-build`.
