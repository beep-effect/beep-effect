keys. A future representation owner can supply a typed adapter, but inference
or taxonomy ownership would exceed this packet. Conflict class remains encoded
in versioned detector identities; a future contract owner may give it a typed
seat while preserving the current evidence-provenance vocabulary.

Proposal facts carry the other assertion's subject, predicate, value and
polarity. Any shape or semantic change must version the detector and preserve
the existing golden vectors as compatibility evidence.
