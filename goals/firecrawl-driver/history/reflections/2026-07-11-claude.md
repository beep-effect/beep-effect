# Reflection — firecrawl-driver (2026-07-11, claude)

## Summary

Retroactive closeout. The packet's mission was fully achieved: `@beep/firecrawl`
shipped to main in PR #210 (merged 2026-06-04) at `packages/drivers/firecrawl`,
with schema-first models (~4,700 lines), typed `FirecrawlError` variants,
an Effect service covering the in-scope method surface, live/fake Layers
(`Firecrawl.layer`, `makeLayer`, `makeLayerFromClient`), watcher-as-Stream,
unit tests, env-gated live integration tests, dtslint, and docgen docs. The
only failure was paperwork: the manifest stayed at "pending-implementation"
with P1-P8 "pending" for five weeks after the merge.

## Tooling experience

This is a paperwork reconciliation, not a fresh work log. Spot-checking the
source tree against each phase description (`git log --reverse` on the package
path, `gh pr view 210`, grep for `watcher`/`Stream`/`Layer` in the service)
was fast and conclusive — every substantive phase outcome demonstrably exists.

## Goal & prompt critique

The packet itself was strong (locked cutline, dual error model, watcher
semantics, explicit exclusions with rationale) — the implementation evidently
executed against it same-day without reopening any decision. The gaps were all
in closure design: no requirement to flip the manifest in the shipping PR, a
closure gate (Greptile 5/5) that left no durable record, and phase/quality
definitions coupled to tooling (repo-exports catalog) that was retired two
weeks later. See findings.

## TODOs worth codifying

See the `todos` block above — chiefly the goals doctor drift check and making
the manifest flip a required part of the shipping PR.

## Lessons (confidence-tiered)

- **HIGH — Critical:** Paperwork that is not flipped in the same commit as the
  outcome will drift; make the manifest/README flip an exit criterion of the
  final phase.
- **MEDIUM — Best practice:** Define packet quality gates in terms of the
  repo's stable quality operator, not specific commands that may be retired.
- **LOW — Consideration:** Ephemeral review scores are fine as gates only if
  the observed score is recorded into the packet at observation time.
