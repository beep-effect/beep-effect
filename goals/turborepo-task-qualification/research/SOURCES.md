## Evidence policy

- Prefer official documentation, immutable release/tag source, registry
  metadata, and the exact local source under audit.
- Keep exact stable, exact published canary, and unpublished `main@SHA`
  separate.
- Treat local clones as source mirrors whose live remote may have advanced.
- Mark source inspection as static; it cannot pass a runtime or deployed-parity
  gate.
- Cite historical opportunity packets only after reconciling them with HEAD.
- Preserve license boundaries: MIT sources may inform implementations under
  their terms; the MPL-2.0 Vercel SDK is reference/client material, not assumed
  reusable server code.
