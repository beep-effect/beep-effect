# Reflection — fallow-advisory-ratchets (2026-07-14, claude)

## Tooling experience

- **Worked:** The packet's native task-ledger `deferred` disposition made the
  descope legible without inventing new bookkeeping.
- **Didn't:** Mixed done/deferred work hidden under one in-progress phase flag
  misrepresented the packet in the generated index for weeks.
- **Frustrating:** Non-canonical phase status tokens written at closeout failed
  GoalManifest decoding and blocked the status flip until repaired.

## Implementation improvement opportunities

- When the reopening trigger fires, start from the shipped boundary-freshness
  wiring in `packages/tooling/tool/cli/src/commands/Quality/` rather than
  re-deriving the ratchet pattern.

## Goal & prompt critique

The packet bundled one proven ratchet retirement with two speculative policy
inventories under a single completion bar. A revised packet shape would ship
the proven ratchet as its own outcome and spawn inventory packets only from a
measured regression.

## TODOs worth codifying

- Keep advisory envelopes intact for security/fix-preview lanes; ratchets must
  narrow debt without blocking inherited findings wholesale.

## Lessons (confidence-tiered)

- **HIGH — Critical:** Manifest phase vocabulary is closed — encode won't-do
  outcomes in prose/ledgers, never as ad-hoc status tokens.
- **MEDIUM — Best practice:** Split mixed-progress phases when descoping so the
  generated index reflects what actually shipped.
- **LOW — Consideration:** Speculative policy inventories age poorly; author
  them when a trigger fires, not when a sibling ratchet ships.
