# PSI baseline and the MemAvailable-0 samples (2026-11-08)

## The recorder had stopped

`beep-psi-sample.service` had been failing on every tick since at least 2026-11-04. The
2026-11-02 storage reclamation retired `~/workstation-diagnostics`, which held both the
sampler and its output, and nothing repointed the unit. The timer still read `active`, so the
failure showed only on the service. The live parcel reads `/proc` directly and was unaffected.

The data survived in the reclamation's retained evidence, ending 2026-11-02T17:41Z. This
baseline uses the last 7 days on record: 19,530 samples, 2026-10-26T17:41Z to
2026-11-02T17:41Z. The exploration's figures (22,938 samples over a longer window) are close:
cpu `some` avg60 p99 30.5 there, 32.5 here. There are no samples from 2026-11-02 to 2026-11-08.

The sampler now runs from `~/.local/share/beep-psi/sample-safe.py` with the same record format,
writing `~/.local/state/beep-psi/safe-pressure.jsonl` with a 14-day ring. It still does no
per-process scans. Reversal is in the decision file.

## MemAvailable 0 is real, not a recorder artifact

Four samples of 110,974 read `MemAvailable: 0 kB`.

| Sample (UTC) | Previous, 30 s earlier | Next, 30 s later | SwapFree | memory `full` avg10 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-10-23T12:41:04 | 2.8 GiB | 84.9 GiB | 8.1 GiB | 18.75 |
| 2026-10-31T13:32:35 | 24.4 GiB | 4.9 GiB | 164 kB | 2.62 |
| 2026-10-31T23:09:19 | 7.4 GiB | 28.1 GiB | 6.8 GiB | 15.72 |
| 2026-10-31T23:32:27 | 21.3 GiB | 36.8 GiB | 12.4 GiB | 10.59 |

The sampler copies the `/proc/meminfo` value verbatim, so it cannot fabricate a zero, and
`0 kB` parses as an ordinary value. The kernel clamps its estimate at zero when free and
reclaimable memory fall below the watermarks. Three samples coincide with heavy memory stall,
and the fourth with swap all but exhausted. These were brief real exhaustion events.

Inference, not observation: the recovery of tens of GiB within 30 s suggests a large process
exited or was killed. The journal no longer holds those boots, so the cause cannot be confirmed.

Consequence: a zero is not noise to filter, but it lasts one sample and comes on quickly, so it
cannot serve as a threshold. The memory floor uses p1, which the four samples do not move.

MemAvailable: p1 13.0 GiB, p10 24.6, p50 46.9; the spill floor is <= 13 GiB.

Each threshold sits at its p99 band. CPU also needs avg300 at p95, so a single read can tell a
sustained load from a burst. Replayed over the baseline, the four PSI and memory rows together
trip on 3.60% of samples, in 254 episodes, 11 of them lasting 5 minutes or more; the longest
ran 35 minutes. A short breach costs one launch placed elsewhere, so that rate suits an advisory
rule. The admission row comes from the gate's own state, not this baseline.

`bun run beep palantiri parcel host-pressure --json` decodes and carries every field the table
names: `PressureStall` (`some` and `full`, each with avg10, avg60, avg300), `HostMemory.availableGib`
and `AdmissionSlots` (`queued`, `hardFloorEngaged`).
