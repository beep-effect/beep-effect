# Reflection — practice-kg-mcp (2026-10-06, claude)

## Summary

On demand, before the packet closes. Since P6 the goal moved the knowledge
graph from a July snapshot to the attorney's live working files and shipped it
to his PC four times (extension 0.2.1 through 0.4.0), with a stable
matter-lookup contract and a correspondent lookup for the docket-intake, mail
and Box workstreams. The engineering is in good shape; the open items need the
attorney (AC-6, G-1..G-5) and a wider correspondent source (-05).

## Tooling experience

- **Worked:** the stable lookup contract (`research/matter-lookup-contract.md`)
  let three other sessions build against the bundle without reading its code;
  `beep-heavy` made heavy corpus work safe on a shared workstation; SSH to the
  attorney's PC plus `--self-check` replaced relaying screenshots; yeet reply
  drafts made review rounds cheap; same-provider subagents with tight briefs
  did most implementation.
- **Didn't:** corpus salvage and extract (whole-file hashing, no resume, a
  source-label filter that spans runs); cheap gates that miss JSDoc and
  cache-policy findings the hosted lanes catch.
- **Frustrating:** the GitHub GraphQL quota shared by every session blocked
  thread replies for minutes at a time; desktop app restarts and account
  switches orphaned background watchers and merge gates.
- **Wished existed:** a bundle-to-bundle diff in `verify.ts`; a corpus
  pipeline that runs stage by stage with completion markers.

## Implementation improvement opportunities

- Add `verify.ts --compare-to` (see findings) and make it part of the build
  hand-off.
- Re-run USPTO enrichment for the working-files run; application numbers for
  new files currently come only from the text scans.
- Bring in the 19 held-out files and re-read the Tesseract-crash pages through
  the CPU per-page OCR path the GPU benchmark found.
- Feed the PST header index (lane E, about 241K messages) into the
  correspondent tables and fix the quoted-address miss (#1521) in the same
  refresh.

## Goal & prompt critique

- Add to GOAL.md: "The attorney's live working folder is the primary input;
  each bundle is checked against its consumers' joins before it replaces the
  previous one." Both defects that mattered would have been caught by that
  sentence.
- The non-negotiable "stop-and-report before schema/lockfile changes" was
  superseded in practice by the autonomy charter; the GOAL should point at the
  charter instead of restating a stricter rule.

## TODOs worth codifying

- `verify.ts --compare-to` and its use before announcing a bundle.
- `lint:jsdoc` on changed packages in yeet cheap gates.
- Headless `--self-check` for every shipped MCP host.

## Lessons (confidence-tiered)

- **HIGH — Critical:** diff every rebuilt bundle against the one it replaces,
  down to per-docket application and patent numbers, before announcing it —
  consumers found both regressions this goal shipped.
- **HIGH — Critical:** never edit a shared landing area; build a private
  hard-link view — moving 19 files broke the Box upload reading the same tree.
- **MEDIUM — Best practice:** a weak evidence source (register, org-name match,
  subject reference, message counts) may fill gaps but never overrides a
  document's own evidence or decides a match alone.
- **LOW — Consideration:** measure the distribution before choosing a
  threshold; the dominance rule (D-23) was set from 61 real anchors, not
  guessed.
