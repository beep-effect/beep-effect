## Public Summary

AdaFace derived an intermediate square allocation directly from detector
coordinates. Non-finite or extreme model output could request an enormous
array before the crop was resized.

## Current-HEAD Validation

- Verdict: confirmed at the packet's initial HEAD.
- Disposition: `remediate`.
- Lane: `L6-photo-bounds`.
- Owner: local photo-face worker.
- Rationale: coordinate trust crossed the model boundary before finiteness,
  image clipping, and allocation bounds.

## Remediation

Status: implemented. Detector coordinates must be finite and produce a
non-empty clipped box. A fixed-output affine warp writes directly to 112 by 112
pixels, and detection requests at most 32 faces per image.

Changed files:

- `packages/tooling/tool/cli/python/photo-face/beep_photo_face/backends/adaface_kprpe.py`
- `packages/tooling/tool/cli/python/photo-face/tests/test_adaface_backend.py`
