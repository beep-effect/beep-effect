# R36 owner reconciliation evidence

These are the bounded owner audits used to reconcile the completed R36 census.
The artifact index records separate hashes for original private artifacts and
published copies. Home paths are replaced with `~`; `.txt` suffixes preserve the
remaining captured content without formatter rewrites. Embedded hashes refer to
the original artifacts or to source at the frozen R36 revision, not necessarily
to these sanitized copies or the subsequently updated packet.

The toolbar disposition here is the final audit. Its earlier provisional
rejection was superseded after inspecting the pinned hook, registry, and React
implementations. The compatibility and transition tables support design
proposals; they do not prove a runtime migration or independent P3 approval.

The parent integration receipt records the accepted inventory and design changes.
Raw lane proposals remain historical evidence and do not override that receipt.
