The Admission.resolve contract takes a HoldId and a ConflictResolution and names no resolver, but HoldResolved and any revision it produces need an author. The layer writes HoldResolved, and a merged text, under the constant lineage agent-memory-store:resolve (AdmissionResolverLineage); a theirs resolution writes the held text under the lineage that submitted it, because the text is that writer's. Outcomes: ours appends only HoldResolved and returns rejected with heldConflict, since the held write was not applied. theirs and merged judge the held operation again on the node as the log holds it at resolve time and write on its current heads together with HoldResolved in one journal transaction, returning landed, unless the node moved on to another text since the hold, which holds the write again instead (decision resolve-re-holds-a-write-whose-node-moved-since-the-hold): a held revise writes its text (reviving a retired node), a held retire retires, a held status move re-runs the lifecycle guard, and merged writes the resolver's text as a revision. A held insert is created at the resolver's placement, or at its submitted one, after a retired predecessor if need be. When the re-judgment cannot apply the write (the predecessor is still missing, the node never reached the log, the status guard refuses) nothing is appended, the hold stays open, and resolve returns held (same HoldId) or rejected with the finding, so the resolver can choose again or close the hold with ours. The read model keeps ours as the text at the head revision the hold recorded and theirs as the submitted text, absent for a retire and for a status move, whose submission is a transition, not a text. Code: packages/tooling/library/agent-memory-store/src/Admission.layer.ts. Rejected: changing the resolve signature in P2 (the contract is implementable as written) and closing a hold whose resolution could not be applied (it would lose the held write).
