  repeat the fixture. This is test data, not a live Tailscale credential.
- #2, #3 and #4: imported article fixtures contain temporary AWS access-key IDs
  within third-party presigned PDF links, including session-token parameters.
  Their signing timestamps are respectively 2023-03-24 16:53:18 UTC,
  2024-07-09 16:55:23 UTC and 2024-07-26 18:48:37 UTC. Every link specifies
  `X-Amz-Expires=300`. Both historical copies were checked. These links and
  temporary credentials expired long before this audit; no request was sent
  to any signed link and no credential was used.

AWS documents that presigned links expire at their configured lifetime or
when their temporary credentials expire, whichever is earlier:
[Amazon S3 presigned URL lifetime](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonS3/latest/userguide/using-presigned-url.html).
Expiry is established from source timestamps and AWS semantics, not inferred
from GitHub's `unknown` validity field. Closing expired credentials does not
claim that the operator manually revoked them.
