# Reflection — effect-vitest-canon (2026-11-02, claude)

## Summary

The packet migrated every in-scope test and support module to the canonical `@effect/vitest` idioms behind a
syntax-only detector ratchet (`beep lint effect-vitest`), an instrumented runner and the promoted `@beep/memfs`
MemoryFileSystem. The final scan on 2026-11-02 reports 3,104 files, 447 findings, `introduced=0`, 0 open rows,
and every remaining row is an owned exception with a named exit. The operator amended D4 on 2026-11-02 (ruling on
#1875) to accept that register as the closeout bar, so the packet closes as `completed-retained` with the burn-down
of the exceptions handed to a successor goal.

## Tooling experience

- **Worked:** the committed baseline plus `introduced=0` ratchet made every wave's gate mechanical, and the
  owner/exit register (the #1855 waves 7 and 8 and #1875 waves 11 and 21 rows in the residue packet's Decision
  Log) turned "exception" from an escape hatch into a reviewed claim. `it.layer` sharing and `MemoryFileSystem`
  carried most of the EV002 and EV010 volume (waves 6 and 7, #2228 and #2250).
- **Didn't:** the "empty baseline" wording of D4 could not be met without deleting real native-subject tests, so
  the last seven EV010 rows (#2463) sat as a blocker until the operator ruled.
- **Frustrating:** `--write` re-keying moved rows to `open`; detector corrections (waves 12, 15, 17, 18) each
  needed a hosted round; the full scan runs for about 53 seconds in the cheap tier.
- **Wished existed:** an incremental scan, and a detector-side judge list per native rule that a lane could
  extend in the same PR as the migration. Also a lane-percentile read that does not go through the admission census:
  the timings report had to read `gh api` job records by hand.

## Implementation improvement opportunities

- Name every native subject class the EV010 and EV014 judges accept in one table next to the rules, so a
  reviewer can check a proposed exception against the list instead of against the rule's source.
- Preserve row identity across span moves in `--write`.
- Group the exception register by exit class in a generated digest; the successor goal starts from it.

## Goal & prompt critique

- GOAL.md says "the lint baseline is empty". It should have said "no open rows; every exception owned with a
  named exit", which is what every wave actually worked toward and what the amended D4 now says.
- The phase table (P1 inventory, P2 remediation, P3 close) did not describe twenty-one numbered waves across two
  months. A standing lane with dated wave rows, as the residue packet kept, was the truer shape.
- The GOAL still names a `/goal` launcher that does not exist; drive the packet from its files and
  `goals doctor`.

## TODOs worth codifying

- Successor goal: burn the 447 owned exceptions down by exit class.
- `--write` keeps status and reason across span moves.
- Incremental or cached full scan for the cheap-gates lane.

## Lessons (confidence-tiered)

- **HIGH — Critical:** State the closeout bar for an exception-bearing ratchet as "no open rows, owned exceptions
  with exits" from the start — the literal "empty baseline" bar delayed this packet's close by a ruling cycle.
- **HIGH — Critical:** Ship a native rule's judge list with the rule and extend it before migrating rows it misses —
  four of the last ten waves were detector corrections.
- **MEDIUM — Best practice:** Treat a repo-wide test migration as a standing lane with dated waves, not two
  long-lived phases.
- **LOW — Consideration:** Budget the full-scan cost in the cheap tier; 53 seconds per push adds up.
