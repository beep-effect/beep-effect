## Why this reached epistemic-bitemporal-edge-core

The routed insight is: “Add boundary fixtures for competing lineages, interpretation/adoption events, qualifier-complete assessments, revisions, and invalidation.” These are concrete cases for the target’s existing immutable payload, evidence-scoped identity, durable disposition, atomic supersession, lineage, and two-axis `asOf(validAt, knownAt)` contract; they do not require widening shared lifecycle state or installing a semantic graph in the authority path ([target SPEC](../SPEC.md#objective), [constraints](../SPEC.md#constraints)).

The owning report’s central convergence is that exact evidence and transition records remain durable while interpretations, preferred views, quality assessments, and semantic products remain replayable projections. In particular, competing sourced assertions must coexist, and supersession must close only the affected logical lineage rather than erase disagreement ([Memory/bitemporal — Design challenges](../../../explorations/academia-corpus-mining/research/t3-memory-bitemporal.md#design-challenges), `5c2aeef6919d` — Ontology Revision as Non-Prioritized Belief Revision; `2ebef49a0b91` — Construction of Knowledge Graphs: State and Challenges).

The corroborating clusters supply boundary cases missing from a generic correction example: adopted interpretations as exact institutional events; context-qualified acceptance and closure; source-support assessments that change when qualifiers or source versions change; OCR and structure corrections that invalidate dependent anchors; and policy, attack, or model-trust revisions whose ancestry must remain reconstructible. Align decision 1 makes this a current high-priority dispatch, while align decision 8 keeps it additive evidence rather than a binding-doc edit.

Align decision 3 fixes the first downstream composition: retained inconsistent evidence plus recoverable preferred working views. The present packet should therefore prove that its core can preserve the necessary histories and lineage partitions, but it should not implement preferred-view selection, contradiction triage, or belief repair, all of which remain outside the target’s stated non-goals.

## Distilled requirements

1. **Competing evidence scopes must coexist without cross-lineage closure.** A fixture passes only if two contradictory assertions with distinct evidence scopes remain queryable at the same valid and known times, while superseding one closes and replaces only that assertion’s logical lineage. This sharpens, without expanding, the SPEC’s evidence-scoped identity and no-overlap partition ([Memory/bitemporal — Design challenges 4 and Corroborations](../../../explorations/academia-corpus-mining/research/t3-memory-bitemporal.md#corroborations), `5c2aeef6919d` — Ontology Revision as Non-Prioritized Belief Revision; `2ebef49a0b91` — Construction of Knowledge Graphs: State and Challenges).

2. **Interpretation and adoption must be distinguishable immutable events.** A fixture passes only if source text, candidate interpretations, and an attorney-, agency-, or tribunal-adoption event retain separate identities and provenance, and a later reopening or replacement does not mutate any prior payload. The adopted event may authoritatively record scoped human disposition, consistent with align decision 2, but must not convert its semantic proposition into source truth ([Legal ontology — Design challenge 1](../../../explorations/academia-corpus-mining/research/t3-legal-ontology-design.md#design-challenges), `36d82e899e75` — Cross: An OWL Wrapper for Reasoning on Relational; `3c06bfc28d1d` — Classifications and the Law: Doctrinal Classifications vs. Computational Ontologies; `a09528e40201` — Semantic Web for the Legal Domain: The next).

3. **Assessments must be qualifier-complete and independently revisable.** A fixture passes only if assertion identity and assessment context preserve applicable dates, jurisdiction, procedural posture, actor role, polarity, scope, source version, evaluator, and assessment policy; changing one qualifier must not silently reuse another assertion’s verdict. Per-span `supports`, `refutes`, `insufficient-evidence`, and qualifier-mismatch outcomes remain separate from anchor fidelity, source authority, disposition, and release ([Retrieval/citation — Design challenges 1–3](../../../explorations/academia-corpus-mining/research/t3-retrieval-citation-grounding.md#design-challenges), `0421a1687b40` — ProVe).

4. **Context-indexed acceptance must preserve its evaluation basis.** A fixture passes only if each acceptance or closure record names the selected fact set, proof standard, source assessments, evaluator or decision-maker, jurisdiction, procedure, reasons, valid time, and knowledge time, so a changed context yields a new state rather than mutation. This is evidence for the bitemporal carrier, not authorization to add a legal argumentation engine to the core ([Legal norms — Direct patterns 3 and 7](../../../explorations/academia-corpus-mining/research/t3-legal-norms-reasoning.md#direct-patterns), `703aea161905` — Argumentation and Standards of Proof; `73abf21862dc` — The adaptive nature of text-driven law; `b61240c6a85e` — An Architecture for Establishing Legal Semantic Workflows in).

5. **Derived corrections must carry dependency-driven invalidation.** A fixture passes only if an OCR, layout, mapping, or interpretation correction preserves the source artifact and superseded output, records producer and version lineage, and identifies every dependent anchor or assessment requiring revalidation. Rebuilding dependents must not rewrite the correction history or source bytes ([Document structure — Versioned artifact DAG](../../../explorations/academia-corpus-mining/research/t3-doc-structure-legal-nlp.md#direct-patterns), `355af156cd8c` — Robust Document Image Understanding Technologies; `94896fbc3276` — A machine learning pipeline for document extraction; `f1c9490e6b58` — Integrated Text and Image Understanding for Document Understanding; `35c09789198c` — Machine Learning for Digital Document Processing: From Layout Analysis To).

6. **Revision order and replay behavior must be explicit.** Tests must cover concurrent supersession, late arrival, conflicting duplicates, replay, and batch permutations. Where chronology has no substantive meaning, permutations must converge; where policy intentionally makes order significant, the resulting records must expose the policy version and causal difference ([Memory/bitemporal — Design challenge 5](../../../explorations/academia-corpus-mining/research/t3-memory-bitemporal.md#design-challenges), `5c2aeef6919d` — Ontology Revision as Non-Prioritized Belief Revision).

7. **Security-relevant revisions must retain non-secret causal lineage.** A fixture passes only if policy, commitment, attack-link, and model-trust changes are append-only, version-bound events that can reconstruct which revision governed a later disposition or action. Exact permissible trace evidence may be durable, but raw secret-bearing content must not be admitted merely to make the audit complete ([Agent security — Corroborations and Tensions](../../../explorations/academia-corpus-mining/research/t3-agent-security-orchestration.md#tensions--contradictions), `2ad7451c2819` — AGENT-FENCE: MAPPING SECURITY VULNERABILITIES ACROSS DEEP; `32499919f05e` — LMSanitator: Defending Prompt-Tuning Against Task-Agnostic; `e77ec0588486` — Securing With Dual-LLM Architecture: ChatTEDU an Open).

8. **Restart and migration proof must preserve every boundary case.** After restart or generated migration, the same paired `validAt` and `knownAt` queries must recover dispositions, competing lineages, interpretation/adoption history, assessment revisions, supersession links, and invalidation ancestry. This directly exercises the target’s existing recovery acceptance criterion rather than introducing a new authority surface ([target SPEC — Acceptance Criteria](../SPEC.md#acceptance-criteria), [Memory/bitemporal — Corroborations](../../../explorations/academia-corpus-mining/research/t3-memory-bitemporal.md#corroborations)).

## Fixture candidates

- **Competing lineages:** Two sources assert incompatible values for the same subject and predicate but have distinct evidence scopes. Supersede the value from source A; prove source B remains open, both former assertions remain historically queryable, and no preferred view is persisted by the core.

- **Interpretation and adoption:** One provision supports two source-linked interpretations. Record an authorized adoption of one interpretation with reasons and procedure, then reopen it and adopt a revision retroactively; prove earlier and later `knownAt` values return the appropriate closure record without changing the provision or rejected interpretation.

- **Qualifier-complete assessment:** Use superficially identical assertions that differ by jurisdiction, effective interval, procedural posture, and actor role. Attach supporting, refuting, insufficient-evidence, and qualifier-mismatch assessments; prove no verdict crosses qualifier boundaries and a new source snapshot supersedes only the relevant assessment lineage.

- **Correction and dependent invalidation:** Preserve a page image, OCR version 1, a derived text anchor, and a support assessment. Append an OCR correction and version 2; prove the old anchor and assessment remain queryable, the replacement lineage is atomic, and the dependent records are marked for revalidation rather than deleted.

- **Revision ordering:** Submit a replacement, duplicate, rejection, and late-arriving correction concurrently and in multiple batch orders. Prove deterministic replay, one non-overlapping authoritative version per logical lineage, durable rejected dispositions, and explicit causal history for any intentional order-sensitive outcome.

- **Policy and model-trust revision:** Record a non-secret execution event under policy version 1, a later attack-link finding, a policy revision, and a model-artifact disqualification. Prove historical queries recover the rule then known, while the current view reflects invalidation; represent any secret encounter only through an approved masked record or keyed digest.

- **Restart boundary:** Persist all preceding fixtures, restart the repository, apply the generated migration in the proof lane, and repeat their as-of queries. Expected answers, lineage, invalidation ancestry, and dispositions must be identical across process boundaries.

## Deferred align questions

> Master align Q1: What canonical names and owners should beep-effect adopt for shape validity, anchor fidelity, semantic stance, source authority/currentness, human disposition, action authorization, and release?

The corpus strongly supports separate typed verdict families because each answers a different question and none implies the next ([Retrieval/citation — Verdict paragraph](../../../explorations/academia-corpus-mining/research/t3-retrieval-citation-grounding.md#verdict-paragraph), [Agent security — Verdict paragraph](../../../explorations/academia-corpus-mining/research/t3-agent-security-orchestration.md#verdict-paragraph)). It supports epistemic ownership here for revisable stance, authority/currentness assessments, and durable disposition history, while anchor fidelity, action authorization, and release remain owned by their existing citation and governance streams. The evidence does not settle final repo names; align decision 2 does settle the semantic constraint that approval is a recorded scoped human disposition, not truth manufacture.

> Master align Q3: Which exact records are retention-bearing authority, which are expirable operational events, which are prunable projections, and which secret-bearing inputs are prohibited from durable storage altogether?

The corpus supports retention-bearing exact source artifacts, sourced assertions, authorized interpretation or decision events, and permissible transition history; semantic indexes, rankings, preferred views, summaries, and regenerated assessments are projections whose lifecycle can differ ([Memory/bitemporal — Design challenge 1](../../../explorations/academia-corpus-mining/research/t3-memory-bitemporal.md#design-challenges)). It also distinguishes exact non-secret execution events from transient secret-bearing inputs, which should be prohibited from durable storage while retaining only policy-approved masked evidence or keyed digests ([Agent security — Tensions and contradictions](../../../explorations/academia-corpus-mining/research/t3-agent-security-orchestration.md#tensions--contradictions)). The corpus does not provide a production-validated retention schedule, and the target SPEC explicitly excludes retention; this packet can preserve classification seams and fixtures without choosing expiry or deletion policy.

## Tensions and limits

- The target forbids automatic supersession from contradiction and excludes contradiction triage. Competing-lineage fixtures should prove coexistence and lineage isolation; align decision 3’s preferred working view remains the first downstream composition.

- Adopted interpretations and institutional decisions may be exact authoritative records of what an authorized actor decided, while their propositions remain contestable interpretations. Treating every human-confirmed semantic edge as source truth would violate both the corpus boundary and the target’s repo-native authority model.

- The two target axes are necessary but do not collapse legal enforceability, efficacy, applicability, observation time, access time, or release time. Those meanings require downstream composition rather than new columns smuggled into this core ([Memory/bitemporal — Design challenge 3](../../../explorations/academia-corpus-mining/research/t3-memory-bitemporal.md#design-challenges)).

- Exact audit and secret minimization pull in opposite directions. The evidence supports complete permissible histories, not persistence of prohibited raw secrets; the exact storage classes and deletion mechanism remain unresolved and outside this goal.

- The corpus is strong on architectural convergence and boundary failures but thin on production validation. No paper validates the complete combination of legal bitemporality, concurrent Postgres supersession, qualifier-complete assessments, privileged documents, secret minimization, attorney disposition, and restart-safe migration; these are fixture hypotheses, not imported performance claims.

- Several corroborating studies are old, domain-specific, prototype-only, or methodologically weak. Align decisions 4 and 7 appropriately defer more mining and park the source packet after dispatch rather than treating corpus volume as proof.
