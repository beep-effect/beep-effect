### Proposed plan (implementing lane)

1. **Schema change first:** add one shared exception-provenance struct to the three registries: `{ owner, reason, evidence?, reconsiderWhen | expiresOn, reviewedOn }`. Make it required for `status: exception`, and enforce it in the schema-first and effect-vitest `--check` paths and in `AllowlistCheck.ts`. Fail when `expiresOn` is in the past and when an allowlist entry is unused. Effect-vitest needs `owner` (defaulting to `package` is acceptable only if the ruling says so).
2. **Detector repairs, each with a paired fixture in the existing file:** FP-3 (EV011 `vi`-only; `effect-vitest-detectors.test.ts` `cases` plus a targeted test), FP-1 (EV014 platform layers), FP-4 (EV006 compound `assertTrue`), FP-5 a/b (`lint-command.test.ts` beside l.623/657). Then re-run `--write` and record how many exceptions each repair retires. Expected: about 16 + 125 + 4 + 7 rows, an upper bound.
3. **Rulings before code:** FP-2 (EV003 per-invocation temp wrappers, 188 rows) and FP-6 (Weak\* collections, 9 entries) are policy disputes, not mechanical false positives. Record a decision, then either repair the detector or keep individually reviewed exceptions.
4. **Fixture gaps to close independent of FP work:** a NoNativeRuntime per-kind negative for 11 kinds; an `SFV4-numeric-domain` negative; named per-kind pairs for `exported-interface` and `exported-type-literal`; JSDoc fixtures for the 18 untested rule codes.
5. **Return `CandorRecord.test.ts:42` to open.** Re-review the 46 high-reuse effect-vitest templates (656 rows) row by row against the new required fields. The 214 rows citing proof docs are the cheapest to certify.
6. Hold every effect-vitest `--write` until the effect-vitest-canon lanes are reconciled (brief B.6). Before any `standards/effect-laws.allowlist.jsonc` edit, notify the effected-port session (stage-1 cross-session constraint).
