# Reflection — ai-metrics-stack (2026-07-14, codex)

## Summary

The retained V1 stack includes privacy-safe collection, encrypted raw archives,
DuckDB/Parquet derivatives, OTLP/Phoenix export, install and timer ownership,
labels, benchmarks, and the accepted June scorecard. Closeout exposed remote
reachability as an environment boundary, not a reason to keep the whole packet
active.

## Tooling experience

- **Worked:** The phase evidence and full-window scorecard made the completion
  basis measurable rather than dependent on a fresh seven-day rerun.
- **Didn't:** This session could not reach `dankserver` over SSH, so the final
  sanitized mirror operation could not be responsibly attempted.
- **Frustrating:** Remote topology work and local product completion shared one
  packet even though they require different authority and network conditions.
- **Wished existed:** A preflight that classifies remote proof failures as
  network-policy blockers before running any mutation-capable command.

## Implementation improvement opportunities

- Keep mirror bundles restricted to sanitized manifests, reports, status, and
  allowed derived Parquet exports; raw encrypted transcripts stay local.
- Score unavailable provider/tool/cost dimensions explicitly as unavailable,
  never as zero performance.

## Goal & prompt critique

The original end-to-end framing let optional mirror, provider, and dashboard
work overshadow a proven measurement stack. The goal should define the credited
scorecard and privacy/operability gates as V1, with remote mirror execution and
new metrics surfaces as separately authorized extensions.

## TODOs worth codifying

- Preserve "won't-do-this-PR" for safe remote operations blocked only by the
  current session boundary.
- Require new scope before P7c or P7d resumes.

## Lessons (confidence-tiered)

- **HIGH — Critical:** Do not attempt remote sync without reachability and
  authority — the denied SSH socket was a safe stop signal.
- **MEDIUM — Best practice:** Close on credited evidence — the June report and
  P6a gates already prove the retained product outcome.
- **LOW — Consideration:** Expand dashboards from user demand — richer surfaces
  are not prerequisites for a working metrics pipeline.
