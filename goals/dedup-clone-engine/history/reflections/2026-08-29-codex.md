# Reflection — dedup-clone-engine (2026-08-29, codex)

## Summary

V1 shipped deterministic clone detection, baseline enforcement, tests and
threshold tuning, plus report-only fuzzy discovery across PRs 180, 183, and 187.
The remaining intra-package toggle is a bounded follow-up, not missing closure.

## Tooling experience

- **Worked:** The repository's established generate-diff-ratchet pattern gave
  clone detection a deterministic route from inventory to enforcement.
- **Didn't:** Fuzzy near-miss discovery was not suitable for the blocking gate.
- **Frustrating:** Adjacent graph ranking and codemod ideas could easily inflate
  the engine beyond the completed V1 contract.
- **Wished existed:** A shared declaration-extraction substrate consumed by
  clone detection and codegraph without duplicate AST walking.

## Implementation improvement opportunities

- Keep fuzzy output report-only until precision and threshold stability justify
  a separate enforcement decision.

## Goal & prompt critique

The goal should state at the outset that deterministic Type-1/Type-2 detection
is the blocking V1 boundary and that fuzzy, graph-ranking, and codemod work have
separate owners.

## TODOs worth codifying

- Require heuristic quality gates to document why each mode is blocking,
  advisory, or deferred.

## Lessons (confidence-tiered)

- **HIGH — Critical:** Ratchet only deterministic findings — baseline drift must
  be reproducible to serve as a CI gate.
- **MEDIUM — Best practice:** Redirect adjacent capabilities to their owning
  packets — V1 closure should not absorb graph or codemod work.
- **LOW — Consideration:** Add intra-package discovery only with evidence — the
  deferred toggle needs a measured noise budget.
