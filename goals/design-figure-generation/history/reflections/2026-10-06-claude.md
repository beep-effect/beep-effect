# Reflection — design-figure-generation (2026-10-06, claude)

## Summary

`beep drawings` renders eight design-patent sheets from one parametric solid, validates them against 37 CFR 1.84 and Patent Center limits, shades planar faces, packs a vision-judge round, and records an attorney sign-off. The live matter's unshaded and shaded sets render deterministically and pass the validator. The attorney has not yet reviewed the live set, and five polish findings from the judge are carried.

## Tooling experience

- **Worked:** replicad over opencascade.js gave perspective hidden-line removal in-process, so no Python fallback was needed. Running hatch lines through the same hidden-line pass as the outlines made occlusion correct for free. The two-run byte-identity check caught the random PDF `/ID` early.
- **Didn't:** the hosted mail connector could not return image attachments, and the in-repo driver has no attachment verb.
- **Frustrating:** each new package edge staled four generated files (policy fingerprint, fallow boundaries, cache baseline, effect-vitest inventory), and each has its own regeneration command. A transitive `@types` package leaked DOM globals into every program compiling the kernel driver.
- **Wished existed:** a spec construct for coplanar placement, and a deterministic check on shading line spacing.

## Implementation improvement opportunities

- The synthetic fixture is too simple to guard the shading code; see the first finding.
- Hatch pitch is corrected for foreshortening under an orthographic approximation. Perspective views use the same factor, which is close but not exact near the frame edges.
- The lid is modelled flush, without board thickness, while the sleeves have it.

## Goal & prompt critique

- The GOAL listed the pipeline under `packages/law-practice/*`; the capability is not law-specific and moved to a foundation capability package after a question to the operator. The launcher should have left the home to the architecture command.
- The live-matter criterion should have named its inputs and their location, as noted in the findings.

## TODOs worth codifying

- The four items in the frontmatter `todos`.

## Lessons (confidence-tiered)

- **HIGH — Critical:** Render the real article before calling a drawing pipeline final — the synthetic fixture passed two judge rounds while three shading and seam defects were still present.
- **MEDIUM — Best practice:** Keep generated geometry at full floating-point precision — rounding breaks coplanarity, and kernels do not warn about it.
- **LOW — Consideration:** Treat a vision judge's "missing feature" finding on an unfiled design as a possible claim-scope question before treating it as a modelling defect.
