# Reflection — effected-promotion (2026-11-01, claude)

## Summary

The packet promoted every `scratchpad/effected` module into its `@beep/*` package or adopted it at its reuse
sites. It also dropped `jsonc-parser`, `yaml`, `toml`, `picomatch` and `get-tsconfig` from the runtime and tooling
consumers. Closeout PR C deletes the lab, pins the four deferred modules (npm registry/publish, `SigstoreSigner`,
`cli/ui*`, `engine`) at `bb2134fcb9`, and archives the lab notes and port reviews. It also opens the W4-l follow-up
goal. The verdict is that the work is complete. The cost sat mostly in shared generated files and in port-parity
evidence that proved fidelity to upstream bugs.

## Tooling experience

- **Worked:** The parity and differential oracles under `ops/` (frozen legacy copies, before records from a sibling
  worktree, outcome classes with no unclassified case allowed) gave every owner switch a mechanical proof. `yeet reply`
  with dispositions closed review rounds cleanly. `goals bootstrap --plan/--write --expect` created the follow-up
  packet without hand-written scaffolding. `beep-heavy` kept full-suite runs from starving sibling lanes.
- **Didn't:** Byte parity with upstream could not see upstream's own bugs. That included the jsonc-parser last-element
  delete, and the `@beep/yaml` regex that backtracked exponentially on CRLF prologues (CodeRabbit on #2469).
  Neither showed up as a parity difference.
- **Frustrating:** Every main move meant a merge plus a ~10-minute regeneration of the JSDoc inventory, the cache
  baseline and the policy fingerprint. Then came a serialized publish slot. zsh's `$var:s` modifier silently turned
  `"$PIN:scratchpad/..."` into an empty path, and six archived files came out empty until `${PIN}` was used.
- **Wished existed:** a pre-push squash-commitlint predictor, a line-shift-tolerant JSDoc inventory, and a doctor
  check that flags merged PRs whose PLAN row still says "in review".

## Implementation improvement opportunities

- `@beep/jsonc` `JsoncModifier` now differs from jsonc-parser in one documented place, the last-element delete. A
  second pass could review the other jsonc-parser removal shapes for the same `parentEnd - 2` assumption.
- `@beep/yaml` gained `quoteStyle: "auto"`, YAML 1.1 quoting, anchor preservation and BOM-prefix handling (#2469,
  #2472). The BOM fix came from a bug found while landing R4, so a BOM row in the yaml-test-suite runner would guard it.
- The 36 gh/git/ScriptedSpawner sites now live in `goals/repo-cli-gh-git-consolidation/research/site-list.md`. That
  goal's first kit features (`Git.isAncestor` and fail-closed parsers) unblock the most rows.

## Goal & prompt critique

- The GOAL said to "flip lifecycle to completed-retained" in the final PR. The repo's completion gate says the
  opposite: keep `active`, ship `proposedCloseout` plus a gating yeet-verdict reference, and let
  `goals completion refresh` flip it after merge. Proposed edit to GOAL: "Ship `proposedCloseout` and the reflection
  in C; `goals completion refresh` after merge is the flip." The packet's own closeout plan already said this.
- "Lanes parallel only when files don't overlap" was right but understated. Every PR overlapped on generated files,
  so in practice publishing was strictly serial. The GOAL could say "one publish at a time; prepare the next lane
  locally".

## Lessons (confidence-tiered)

- **HIGH — Critical:** Pair byte-parity oracles with an expected-value check per case — parity with a buggy upstream
  proved the port had copied the bug (#2473: 622 corrupting deletes classified only once checked against the
  intended document).
- **HIGH — Critical:** Replacing a tolerant parser with a strict one changes each caller's failure semantics; decide
  them per site — two R3b sites silently skipped a gate or protection.
- **MEDIUM — Best practice:** Rebuild a stacked lane onto main by re-applying its own diff, and stop its background runs
  first — R3b was rebuilt twice (09b648bd71, bb2134fcb9) without R4 history, and one stale final-check had to be killed.
- **MEDIUM — Best practice:** Create follow-up packets when the deferral is decided — W4-l's packet waited 9 days.
- **LOW — Consideration:** Use `${var}` before `:` in zsh path strings, and short shas in Markdown tables that gitleaks
  scans.
