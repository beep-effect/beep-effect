`tsconfig.check.json` and regenerated `standards/fallow.boundaries.generated.jsonc`; `bun.lock` records the workspace
edge. No third-party dependency is added.

## Turbo coupling, measured against the lane base

Measured from the workspace `dependencies` closures at the lane base 7b6d19133c (PR #2246, 3-A4) and at this branch:

- `@beep/repo-utils`: 6 workspaces before, 11 after. It gains `@beep/package-json`, `@beep/npm-spec`, `@beep/semver`,
  `@beep/spdx` and `@beep/jsonc`. An edit to one of those five packages' `src/**` now invalidates repo-utils'
  `#build`, `#check` and `#test` entries.
- `@beep/repo-cli` (62), `@beep/repo-docgen` (20) and `@beep/scratchpad` (61): unchanged. All three already reached
  `@beep/package-json` and its whole closure (3-A4, 3-A9), so the new edge adds no task and no closure member.
- `@beep/oip-web` and `@beep/effect-drizzle` reach repo-utils only through `devDependencies`. Their test-time
  closures grow from 15 to 19 and from 10 to 15 workspaces; their production closures are unchanged.

The coupling is accepted: `@beep/package-json` is the one owner of the package.json field model (W3-f, D-08), and
the five modeling packages were promoted as stable, low-churn kits.
