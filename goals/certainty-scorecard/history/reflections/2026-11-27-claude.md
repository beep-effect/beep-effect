# Reflection — certainty-scorecard (2026-11-27, claude)

## Summary

The goal shipped its instrument. `beep ci certainty` and its nightly score every code head's push to all required
contexts green, beside runner-minutes, Yeet-mergeable time, the main verdict rate and the sibling goals' rows.
Seven nights in a row were certified, and the economics fold now counts retired lanes, T3 lanes and terminations
separately. It took twenty slices, 1 to 16 plus their b slices and 18, along with record PRs and #3305. This
reflection was written at the first closeout attempt. Criteria 1 and 3 to 9 are ticked from committed tests, live
reads and the first nightly on #3609's code. That nightly showed that two rows of "Numbers the scorecard reports" live
only in other commands, so the goal reopened for slice 19 (decision `criterion-2-rows-built-into-certainty`).

## Tooling experience

- **Worked:** The `beep ci route replay --corpus` restore gave back the exact old receipt (5,083 / 242 / 0) on
  current main. `yeet economics --fleet --json` decodes into something jq can work with. `BEEP_FLEET_STATE_ROOT`
  made it possible to test a real `roots.json` without touching workstation state. `quality scheduler history`
  gives one line per source.
- **Didn't:** A plain `--fleet` census leaves T3 lanes out without saying so, because the workstation has no
  `roots.json`.
- **Frustrating:** The replay resolves the corpus by basename next to the receipt, so a scratch receipt needs a
  copy of the corpus beside it. The replay of the 6,328-run receipt also took about 7 minutes and printed nothing
  until it finished.
- **Wished existed:** A jobs-read count in the lane-timings output, and a census mode that limits economics to
  journals filed on a given day, which would make the retirement check a single command.

## Implementation improvement opportunities

- Print the jobs read per attempt in the lane-timings summary, and the partial-read reasons, so the 502 criterion
  can be checked from the reader alone.
- Warn in `yeet economics --fleet` when the T3 worktree root exists but no roots list is configured.
- Let `ci route replay` print progress while it classifies heads.

## Lessons (confidence-tiered)

- **HIGH — Critical:** Before the closeout, check that a cited baseline population can still be read. A number
  measured on journals that retirement deletes cannot be replayed later, so record it in a form that does not
  need the old rows.
- **MEDIUM — Best practice:** Prove a live census across a retirement by intersecting the run ids filed into the
  store that day with the census's `(retained)` rows, and check that no `(runId, attemptId)` repeats. It is
  cheap and needs no fixture.
- **LOW — Consideration:** Capture slow, silent commands (route replay, fleet census) to files in the background
  and keep working; each took minutes.
