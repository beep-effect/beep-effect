Slice 9 lands TURB-05's shadow on `perf/local-proof-force-reason` (decision files `turb-05-force-receipt-on-attempt-row`, `turb-05-installed-tree-digest` and `turb-05-patch-only-commits-unforced-today`). `decideTurboForce` in `Yeet/internal/TurboForce.ts` is the force decision, moved from `Handler.ts`. Repair, verify and publish force every dependency-sensitive step when `bun.lock` changed, as before, and now also when a file under `patches/` or the root `patchedDependencies` changed, or the root `overrides` or `resolutions` changed. That widening closes the stale-green hole for patch-only and override-only commits (#2873 was one) and is the only change to the forced set. Every repair, verify and publish attempt-started row now carries `turboForce`. It holds the actual reasons (each trigger that applied) and a shadow: the narrowed reasons (the actual ones without `lockfile-changed`) and, when `bun.lock` changed, each package's locked installed-tree digest at the merge base and HEAD, with the packages whose digest changed. A run logs one `turbo force shadow:` line when it forces or the shadow is unavailable. Nothing reads the shadow to plan or decide (ruling D14).

Gate evidence: `test/yeet-turbo-force.test.ts` (29 cases, Node and Bun, a `Git.makeTest` repository double and WebCrypto digests, no `node_modules` or live repository reads). It checks the digest inputs line by line; that a nested bump moves one package and a root bump moves every package; the reasons and log lines of lockfile, patch-only, `patchedDependencies` and override forces; that a failed diff forces nothing and an unreadable merge base or root manifest keeps the path triggers; and, for every mode across eight install-input scenarios, that every run the lockfile check forced is still forced, that only the patch and override triggers are added, and that a forced run forces the steps a lockfile force always did. `test/yeet-command-wiring.test.ts` asserts the receipt reaches the attempt-started row of a real publish run. A row written before the field decodes with it absent. `TurboForce.ts` has full statement, branch and function coverage.

Measured on this checkout under load: the decision takes 43 to 55 ms, and the shadow takes 0.62 s on a lockfile attempt (209 packages) and 17 ms otherwise. Still open for TURB-05: the undeclared-import invariant, a shadow ledger over at least 10 lockfile attempts in fresh-install and pre-installed layouts, and the flip.
