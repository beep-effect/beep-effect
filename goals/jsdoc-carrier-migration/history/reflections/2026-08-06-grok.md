## Tooling experience

- **Worked:** local CLIProxyAPI at `127.0.0.1:8317` with model `grok-4.5` once Bearer auth was wired; append-only resume on `titles.jsonl`; dry-run apply residue measurement; yeet for P2 (#606) and P3 publish.
- **Didn't:** unauthenticated titles client (pre-fix); high concurrency without backoff (auth_unavailable); batch-of-N titles scheduling.
- **Frustrating:** multi-hour title pass even with concurrency 8; yeet monitor flaking when checks are not yet registered.
- **Wished existed:** a built-in concurrent titles runner with auth/backoff in P1.
