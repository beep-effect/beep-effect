# Practice Box onboarding closeout

The engine did what it was built for on a live tenant: 10,155 files in two
runs, every upload verified against Box's SHA-1, every source re-hashed
unchanged, and three interrupted runs resumed without a duplicate upload or an
overwrite. The never-overwrite rule and the identical-plan gate both stopped a
run that should have stopped.

What the packet got wrong at the start was identity, not mechanics. It
treated the salvaged old-drive corpus as "his current matter files" and
guessed at matter keys and client names. The attorney's live folder, found
only after the first migration, was the real primary source and answered most
of those questions by itself. The second run mirrors his layout and records
the knowledge-graph key beside it, which is the shape the first run should
have had.

Left for after this packet: acting on his locked review answers, re-linking
matter folders when the knowledge graph publishes its next bundle, retiring
the first run's duplicates and the seven empty folders once a tool that can
prune exists, and the two reconciler improvements above.
