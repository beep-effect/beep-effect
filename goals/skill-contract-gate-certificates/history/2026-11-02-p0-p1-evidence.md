| Input | Where it lands |
| --- | --- |

## Limitations

- No signing, DSSE wrapping or external verification.
- The certificate is a typed value; `judge-lint` / `judge-ingest` do not yet write
  it to disk, and nothing consumes it.
- The QA producer always reports reach `complete` (it lists all five declared
  gates); `incomplete`/`unknown` reach is exercised only by kernel tests.
- Digests are computed by the producer; the kernel compares them and never hashes.
