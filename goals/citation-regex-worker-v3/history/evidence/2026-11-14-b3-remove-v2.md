## Deletions and importer census

Its sole runtime importer was the removed one-request program tests. Both bundled
entries already import `citationRegexSessionProgram`; build and asset paths retain

## Isolation contract

Weakened, within one invocation only:

- Jobs share a process. Memory corruption in one job could affect a later job.
- A complete, bounded, validated result is consumed before worker retirement.
  `CitationRegexSession.test.ts`, "reuses one real ... worker and restarts only
  after lane handover", observes one started and zero confirmed before scope close.

Strengthened:

- A job deadline excludes startup time. "expires only the ... job timer during
  a catastrophic regex and confirms death before return" uses the sleep-counting
  manual clock, real Bun and Node children, and proves parent responsiveness.
- An acquired native lease cannot be stranded between acquisition and spawn.
  `CitationNativeLaneReservation.test.ts`, `CitationNativeLaneTransfer.test.ts`
  and `CitationNativeLaneFence.test.ts` exercise cancellation, late spawn and
  retained quarantine with real children and confirmed retirement.
- Protocol sequencing and idle-state deviations fail closed across reused jobs.
  Session tests kill on wrong sequence, early EOF, oversized frame and idle frame;
  interruption during Sent/Running confirms death; a later job never respawns.
- Per-job engine/descriptor authority survives reuse: the real-process mixed
  dialect test and invalid-engine job refusal cover both child runtimes.

No limits, public CitationEngine shape or extraction result changed. Parent-owned
SIGKILL and NativeProcess's confirmation/quarantine path are unchanged. The accepted
B2 spawner hand-off fence remains: a handed-off start can be born late but retains
ownership and must be retired; no new hand-off follows poisoning.
