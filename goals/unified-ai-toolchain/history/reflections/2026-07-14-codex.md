# Reflection — unified-ai-toolchain (2026-07-14, codex)

## Summary

The retained deliverable is the `@beep/ai-sync` V1 schema truth layer with
source pins, native schemas, drift detection, transform evidence, and real
`.codex/config.toml` validation. Closeout revealed that V2/V3 were a speculative
ten-phase product expansion rather than unfinished requirements for V1.

## Tooling experience

- **Worked:** Tiered source metadata, generated hashes, strict/local drift
  checks, and mandatory dogfooding made V1 claims directly testable.
- **Didn't:** Reopening the packet for V2/V3 converted a completed library into
  an apparently active roadmap without a triggering incident.
- **Frustrating:** Known upstream drift can look like a closure defect even when
  it is intentionally retained evidence for future demand.
- **Wished existed:** A portfolio status that distinguishes a completed product
  from a conditional, incident-triggered roadmap.

## Implementation improvement opportunities

- Keep transforms evidence-backed and mark lossy behavior explicitly.
- If drift reopens the work, start with operator audit/check commands before
  canonical source files or native emitters.

## Goal & prompt critique

The version ladder should have made V1 independently terminal from the start.
V2 automation and V3 canonical emission should be spawned only after a real
drift incident supplies requirements, priority, and representative failing
inputs.

## TODOs worth codifying

- Preserve the three known drift entries as the reopening evidence ledger.
- Require dry-run plans and loss reports before any future native apply path.

## Lessons (confidence-tiered)

- **HIGH — Critical:** Do not invent schemas for undocumented surfaces — `na`
  and `unknown_schema` are truthful compatibility states.
- **MEDIUM — Best practice:** Reopen from an incident — concrete drift should
  determine which V2 operator capability is actually needed.
- **LOW — Consideration:** Delay canonical emission — validated native truth is
  valuable without a universal source format.
