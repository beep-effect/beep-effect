The packet is complete and retained. PR #655 merged its bounded fixes, three

## Tooling experience

- **Worked:** The sanitized triage ledger and per-finding Codex IDs made it
  possible to match deferred findings to later packets and merged commits.
- **Didn't:** The packet had no final status note or reflection after PR #655
  merged, so the generated goal index continued to present it as active.
- **Frustrating:** Deferral ownership lived in narrative text instead of a
  decoded handoff field that the goal tooling could inspect.
- **Wished existed:** A closeout check that resolves repeated Codex IDs across
  retained packets and reports which findings landed later or moved to another
  owner.

## Implementation improvement opportunities

- Add `handoffTarget` and `requiredExternalEvidence` fields to deferred triage
  entries.
- Let the goal index distinguish packet completion from unresolved risk owned
  by a different packet or operational arc.

## Goal & prompt critique

State the terminal rule directly: a packet may close as `completed-retained`
after its bounded work merges when each residual finding has a named owner and
proof requirement. The closeout must also say whether dashboard closure was
verified.

## TODOs worth codifying

- Add cross-packet Codex ID reconciliation to the findings workflow.
- Add a lint rule for completed security packets with prose-only deferrals.

## Lessons (confidence-tiered)

- **HIGH — Critical:** Preserve unresolved security ownership during closeout.
  A lifecycle flip must not turn a handoff into accepted risk.
- **HIGH — Best practice:** Match repeated findings by Codex ID. CSF numbers are
  packet-local and cannot prove identity across captures.
- **MEDIUM — Consideration:** Record dashboard state separately from repository
  merge state so retrospective closeout does not overclaim external evidence.
