## Public Summary

PST processing reads bodies and attachments into memory, constructs complete
base64 EML values, re-encodes them, and accumulates JSONL. The byte budget is
optional and, when present, is checked after the expensive allocation.

## Current-HEAD Validation

Confirmed reachable at the current branch head before remediation; the exact affected surface and source commit are recorded above.

## Remediation

Applied a default 64 MiB libpff EML materialization budget when callers omit one.
