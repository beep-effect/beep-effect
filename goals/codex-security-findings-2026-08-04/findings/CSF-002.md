## Public Summary

Judge evidence discovery and citation validation rely on following `stat`,
lexical containment, and existence. A symlink inside a QA round can therefore
name a file outside the round and be handed to the vision judge.

## Current-HEAD Validation

Confirmed reachable at the current branch head before remediation; the exact affected surface and source commit are recorded above.

## Remediation

Canonicalized the QA round root and treat linked or non-regular judge evidence as absent.
