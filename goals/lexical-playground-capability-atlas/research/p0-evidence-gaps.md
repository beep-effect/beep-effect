# P0 evidence gaps

Date: 2026-08-24

This ledger is the live-interaction remainder for `editor-capability-atlas/v1`. It contains every atlas entry whose `evidenceStatus` is `unverified`, grouped by its primary activation surface. The source and inventory reconciliation is complete enough for the atlas verifier to pass, but source registration and menu presence do not prove behavior.

P0 is not complete. 162 entries still require the applicable create, edit, select, undo, redo, copy, paste, serialize, delete, accessibility, and responsive exercise, or a user-approved Exception Ledger waiver that names the exact atlas ID, rationale, owner, and follow-up goal.

A follow-up browser session should update the atlas entry to `verified-live` only after its applicable lifecycle passes. If an interaction is genuinely unavailable or unsafe, keep it unverified until the user approves a waiver in `SPEC.md`.

## Exercise protocol

For each row:

1. Open the pinned Lexical Playground reference and capture recorded interaction evidence.
2. Exercise every listed activation path, not only the primary group named here.
3. Cover the applicable lifecycle and verify focus restoration, accessible names/roles, keyboard-only use, narrow layout, and a touch alternative.
4. Exercise import/export or clipboard paths when the atlas entry declares them.
5. Record network and permission behavior for speech, media, embeds, clipboard, and sharing.
6. Update `capability-atlas.json` with exact evidence and change `evidenceStatus` only when the entry's stated proof gaps are closed.
