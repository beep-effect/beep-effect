## New findings and unverified boundaries

New findings, all P3:

- **A paused worker is silent.** `prometheus-alerts.yml.j2:227-229` alerts on stopped, degraded, stale and clock-regression but not paused, and `ObservationWorker.ts:299-302` returns 200 whenever the loop runs. Every maintenance operation leaves the worker paused by design, so a forgotten resume after backup or seal stops collection with no signal. Add a long-window alert on the paused phase, or distinguish paused from idle in the health status.
- **The plan renderer cannot check the page budget against the launch config.** `ObservationDeployment.ts:100` says the two must match, but the renderer never reads the launch file. A mismatch surfaces only as a content-free `resources` refusal at `ObservationWorker.ts:155-162`. Let the plan command accept the launch config path and refuse a mismatch at plan time.
- **Independent health is host-local.** `observation-health.yml:5-10` requires same-host socket and journal paths and root. The monitoring role targets dankserver while the canary worker ran on the workstation. Production enrollment needs the probe installed on the worker host, together with the transport design noted above.
- **The 30-second maintenance bound can starve a large operation.** `observation-maintenance-http.ts:190` interrupts the operation and returns 503. A seal or backup that legitimately exceeds 30 s at the 32 MiB ceiling can never complete through this route. Document the out-of-band fallback or make the bound configurable within policy.
