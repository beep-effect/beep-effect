# Reflection — jsdoc-lead-single-source (2026-11-24, claude)

## Summary

JSDoc leads now come from one source. Docgen derives a missing lead from the export's
`$I.annote*` description (#3496). The JSDoc inventory flags a lead that restates its
annotation as `restating-lead` and ratchets it. `beep/require-description` accepts a derived
lead (#3532). Six waves (#3496, #3506, #3512, #3514, #3532, #3535) took `restating-lead` from
8,726 to 507. The 507 left are in fenced or lane-owned files. Both acceptance criteria hold:
type companions, barrels and annotated schemas pass docs lint and docgen without a lead, and a
restating lead is flagged.

## Tooling experience

- **Worked:**
  - Per package, the scratch codemod plus the inventory's `restating-lead` rows made each wave
    mechanical. The codemod's guard kept every row it could not prove restated.
  - `yeet final-check --skip coverage=...` followed by `yeet open` was reliable, and the
    stale-base refusal caught every overlapping main merge before it reached review.
  - `quality jsdoc-ratchet --regenerate` gave an exact before/after for each PR body.
- **Didn't:**
  - The wave 6 move of the `packagesNeedingRemediation` ceiling from 15 to 16 reached the PR, and
    a review thread caught it rather than a local gate.
  - The generator reruns after each main merge (schema-first, schema-catalog, observability
    catalog, decisions write) needed a manual memory cap. Earlier, earlyoom killed schema-first
    while an unrelated 29 GB process was running.
- **Frustrating:**
  - Rebuilding the four P5 fence lists by hand every wave.
  - Three reseals (seal test digests, policy fingerprint, `turbo.json`) for one inventory change.
- **Wished existed:**
  - A fence-intersection command.
  - A merge-ref re-measure for new ratchet totals.
  - An inventory `--json` per-export dump to replace scratch ts-morph censuses.

## Implementation improvement opportunities

- Extend `derivedLead` to `{@inheritDoc X}` and to `annoteSchema` after `S.brand` in a pipe.
  The 24 schema files wave 6 restored would then sweep cleanly.
- Have `beep/require-description` and the inventory share one derived-lead predicate, so they
  cannot drift.
- Sweep the remaining fenced files once the infra-alchemy-rebuild and text-knowledge-stack
  lanes finish, in a deploy-worded wave behind the closure-wave proofs
  (`decisions/2026-11-23-closure-wave-proofs.md`).

## Lessons (confidence-tiered)

- **HIGH — Critical:**
  - Measure a new ratcheted total on the tree a PR merges into, not on its branch base.
    Concurrent merges added 32 leads between #3496's measurement and its merge, and main went
    red.
  - Before a sweep removes a lead, check that the export still has a summary afterwards. A
    removed lead the derivation cannot replace grows the remediation ceiling, and in wave 6 a
    review thread found it, not a local gate.
- **MEDIUM — Best practice:**
  - Intersect every wave's diff with the P5 fence sets before opening, not after. Wave 3's
    64-file revert cost a full extra cycle.
  - Make a gate-behaviour rule change in its own wave, with the subset property ("reports can
    only disappear") measured in the PR body.
- **LOW — Consideration:**
  - Package-batched waves of a few hundred files kept merge conflicts small. Taking theirs and
    re-running the codemod resolved every conflict in under a minute.
