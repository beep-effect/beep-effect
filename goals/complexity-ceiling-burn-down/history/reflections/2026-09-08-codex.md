node may intentionally be null or undefined. Keep validation's laziness at the
existing batch boundary, and test the cap independently from conformance.

than adding another configuration abstraction.

old CRAP and line-count allowances obsolete, so the final wave tightens them.

acceptance comes from the subsequent baseline comparisons, which all pass.

predicate auditable without widening the frozen 7-15 band. The historical
