# Reflection — speed-loop (2026-08-08, codex)

## Summary

The campaign moved from Blacksmith exit through EC2 groundwork and a
supervised burst into a sustained shipping loop: more than eleven PRs merged,
the burst landed an eight-PR wave, and the final research produced an explicit
adopt-then-wrap decision. The packet closes completed-retained with the small
wrap-up widgets shipped, the knowledge-sidecar ideas superseded, earlier
unshipped widgets parked, and the fleet endgame graduated intact.

## Tooling experience

- **Worked:** the research → probe → grill → ship → harvest ladder kept design
  claims tied to lived failures; merged-tree verification and jobs-API
  fingerprints turn two recurring failure classes into mechanical checks; the
  retained ledger preserves why each parked or graduated item moved.
- **Didn't:** text merging generated artifacts could produce a believable
  false result from either side, and count-only ratchets could certify stale
  inventories.
- **Frustrating:** full jsdoc inventory generation took about five minutes per
  pass and repeated during merge repair; session-scoped runner state had to be
  rediscovered instead of addressed through porcelain.
- **Wished existed:** a regenerate-only merge driver, bounded inventory mode,
  anchor-integrity check, proof hashes wired through `ProofState.laneProofs`,
  and runner-emitted peak RSS.

## Implementation improvement opportunities

- Complete the two wrap-up follow-ups: export `ProofState.laneProofs` for
  proof-hash staleness and emit peak RSS from the runner.
- Make generated artifacts fail closed on merge, then regenerate from the
  merged tree.
- Bake fallback native-build prerequisites such as `libsecret-1-dev` into the
  worker image so a download timeout does not become an unrelated red lane.

## Goal & prompt critique

The standing-loop contract was valuable early, but its numeric stop rule did
not anticipate the work changing categories from repo-quality optimization to
fleet operations. I would add: "The operator may close by signed adoption and
graduation decision when remaining candidates form a separately owned program;
record dispositions and preserve the original measurements." That keeps the
stop rule honest without forcing a successor campaign into an exhausted packet.

## TODOs worth codifying

See the frontmatter `todos`. The generated-artifact merge driver and bounded
inventory mode stay general repo tooling work; runner image, RSS, and durable
lane-control state belong to `goals/ci-fleet-endgame`.

## Lessons (confidence-tiered)

- **HIGH — Critical:** Prove the merged tree, not only branch HEAD — clean
  textual merges repeatedly hid semantic breakage across coupled files.
- **HIGH — Critical:** Treat friction receipts as design inputs — the runner
  burst's exact failures directly shaped the adopted controller, AMI, cache,
  and porcelain contracts.
- **MEDIUM — Best practice:** Graduate a coherent successor rather than let a
  successful standing loop become a permanent catch-all.
- **LOW — Consideration:** Retained parked widgets remain useful only when a
  future packet cites their evidence and re-probes current value.
