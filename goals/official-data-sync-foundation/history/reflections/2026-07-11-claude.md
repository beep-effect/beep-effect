## Goal & prompt critique

The packet itself was well-shaped (compact GOAL.md, normative SPEC, explicit
verificationCommands including the manifest jq check). Its one structural gap
is that the completion gate is defined entirely in terms of the PR, not the
packet record — closing the loop on the manifest/reflection should be part of
the same gate.

## Lessons (confidence-tiered)

- **HIGH — Critical:** Close the paperwork in the merge commit. P3/lifecycle
  flips deferred to "later" become multi-week drift (merged 2026-06-21,
  reconciled 2026-07-11).
- **MEDIUM — Best practice:** Keep implementation evidence as dated files with
  exact commands and quarantined external blockers; it is what makes
  retroactive reconciliation truthful rather than reconstructed.
