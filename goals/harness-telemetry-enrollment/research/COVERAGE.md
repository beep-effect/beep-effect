## What has passed

The [admission report](CURRENT-SOURCE-ADMISSION.md) distinguishes successful source-bound synthetic delivery from real activity. A two-event fixture proves the chosen collector and backend path only. It does not estimate how many daily sessions, tools or model calls are missing.

## What a verified row requires

For each route retain five separate results: capability, effective configuration, actual launch/source identity, normalized allowlisted metadata, and exact backend receipt. Launch counts and emitted events need independent denominators. Cancellation, restart, resume, child handoff and clone/worktree identities require their own tests. A successful AGENT interval may carry unknown token usage; it must not be shown as a model request or invented model latency.

## Privacy and recovery

Metadata plus explicitly curated sanitized task examples is the approved policy. No raw prompts, responses, tool arguments, Electron conversations or account identifiers are admitted merely because a source exposes them. The historical forwarder service and timer remain masked/inactive. Historical proof and data are retained; this packet does not delete Phoenix projects or backfill activity.
