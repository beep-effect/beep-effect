# Reflection — outbox-draft-basis (2026-11-13, claude)

## Summary

An outbox draft now records the Graph items it rested on, `get_draft` and
`check_drafts` report whether that basis changed, and a stale draft gets the
Outlook category `Draft - recheck before sending`. The tenant proof showed the
category stays on the sender's side. The work shipped in two pull requests,
#3137 and #3172; the second waited a day on a mail outage and a human send.

## Tooling experience

- **Worked:** decision files through `goals decisions add`; the Connect
  backend for 1Password once the service-account lane hit its hourly limit;
  a small scratch script over the driver's own service for tenant probes.
- **Didn't:** the agent could not send the test message. Its click on Send
  was refused by the permission layer, which was the right outcome under
  the operator's ruling but cost a relay round.
- **Frustrating:** the mail connector on the lane was signed in to a
  different account from the outside recipient, so the last check had to be
  made by the orchestrator session.
- **Wished existed:** a final-check that prints which gate it is on.

## Implementation improvement opportunities

- A draft with no recorded message id that moves before `check_drafts` first
  reads it is still retired as `missing`. The tenant proof shows the create
  response always carries the id, so only records written before ids were
  stored can hit this.
- The category colour is a manual step in the attorney's Outlook. With a
  MailboxSettings role the server could create the category itself.

## Goal & prompt critique

- `GOAL.md` says "write no category before that proof is recorded" but does
  not say the proof blocks the merge of the second pull request. State it:
  "PR 2 opens labelled `owner-merge-hold` and merges only after the proof".
- The closeout paragraph still describes the two-pull-request route; the
  one-pull-request route (#3248) replaced it mid-goal.

## TODOs worth codifying

- HTTP status in `M365Error` messages.
- A runbook section for tenant probes.

## Lessons (confidence-tiered)

- **HIGH — Critical:** re-read the system of record after a person reports
  an action — three "sent" answers were false.
- **MEDIUM — Best practice:** probe vendor identity behaviour before
  building on it — two review rounds rested on guesses that happened to hold.
- **LOW — Consideration:** ask for an observable rather than a yes/no when a
  person does a step for a proof.
