# Reflection — knowledge-surface-automation (2026-10-16, claude)

## Summary

The goal landed all five workstreams' automation: the knowledge doctor with causal
grouping, a sealed-baseline gate with leases and a CI mint App (C), the vendored-skills
warehouse with lock v2, materialize, the update train and the review ritual (B), the
goals catalog and next/explain commands (D), and self-hosted bootstrap / graduate / adopt
(E). Ritual 1 classified the three vendored patches. The first sealed baseline (#1790) was
minted by `beep-knowledge-mint[bot]`, admitted by the seal guard, and merged under the
operator's ruling (f7452d7473), which ended the gate's report-only period: the
knowledge-doctor gate is live. Verdict: done, with one
recurring weakness - this goal's own PRs introduced five main reds through unchecked
generated artifacts.

## Tooling experience

- **Worked:** the plan / `--expect <planDigest>` pattern for every lock and baseline
  writer made each mutation reviewable and refused stale plans correctly (the shadcn
  relabel after oracle's write). Path-scoped codemods (the post-#1758 S.Opaque procedure)
  and `lint effect-vitest --write` made main merges mechanical. The memory-only GitHub App
  registration (manifest flow, `cat | op item create` pipe) provisioned the mint App
  without the key touching disk, argv or chat.
- **Didn't:** the merge gate merged a stale head (#1826, ep31). Hand-splicing the
  effect-vitest inventory reordered thousands of lines until the orchestrator ruled
  regenerate-only. Interactive `skills review` was the wrong surface for an operator who
  needed the labels explained first.
- **Frustrating:** five self-inflicted main reds; the weekly Opus limit killed two
  subagents mid-task (B-6 and the ep11 fix), which survived only because their WIP was
  committed to local backup branches; GraphQL pool exhaustion forced REST for most PR
  operations; a stale 1Password desktop agent blocked commit signing until restarted.
- **Wished existed:** a single "generated artifacts" check that runs every regenerate
  `--check` the CI runs; a gate warning when the PR head moves after a final.

## Implementation improvement opportunities

- Multi-skill `patch label` / `lock-plan` plans under one lock digest.
- The mint bootstrap and the reviewer bootstrap share a manifest-flow core; extract it
  once #1749's reviewer app lands, instead of keeping two adapted copies.
- `knowledge doctor ci` should print the seal guard's admission reasons on success too,
  so a reviewer of the baseline PR sees why it was admitted without reading code.

## Lessons (confidence-tiered)

- **HIGH — Critical:** never push after calling a PR final without sending a new final
  before the gate merges — the gate merges the pinned head and a squash drops later
  pushes (#1826 → ep31).
- **HIGH — Critical:** run the generated-artifact checks the CI runs before calling
  final — five reds in this goal came from skipping them.
- **MEDIUM — Best practice:** regenerate generated files with their tool and commit the
  result; never splice them by hand (S31 forbids growth, S30 keeps merge-only pushes
  cheap).
- **MEDIUM — Best practice:** when a subagent may hit a quota wall, keep its WIP in a
  committed local backup branch so another route can finish it.
- **LOW — Consideration:** prefer REST over GraphQL for PR writes during fleet-wide
  polling.
