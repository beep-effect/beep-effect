## Missed mistakes and implementation consequences

### Missed or underdeveloped category mistakes

1. **A byte string is not its contextual use (NR, role-as-kind risk).** `Artifact.role`, `path`, and `accessClass` (L219–227) attach use/location/classification to a hash-identified byte object. The same PDF bytes can be evidence in one capture and a primary output in another. Reusing the hash then makes role an apparently intrinsic property. No rigid Role subclass is present; the landed issue is the audit's unqualified byte-identity interpretation of these contextual fields.

2. **Stable source, revision request and observed snapshot overlap (SE/SC).** `Source.revision` coexists with `Source.versions[].revision`; captures separately carry requested and captured revisions. `SourceVersion` has locators but no immutable-content binding. An unversioned web URL requested twice can have the same revision descriptor and different captured bytes. The audit's “snapshot” claim therefore exceeds the observations. A record does not become an enduring Expression just because capture snapshots are separate.

3. **Capture disposition is multidimensional (RP/EV).** `status`, `complete`, and `reason` can disagree without violating these field schemas: `status="readable"`, `complete=false`, empty artifacts is structurally permitted. Readable-for-extraction and complete-for-a-specific-question need different evidence. The schema alone does not assert world truth, but it also does not justify calling all these values intrinsic phases or treating `complete` as universally applicable.

4. **Intake ID does not necessarily identify the depth anchor (NR).** One intake can include report A directly citing X and report B citing Y which cites X. X has depths 1 and 2 relative to different roots inside the same intake. `Derivation.intakeId` alone cannot distinguish those paths or decide shortest-path versus path-specific depth. An edge depth also needs a convention: parent depth, child depth or distance of the whole derivation. The audit's identity pair omits even the intake context.

5. **Finding evidential applicability is not byte-bound (EV/NR).** `Finding` has a stable `sourceId`, untyped `span`, and optional-by-empty-string `occurrenceId`/`modelRunId`, with no capture/artifact/version identifier (L662–679). If a page changes between reads, the same span and source can point to different text. A summary saying “X supports Y” is not evidence that the source asserted that relationship; no asserting reading/agent/context is required here. This is an exposure to evidence-is-truth, not a claim that any uninspected projector actually makes that inference. Packet relevance likewise has no time or liveness evidence in `topicIds`.

6. **Probe result, policy and capability can collapse (EV, Policy-as-fact, NR).** `Qualification` has adapter, status, required, time and evidence, but no target, adapter revision, environment or credential-scope identity. A route verified for one public page may fail for a private page. `required=true` is a policy judgment; it does not describe what occurred or what is universally necessary. Timestamping a result does not supply its applicability context.

7. **Privacy is assigned, not an intrinsic byte property (RP/NR).** Identical bytes can be public in one context and private in another. Sources/artifacts default missing classification to public, while Document, Finding, Occurrence and Intake have no direct partition. A private DM title or finding can exist without a classified Source. CQ5 enforcement may be implemented elsewhere, but the schema alone neither classifies every potentially private record nor establishes propagation and conflict semantics.

8. **The grouped row conceals distinct referents (SY/SE/IC).** Individually: `Qualification` needs probe-versus-result identity; `Intake` needs census-versus-intake-execution identity; `IntakeFile` needs file-content-versus-census-membership identity; `IntakeRoot` needs `(intake, repository/root, commit)` context; `Reference` needs its containing document/snapshot and reference-site context; `Manifest` needs layout-specification-versus-particular-file identity. In particular, `Reference` has no `id`, hash, containing-document ID or path (L293–333), and `Manifest` has no such key either (L633–645). The blanket identity statement cannot apply literally to them.

9. **Closure and expected counts are not automatically facts (CL/EV).** `Intake.files` and hashes support a census, but do not declare which roots, inclusion/exclusion rules or time boundary make it complete. `Document.expectedOccurrences` (L273) has no estimator/parser-version basis. It could be an exact parse count, a manual expectation or an estimate. **INDETERMINATE — estimate-as-quality:** no percentile/bound field is observed, so that failure mode is not established. Evidence needed: count derivation and whether it is normative, estimated or measured. A zero missing-count over a partial intake is a concrete vacuous-green countermodel.

10. **Coverage omissions in the audit (SY/ET).** `LibraryCatalogSchemaVersion`, `LibraryCitoRelation`, and `LIBRARY_VALIDATOR_CONTRACT` are observed but not dispositioned in the verdict table. Their existence does not warrant new classes. The schema-version and validator-contract strings describe specifications, not executions; the relation vocabulary needs predicate argument semantics, not just allowed local names. No supplied superclass graph demonstrates an anti-rigid superclass with a rigid subclass or conflicting inherited identity providers; those OntoClean BLOCK checks remain untested rather than passed.

## Revision requests

1. **Reopen source identity and category.** Provide explicit sameness/persistence tests across resolution changes, URL aliases, repository commits, source relocation and loss of citation membership. Distinguish resource, cited-resource role, expression, revision request and captured snapshot. Explain which distinctions are necessary for which operational decision; retain unresolved alternatives where the observations cannot decide.

2. **Supply per-term identity analyses.** Replace minted ID/path/hash assertions with criteria for each claimed referent, plus false-merge and false-split fixtures. Address records versus content, multi-target citation sites, simultaneous executions, portable catalogs and contextual artifact uses. State the practical hash assumption separately from the semantic equality criterion.

3. **Replace the phase blanket.** Analyze lifecycle, terminal result, epistemic assessment, route capability and classification independently. Test intrinsic versus relational dependence and possible change while the bearer persists. Explain the four actual Ownership values, not just two. Do not infer anti-rigidity merely from a field being editable or a result being a vocabulary member.

4. **Reassess run/agent/plan commitments.** Keep execution, result record, repeatable model configuration, software agent and acquisition route specification distinct in the proposal. Require compatibility evidence before assigning external classes; include the bare-link-list/DM counterexample to Report typing and the site-versus-relation counterexample to Citation typing.

5. **Replace every failed null discriminator.** For each retained term, identify observation-backed evidence that fails in the specific pure-DTO twin given above. CQ usefulness, persistence, checksums, IDs, validation coverage and annotations alone do not meet this test. If no such evidence exists, preserve null or record insufficient evidence; do not force admission.

6. **Make warrants term-specific.** Separate necessary decision terms from support terms and name the decision term each support serves. Individually review all six terms hidden in row 17 and disposition the omitted vocabulary/contract symbols. Bind CQ claims to the brief's actual decisions and state where a formal Must/Should CQ and fixture are still missing.

7. **Resolve the depth and applicability counterexamples.** Specify anchor/path identity and depth convention for multiple roots and multiple paths within one intake. State how evidence binds a finding to source version, capture, bytes, reading and assessment context; state scope for qualification, completeness and topic liveness. Do not equate a relation code or successful probe with world truth.

8. **Provide evidence for consequences outside the schema.** Cite fold inputs/exclusions, mappings and emitted graphs for Activity/agent alignment, SourceVersion non-subsumption, SKOS/SHACL vocabularies, and DTO exclusion. Include positive witnesses and must-fail counterexamples; a schema annotation or an empty passing query is insufficient. Mark each consequence unverified until the relevant evidence exists.

9. **State census and privacy contracts.** Specify closure scope for “missing,” provenance for expected counts, and partition propagation/conflict behavior for private documents, findings and identical-byte artifacts. Distinguish schema guarantees from projector/validator guarantees and test them against private records without a classified source.

10. **Limit the acceptance claim to the evidence and authority actually supplied.** The shared six-law text requires named-human acceptance; the audit instead invokes the repo autonomy charter as a replacement. That charter is a real higher-level instruction, so this review does not invent an approval stop. Nevertheless, an autonomous reversible decision is not evidence that named-human ratification occurred. Label the authority/waiver explicitly, preserve the unresolved semantic commitments, and replace “drift cannot happen” with a testable statement about pinned inputs, mappings and rendered outputs. This Markdown review is not a successful full artifact-validator or reasoner gate.
