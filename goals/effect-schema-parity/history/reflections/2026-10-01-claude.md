# Reflection — effect-schema-parity (2026-10-01, claude)

## Summary

The packet retired or trimmed every `@beep/schema` concept upstream Effect
covers at `df77fff939`. The operator ruled the two boundary deferrals on
2026-10-01: URL is ADAPT (upstream `S.URL` re-encodes a canonical `href`, a
different concept) and MimeType is KEEP (upstream `Mime` is a lookup over a
subset of the accepted types). The P5 close
retired `withCodecStatics` and every codec facade attached through
`withStatics` (390 steps in 170 files; 833 compiler-located reads plus 25 by
hand), added the `SFV4-codec-static` gate, fixed the F13 guard sites, and
left the schema-first parity backlog at zero. Single-checker instantiations
fell on all three baseline packages.

## Tooling experience

- **Worked:** the whole-repo tsgo program (72 s) as a rewrite oracle;
  `beep lint schema-first` occurrence anchors (the F15 rule plugged into the
  P4 ratchet without new plumbing); `beep quality check-census --gate-only`
  for deterministic before/after numbers; the #1347 precedent for sync codecs.
- **Didn't:** the ts-morph codemod engine at repo scale (14+ minutes per
  package); `beep lint effect-vitest --write` rewrote 56k lines of inventory
  for 17 re-anchored findings, so the anchors were patched in place.
- **Frustrating:** baselines stale on main (catalog, check-census) inflate a
  closeout diff with drift the PR did not cause; app-local `@/*` aliases make
  a root program blind to app reads.
- **Wished existed:** a per-file re-anchor mode for the effect-vitest
  inventory; a hosted check-census lane.

## Implementation improvement opportunities

- The codemod scripts live outside the repo (lane-local); a future
  retirement of the same shape would benefit from a committed
  "remove, then rewrite from diagnostics" driver in repo-cli.
- `withStatics` still carries 200 domain helpers; a follow-up could census
  whether those helpers belong as module functions instead of statics.
- Class-body static codecs (`static readonly decodeOption = S.decodeUnknownOption(Self)`)
  remain; they are a different idiom and were left out of F15 on purpose.

## Goal & prompt critique

- The P5 row bundled four kinds of work (detector, retirement, perf gate,
  closeout). P5a split the perf gate out, which helped; the statics
  retirement would also have been easier as its own PR before the closeout.
- GOAL.md should say that "backlog zero" depends on every sibling
  retirement landing first (group C held the opaque backlog until it merged).

## TODOs worth codifying

- Scoped effect-vitest re-anchor.

## Lessons (confidence-tiered)

- **HIGH — Critical:** Let the compiler find the call sites of a retired
  member — delete first, rewrite from diagnostics. Text censuses collide on
  member names and checker-driven ts-morph scans do not scale here.
- **MEDIUM — Best practice:** Expect lint laws that a wrapper hid to fire
  once the wrapper goes; budget a pass for them in the same PR.
- **LOW — Consideration:** Re-measure committed performance baselines in
  every PR that moves them, or they drift until a gate run fails on an
  untouched tree.
