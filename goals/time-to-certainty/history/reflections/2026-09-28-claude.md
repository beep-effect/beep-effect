## Tooling experience

- **Worked:** the attempt journal facts (A5), detached proof jobs (B5), `--until-ready` (B7) and the
  heavy admission label (B8) turned PR babysitting into a job the agent can wait on and read back,
  and the friction ledger kept every hard lesson as a dated receipt instead of a closeout memory.
- **Worked:** adversarial Opus 5.5 refute rounds caught factual errors in receipts and rulings before
  they were published, three times on 2026-09-28 alone.
- **Didn't:** fresh lane worktrees repeatedly lacked built upstream outputs, the Effect reference
  checkout, or a signing socket, and each cost a round before the real work started.
- **Didn't:** inherited reds on `main` (coverage, oxlint, fallow) taxed unrelated PRs for hours at a
  time, and two heavy runners for the fleet made queue time the largest term of several trains.
- **Frustrating:** hosted and local capture limits (64 KiB console, 512 KiB step capture) hid the
  failing step more than once.
- **Wished existed:** a per-clone view of every session's live processes, so a sweep or a retire can
  see another session's running work before it acts.

## Implementation improvement opportunities

- The proof ledger's concurrency relies on one `O_APPEND` write per attempt and a tolerant reader;
  if `proof-report` ever shows malformed rows on the shared ledger, the next step is a rename
  publish per append, not a lock.
- The epoch covers six root inputs; `standards/*.jsonc`, the Biome configuration, the workflow
  files and `scripts/` are lane inputs outside it. Close that gap before any reuse is enforced.
- Attempt journals die with the lane just as the proof ledger did, so the close M3 join matched 86
  of 977 PR runs; moving journals to the owning clone is ruling 71's twin and would make the next
  close run's M3 honest.
- Classify the M4 fingerprint repeats by the three named false-red classes (B1, B2, B4), or the
  gate's line 3 stays unanswerable however long the sample runs.

## Lessons (confidence-tiered)

- **HIGH — Critical:** Make every safety criterion able to fail in production before counting it —
  a bar that passes by construction is not evidence (ruling 72).
- **HIGH — Critical:** Size an evidence store's lifetime to its sample — per-lane stores deleted at
  retirement can never reach a cross-branch bar (ruling 71).
- **MEDIUM — Best practice:** Pause a packet on a recorded flip condition when the remaining outcome
  needs time the packet cannot buy, and leave closing with lines carried to the operator.
- **LOW — Consideration:** Batch the remaining packet work into one PR when the operator pays for
  runners and queue time; the rulings and the close measurement read better together.
