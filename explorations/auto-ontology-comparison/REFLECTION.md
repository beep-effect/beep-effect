# Research closeout reflection

Two completed assessments and divergent ideation produced a bounded decision:
retain the current architecture and test specific governance and validation gaps.
The useful correction was to inspect consumers as well as models: formal schemas,
certification flags and export syntax do not establish end-to-end guarantees.

Provider failures delayed completion without adding evidence. Missing assessments
are now recorded as omissions, and the final research scope does not depend on
retrying them. The public packet contains pinned evidence links and corrected
experiment plans, rather than presenting unedited proposals as established facts.

No runtime experiment or adoption was validated. The next evidence-bearing step
is a separately admitted deterministic fixture experiment, chosen for an actual
consumer. Keep annotation parity, temporal targets and unsupported constraints
explicit; otherwise a comparative result can measure the harness instead of the
systems.
