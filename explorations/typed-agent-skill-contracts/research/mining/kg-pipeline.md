# Lane: kg-generation-pipeline

## Inventory table

| skill | one-line what-it-is | maturity (prototype/solid/polished) | steal-score 0-5 |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| `kg-generator` | Prompt-heavy router for turning URLs/files into schema.org-first JSON-LD or Turtle, with content-shape templates and a grep-based compliance audit. | solid | 4 |
| `document-to-kg-skill` | Four-stage harness that freezes source/base/format/destination, generates RDF, pauses for enrichment approval, then saves. | solid | 4 |
| `csv-to-rdf-det-generator` | Design checklist for a Virtuoso WebDAV ingestion adapter that maps CSV rows to RDF before named-graph load. | prototype | 3 |

## Per-skill notes

### kg-generator

- What it actually does: routes source acquisition, chooses one of six content-shape prompts, asks for serialization and generation modality, instructs the model to mint RDF, runs a shell compliance audit, and optionally hands RDF to a separate infographic harness; the operational sequence is in `ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md` and the only executable gate is `ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/scripts/validate-kg-compliance.sh`.
- Its useful architectural boundary is “fetch first, transform second”: browser/REST/MCP/OAuth routing is scoped to content retrieval, while RDF construction happens after extraction; this prevents network/tool routing from becoming part of the semantic transformation contract (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`).
- It distinguishes document entity IRIs from named-graph storage IRIs: source URL plus fragment for domain entities, DAV artifact URL for the SPARQL `GRAPH` target (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`).
- It has explicit content-shape templates for generic documents, business/market analysis, conference recaps, thesis/framework articles, social posts with threaded comments, and news articles with separately attributed agent commentary (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`).
- The social-thread template preserves verbatim post/comment text, explicit `schema:parentItem`, thread-wide `schema:position`, reference-link lists, and exact interaction counts (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`).
- The thesis/news templates separate source-authored material from agent-authored commentary: the skill is `schema:author`, the human is `schema:accountablePerson`, and `prov:wasGeneratedBy` records generation (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`).
- Collection handling is unusually concrete: inspect sitemap/navigation/search indexes, model endpoints/services, preserve SPARQL text as `schema:SoftwareSourceCode`, link it to `schema:target`, and retain encoded live-query actions (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`).
- Notable human-read files: `ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md` for the complete contract; `ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/scripts/validate-kg-compliance.sh` for the real enforcement boundary; `ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/references/identifier-patterns.md` and `ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/references/naics-identifier-pattern.md` for identifier policy; `ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/prompts/generic-jsonld.md` and `ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/prompts/business-market-analysis-turtle.md` for older standalone prompt variants.
- Standout mechanism: category-specific canonical-subject ladders. Organization, software, country, person, and concept identities do not share one naive minting rule; each has an authority priority and a local fallback (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`, `ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/references/identifier-patterns.md`).
- Standout mechanism: source-grounded local fragments plus canonical external subjects. A concept with a confirmed authority IRI should be that subject, not a local alias pointing outward; document-local fragments are reserved for source-specific or unresolved entities (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`).
- Standout mechanism: graph reachability and inverse-link requirements make disconnected extracted facts a compliance concern, not merely a visualization problem (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`).
- Standout mechanism: the `Agent's Choice` split defines a useful production heuristic—large entity/comment counts or SPARQL content select deterministic script-assisted construction, while small sources allow direct generation (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`).
- Standout mechanism: exact-source preservation is made type-specific—numbers for market analysis, exact quotations for event/news material, verbatim text and threading for social posts, framework positions for thesis articles (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`).
- Standout mechanism: NAICS is modeled as a paired invariant—`schema:naics` plus a Census lookup identifier—rather than an opaque `PropertyValue` wrapper (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/references/naics-identifier-pattern.md`).
- Weakness: the contract is duplicated across a 1,292-line skill and two prompt files, and the copies have already diverged. The standalone business prompt hard-codes one specific autopilot-market example and exact 12/10/7 counts, while the main prompt generalizes the source shape and treats counts as floors (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/prompts/business-market-analysis-turtle.md`, `ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`).
- Weakness: format policy contradicts itself. Early guidance says not to default to dual-format output; a later “Dual-Format RDF Generation” section says to produce Turtle and JSON-LD by default (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`).
- Weakness: canonical IRI ladders contradict themselves. Software is DBpedia-first near the harness boundary but vendor-homepage-first in later checklists; organization ordering alternates between LinkedIn-before-homepage and vendor-site-before-LinkedIn (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`).
- Weakness: LinkedIn normalization is self-conflicting. Generation rules require `owl:sameAs` between `www` and non-`www` profile forms, while audits explicitly forbid treating those forms as a valid cross-reference (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`).
- Weakness: audit numbering and thresholds drift. Prompts require 10 or 12 questions, while the shell script accepts 8; “X/16” and similar totals do not consistently match the enumerated subchecks (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`, `ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/scripts/validate-kg-compliance.sh`).
- Weakness: the shell gate is lexical, not RDF-aware. It checks strings with `grep`, does not parse RDF, does not validate SHACL, cannot establish reachability/inverse completeness/canonical-primary identity/source coverage, and can reject harmless prefix aliases such as `schema1:` (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/scripts/validate-kg-compliance.sh`).
- Weakness: its class/property coverage check counts only `owl:Class` and `owl:ObjectProperty`, while the prompts and outputs commonly use `rdfs:Class` and `rdf:Property`; this lets a denominator of zero report success (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/scripts/validate-kg-compliance.sh`, `ai-agent-skills/kg-output/the-semantic-medallion-claude_code-1.ttl`).
- Weakness: line 89's backticks execute `, :` as a shell command when the ontology-link check fails; `set -euo pipefail` plus `echo | grep -q` also makes early termination possible on large content (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/scripts/validate-kg-compliance.sh`).
- Weakness: “script-assisted” is described but no reusable entity-map schema or RDF builder exists under `ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/`; the executable implementation is only the compliance shell script (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`, `ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/scripts/validate-kg-compliance.sh`).

### document-to-kg-skill

- What it actually does: wraps generic document conversion in a strict four-step dialogue—collect source/base/format/destination, call `OAI.DBA.chatPromptComplete`, perform mandatory review and enrichment approval, then save a slug-derived RDF file (`ai-agent-skills/document-to-kg-skill/SKILL.md`).
- Harness mode makes RDF the source of truth and delegates any HTML/Markdown artifact to `rdf-infographic-skill`; it also requires collection discovery and preservation of SPARQL-bearing pages (`ai-agent-skills/document-to-kg-skill/SKILL.md`).
- Notable human-read files: `ai-agent-skills/document-to-kg-skill/SKILL.md` for lifecycle and authority rules; `ai-agent-skills/document-to-kg-skill/references/document-to-knowledge-graph-prompt.md` for the actual model contract.
- Standout mechanism: all four session variables are frozen before generation, which gives the pipeline an explicit input manifest rather than relying on conversational inference (`ai-agent-skills/document-to-kg-skill/SKILL.md`).
- Standout mechanism: post-generation enrichment is approval-gated. Candidate FAQ/terms/HowTos and additional entity types are proposed separately, then incorporated only after approval (`ai-agent-skills/document-to-kg-skill/SKILL.md`).
- Standout mechanism: failure is supposed to be closed for ambiguous source, canonical page URL, destination, resolver, and artifact scope (`ai-agent-skills/document-to-kg-skill/SKILL.md`).
- Standout mechanism: authority denotation is centralized by category and explicitly rejects fabricated DBpedia/Wikidata identities (`ai-agent-skills/document-to-kg-skill/SKILL.md`).
- Standout mechanism: source collections and executable SPARQL examples have first-class coverage requirements, including endpoint target and live-query action links (`ai-agent-skills/document-to-kg-skill/SKILL.md`).
- Weakness: the dialogue is over-serialized. Mandatory announcement, wait, full input confirmation, generated output, approval round, and final save are safe but expensive for automated fleet use (`ai-agent-skills/document-to-kg-skill/SKILL.md`).
- Weakness: `file:` policy conflicts. Source handling says a user-approved `file:` base is acceptable when no canonical URL exists, but the checklist and prompt categorically ban `file:` IRIs (`ai-agent-skills/document-to-kg-skill/SKILL.md`, `ai-agent-skills/document-to-kg-skill/references/document-to-knowledge-graph-prompt.md`).
- Weakness: the prompt's numbered requirements are out of order and duplicated (`30`, then another `30`, then `31`, `33`, `32`), and the Turtle note says all 21 guidelines apply although the prompt contains more than 30 (`ai-agent-skills/document-to-kg-skill/references/document-to-knowledge-graph-prompt.md`).
- Weakness: the “0 FAIL” self-audit has only 11 broad assertions and does not cover all prompt requirements, despite claiming one check per rule (`ai-agent-skills/document-to-kg-skill/references/document-to-knowledge-graph-prompt.md`).
- Weakness: there is no executable parser, SHACL suite, canonical-IRI resolver, source-span coverage check, or test fixture in this directory; validity is delegated to model self-review (`ai-agent-skills/document-to-kg-skill/SKILL.md`, `ai-agent-skills/document-to-kg-skill/references/document-to-knowledge-graph-prompt.md`).
- Weakness: it is coupled to OpenLink-specific `OAI.DBA.getSkillResource` and `OAI.DBA.chatPromptComplete`, so the useful harness must be separated from its transport to port cleanly (`ai-agent-skills/document-to-kg-skill/SKILL.md`).

### csv-to-rdf-det-generator

- What it actually does: specifies the lifecycle of a Virtuoso WebDAV ingestion adapter—accept upload through `_DAV_RES_UPLOAD`, persist the DAV row, parse CSV, map rows to triples, load a target graph, and persist graph/source/mapping metadata (`ai-agent-skills/csv-to-rdf-det-generator/references/csv-to-rdf-pipeline.md`).
- The assigned files never expand the acronym “DET” and never call it “Data Exchange Template”; mechanically, it is a Virtuoso DAV hook family and graph-backed ingestion variant (`ai-agent-skills/csv-to-rdf-det-generator/SKILL.md`, `ai-agent-skills/csv-to-rdf-det-generator/references/det-hook-checklist.md`).
- Notable human-read files: `ai-agent-skills/csv-to-rdf-det-generator/references/csv-mapping-design.md` for the mapping decision record; `ai-agent-skills/csv-to-rdf-det-generator/references/csv-to-rdf-pipeline.md` for stage order; `ai-agent-skills/csv-to-rdf-det-generator/references/det-hook-checklist.md` for lifecycle coverage; `ai-agent-skills/csv-to-rdf-det-generator/references/verification-queries.md` for smoke probes.
- Standout mechanism: mapping design is explicit before code—header-to-predicate, row-to-subject, base namespace, datatype inference, multi-value cells, nulls, and fixed/generated/user-supplied mapping profile (`ai-agent-skills/csv-to-rdf-det-generator/references/csv-mapping-design.md`).
- Standout mechanism: transformation is separated from directory listing; `_DAV_DIR_LIST` is a pure DAV-row shaping path, while upload/delete/update own side effects (`ai-agent-skills/csv-to-rdf-det-generator/references/csv-det-template.md`).
- Standout mechanism: the minimum verification ladder checks mount, persisted resource, DAV listing, named graph, then one known row's subject and datatypes (`ai-agent-skills/csv-to-rdf-det-generator/references/verification-queries.md`).
- Standout mechanism: enough metadata must survive ingestion to make delete/update capable of reversing graph-side effects (`ai-agent-skills/csv-to-rdf-det-generator/references/det-hook-checklist.md`).
- Weakness: this is a checklist, not a generator. `csv-det-template.md` contains 18 lines of guidance and no SQL template, parser, mapping contract, fixtures, or executable validation (`ai-agent-skills/csv-to-rdf-det-generator/references/csv-det-template.md`).
- Weakness: transactional semantics are unspecified: no rollback when RDF load fails after DAV persistence, no idempotency key, no replace-vs-append rule, no concurrency strategy, and no tombstone/retry state (`ai-agent-skills/csv-to-rdf-det-generator/references/csv-to-rdf-pipeline.md`, `ai-agent-skills/csv-to-rdf-det-generator/references/csv-det-template.md`).
- Weakness: CSV dialect, encoding, quoting, duplicate headers, malicious formula cells, huge-row streaming, and row-level rejection policy are absent (`ai-agent-skills/csv-to-rdf-det-generator/references/csv-mapping-design.md`).
- Weakness: graph verification uses an unbounded `select *`, and none of the checks assert source-to-triple counts, constraint conformance, provenance, or deletion correctness (`ai-agent-skills/csv-to-rdf-det-generator/references/verification-queries.md`).

### rdf-det-variant-generator

- What it actually does: outlines an `RDFImport`-derived Virtuoso DAV adapter for storing RDF resources and loading/removing their named-graph effects through `_DAV_*` hooks (`ai-agent-skills/rdf-det-variant-generator/SKILL.md`, `ai-agent-skills/rdf-det-variant-generator/references/rdf-det-template.md`).
- Notable human-read files: `ai-agent-skills/rdf-det-variant-generator/references/det-hook-checklist.md` for the 13-element DAV row and hook invariants; `ai-agent-skills/rdf-det-variant-generator/references/rdf-det-template.md` for lifecycle ordering; `ai-agent-skills/rdf-det-variant-generator/references/virtuoso-rdf-doc-types.md` for MIME routing boundaries; `ai-agent-skills/rdf-det-variant-generator/references/verification-queries.md` for probes.
- Standout mechanism: lifecycle surface is explicit—search, ID wrapping, single-row materialization, listing, upload, delete, collection creation, parent lookup, and property accessors (`ai-agent-skills/rdf-det-variant-generator/references/det-hook-checklist.md`).
- Standout mechanism: `_DAV_RES_UPLOAD` ordering is specified as DAV write, DET metadata persistence, then RDF load/queue; delete must clear both DAV state and graph effects (`ai-agent-skills/rdf-det-variant-generator/references/rdf-det-template.md`).
- Standout mechanism: list construction prefers explicit vector accumulation when builder behavior is uncertain, and `_DAV_DIR_SINGLE` has a 13-element row invariant (`ai-agent-skills/rdf-det-variant-generator/SKILL.md`, `ai-agent-skills/rdf-det-variant-generator/references/det-hook-checklist.md`).
- Standout mechanism: content-type routing must be centralized and limited to loaders proven available in the target Virtuoso deployment (`ai-agent-skills/rdf-det-variant-generator/references/virtuoso-rdf-doc-types.md`).
- Weakness: no runtime SQL exists despite the skill promising scaffolding; the “template” is 22 lines of prose (`ai-agent-skills/rdf-det-variant-generator/SKILL.md`, `ai-agent-skills/rdf-det-variant-generator/references/rdf-det-template.md`).
- Weakness: the supported-format list is intentionally vague, with “other RDF syntaxes” deferred to deployment inspection; there is no capability-discovery procedure or MIME-to-loader table (`ai-agent-skills/rdf-det-variant-generator/references/virtuoso-rdf-doc-types.md`).
- Weakness: no atomicity, graph collision policy, parse-failure state, queue contract, checksum, retry, duplicate upload behavior, or security boundary is specified (`ai-agent-skills/rdf-det-variant-generator/references/rdf-det-template.md`).
- Weakness: verification proves only that rows and some triples exist; it does not prove the correct graph, replace/delete symmetry, isolation, or absence of partial loads (`ai-agent-skills/rdf-det-variant-generator/references/verification-queries.md`).

### infographic-describer

- What it actually does: scrapes a WebDAV HTML directory listing, classifies supported file extensions, derives names/categories from filenames, probes thumbnails, emits Turtle metadata, and parses the result with RDFLib if installed (`ai-agent-skills/infographic-describer/scripts/describe-infographics.py`).
- Notable human-read files: `ai-agent-skills/infographic-describer/scripts/describe-infographics.py` for actual behavior; `ai-agent-skills/infographic-describer/references/shacl-templates.md` for intended property cardinalities; `ai-agent-skills/infographic-describer/SKILL.md` for the discovery/probe/generation workflow.
- Standout mechanism: content IRI and entity IRI are deliberately distinct—file URL versus file URL plus `#this`—and `wdrs:describedby` points to the resolver description (`ai-agent-skills/infographic-describer/SKILL.md`, `ai-agent-skills/infographic-describer/scripts/describe-infographics.py`).
- Standout mechanism: optional metadata is probed rather than fabricated; a thumbnail triple appears only after HTTP 200 (`ai-agent-skills/infographic-describer/SKILL.md`, `ai-agent-skills/infographic-describer/scripts/describe-infographics.py`).
- Standout mechanism: the shape document distinguishes mandatory name/content/type/encoding properties from optional thumbnail and dates (`ai-agent-skills/infographic-describer/references/shacl-templates.md`).
- Standout mechanism: serialization escapes backslashes and quotes before Turtle generation, then syntax-parses the saved graph when RDFLib is available (`ai-agent-skills/infographic-describer/scripts/describe-infographics.py`).
- Weakness: “using SHACL shapes as the property contract” is not executable. The script never loads the shape file, never runs a SHACL engine, and validates syntax only (`ai-agent-skills/infographic-describer/SKILL.md`, `ai-agent-skills/infographic-describer/scripts/describe-infographics.py`, `ai-agent-skills/infographic-describer/references/shacl-templates.md`).
- Weakness: `check_describe()` is defined but never called, so existing store metadata is not actually merged or used to improve descriptions (`ai-agent-skills/infographic-describer/scripts/describe-infographics.py`).
- Weakness: descriptions are always `"Infographic: {filename-derived name}"`, including MP4 and HTML resources, and category detection is substring-based (`ai-agent-skills/infographic-describer/scripts/describe-infographics.py`).
- Weakness: the default output path is a hard-coded personal macOS directory, while the rest of the repository guidance is intended to be reusable (`ai-agent-skills/infographic-describer/SKILL.md`, `ai-agent-skills/infographic-describer/scripts/describe-infographics.py`).
- Weakness: if RDFLib is unavailable, the script labels the save as success without syntax validation; it also catches all thumbnail errors as “no thumbnail,” losing error provenance (`ai-agent-skills/infographic-describer/scripts/describe-infographics.py`).
- Weakness: no `prov:wasGeneratedBy`, source listing snapshot, HTTP response timestamp/ETag, checksum, or extraction confidence is emitted (`ai-agent-skills/infographic-describer/scripts/describe-infographics.py`).

## Cross-cutting patterns in this lane

- The implicit pipeline is: acquire source → freeze canonical source identity → select a content/mapping profile → mint/resolve entity IRIs → construct RDF → syntax parse → contract audit → save → optionally load named graph through DAV → verify with SPARQL → derive HTML/Markdown views (`ai-agent-skills/document-to-kg-skill/SKILL.md`, `ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`, `ai-agent-skills/csv-to-rdf-det-generator/references/csv-to-rdf-pipeline.md`, `ai-agent-skills/rdf-det-variant-generator/references/rdf-det-template.md`).
- There are three distinct identity planes: source/document IRI, entity IRI, and storage/named-graph IRI. The repository is at its best when it keeps these separate (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`, `ai-agent-skills/infographic-describer/SKILL.md`).
- “Schema-first” here means prompt-level property/type checklists, not a compiled domain model. The shape information is scattered among prose, tables, SHACL snippets, and regexes (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`, `ai-agent-skills/infographic-describer/references/shacl-templates.md`, `ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/scripts/validate-kg-compliance.sh`).
- Provenance is strongest at artifact/agent and coarse source levels (`prov:wasGeneratedBy`, occasional `prov:wasDerivedFrom`), but weak at claim/evidence-span level (`ai-agent-skills/kg-output/loops-to-graphs-semantic-web-context-claude_code-1.ttl`, `ai-agent-skills/kg-output/the-semantic-medallion-claude_code-1.ttl`).
- The two DET skills correctly treat delete/update behavior as part of ingestion correctness, not an afterthought, but neither specifies the atomic state machine needed to guarantee symmetry (`ai-agent-skills/csv-to-rdf-det-generator/references/det-hook-checklist.md`, `ai-agent-skills/rdf-det-variant-generator/references/det-hook-checklist.md`).
- Validation is layered in the prose—syntax, structural compliance, source preservation, authority identity, query probes—but only syntax and shallow lexical checks are executable (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/scripts/validate-kg-compliance.sh`, `ai-agent-skills/infographic-describer/scripts/describe-infographics.py`).
- The skills repeatedly encode “do not fabricate” and “preserve exact wording/numbers,” yet none stores source spans or extraction evidence sufficient to prove those claims (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`, `ai-agent-skills/document-to-kg-skill/references/document-to-knowledge-graph-prompt.md`).
- Companion HTML/Markdown is conceptually a projection of RDF, a valuable anti-drift rule, but the lane delegates its enforcement to an unassigned skill rather than providing a local end-to-end gate (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`, `ai-agent-skills/document-to-kg-skill/SKILL.md`).
- Explicit RDF contracts buy deterministic joins, durable identifiers, type-aware errors, queryable provenance, reversible ingestion, and stable external interchange. A cognee-style embedding-plus-graph ingestion buys fast fuzzy recall and lower initial modeling cost, but cannot by itself guarantee which entity an embedding hit denotes, whether a claim came from source or agent commentary, or whether delete/update removed the right graph; those are exactly the contracts emphasized by `ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md` and the DET checklists.
- The productive comparison is not RDF versus embeddings. RDF should be the canonical assertion/provenance layer; embeddings should be a derived discovery index keyed by canonical entity/claim/source-span IDs and safely rebuildable from the explicit graph (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`, `ai-agent-skills/csv-to-rdf-det-generator/references/csv-mapping-design.md`).

## Steal-worthy for beep-effect (ranked, concrete — name the mechanism and the port)

1. **Typed ingestion manifest.** Port the four frozen inputs and mapping choices into an Effect `Schema.Class` such as `IngestionManifest { source, canonicalDocumentIri, serialization, destination, artifactScope, mappingProfileId }`; reject ambiguity with tagged errors before acquisition or generation (`ai-agent-skills/document-to-kg-skill/SKILL.md`, `ai-agent-skills/csv-to-rdf-det-generator/references/csv-mapping-design.md`).
2. **Phase-typed source→graph state machine.** Model `Acquired → Normalized → EntityMapped → Serialized → SyntaxValidated → ContractValidated → Persisted → Indexed` as branded states/services, with `ParseError`, `IriResolutionError`, `ConstraintViolation`, `PersistenceError`, and `VerificationError`; encode the DET upload/delete ordering as transactions and compensations (`ai-agent-skills/csv-to-rdf-det-generator/references/csv-to-rdf-pipeline.md`, `ai-agent-skills/rdf-det-variant-generator/references/rdf-det-template.md`).
3. **Canonical IRI policy service.** Replace prompt ladders with a `Context.Service` that accepts entity category plus evidence candidates and returns `CanonicalIriDecision { selected, tier, alternatives, evidence, confidence }`; make “no confident authority” a valid local-fallback result rather than a hallucination opportunity (`ai-agent-skills/document-to-kg-skill/SKILL.md`, `ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`).
4. **Source-span provenance ledger.** Give every extracted claim/entity/property an evidence record containing source artifact ID, byte/text span, extractor/agent ID, timestamp, modality, and transformation activity; emit PROV-O from that ledger instead of attaching only document-level `prov:wasGeneratedBy` (`ai-agent-skills/kg-output/loops-to-graphs-semantic-web-context-claude_code-1.ttl`, `ai-agent-skills/kg-output/the-semantic-medallion-claude_code-1.ttl`).
5. **Executable contract registry.** Express prompt requirements once as Effect schemas plus rule IDs; generate model instructions, SHACL, CLI checks, and human checklists from the same registry. This directly removes the threshold/ordering contradictions in `ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md` and `ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/scripts/validate-kg-compliance.sh`.
6. **Parser-first multi-gate validation.** Gate in order: RDF parse → vocabulary existence/range checks → SHACL → graph reachability/inverse policy → canonical-IRI policy → source-coverage/provenance → named-graph smoke queries. Never use regex as the truth layer (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/scripts/validate-kg-compliance.sh`, `ai-agent-skills/infographic-describer/references/shacl-templates.md`, `ai-agent-skills/rdf-det-variant-generator/references/verification-queries.md`).
7. **Deterministic CSV mapping profile.** Port header normalization, predicate mapping, subject keys, datatype rules, multi-value syntax, and null policy into a versioned `CsvMappingProfile`; hash it and attach the hash/profile ID to every ingest activity and graph (`ai-agent-skills/csv-to-rdf-det-generator/references/csv-mapping-design.md`, `ai-agent-skills/csv-to-rdf-det-generator/references/csv-to-rdf-pipeline.md`).
8. **Reversible graph publication contract.** Make every uploaded artifact own a graph version and content hash; update is compare-and-swap or replace-by-version, delete uses recorded graph ownership, and all failure paths compensate. This makes the good DET delete/update intuition real (`ai-agent-skills/csv-to-rdf-det-generator/references/det-hook-checklist.md`, `ai-agent-skills/rdf-det-variant-generator/references/det-hook-checklist.md`).
9. **RDF-first projection boundary.** Generate HTML/Markdown/browser QA views only from a validated graph snapshot, and store the snapshot hash in each projection so parity can be tested (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`, `ai-agent-skills/document-to-kg-skill/SKILL.md`).
10. **Verbatim-thread contract.** For legal/patent discussions, reviews, and agent-to-agent traces, reuse the social-thread mechanics: immutable verbatim message payload, parent edge, global sequence, author identity, edit marker, and separately modeled analytical restructuring (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`).
11. **Agent-authored commentary attribution.** Preserve the distinction between `author` as generating software agent and `accountablePerson` as principal; add the agent invocation/run IRI and prompt/skill contract version for stronger multi-agent provenance (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`).
12. **Hybrid KG plus embedding index.** Ingest validated entity/claim/source-span records into cognee (or another vector/KG discovery layer) only after canonical IDs exist; store embedding model/version as derived-artifact provenance and allow total reindex from RDF (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`, `ai-agent-skills/kg-output/the-semantic-medallion-claude_code-1.ttl`).

## Dead ends (what to NOT spend time on, and why)

- Do not port the 1,292-line prompt wholesale. Its repetition and internal contradictions are maintenance debt; extract a rule registry and content-profile deltas instead (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md`).
- Do not treat `validate-kg-compliance.sh` as a validator design to emulate. Keep its human-readable rule IDs, discard the grep implementation (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/scripts/validate-kg-compliance.sh`).
- Do not spend time porting the hard-coded autopilot market-analysis prompt; it is a stale worked example disguised as a reusable template (`ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/prompts/business-market-analysis-turtle.md`).
- Do not claim SHACL compliance from the infographic skill. The shape snippets are useful requirements, but no SHACL execution exists (`ai-agent-skills/infographic-describer/references/shacl-templates.md`, `ai-agent-skills/infographic-describer/scripts/describe-infographics.py`).
- Do not implement a Virtuoso DET from either “template” without first obtaining a real `RDFImport` implementation and deployment-specific loader contracts; the assigned files contain no executable SQL (`ai-agent-skills/rdf-det-variant-generator/references/rdf-det-template.md`, `ai-agent-skills/csv-to-rdf-det-generator/references/csv-det-template.md`).
- Do not use FAQ/glossary minimum counts as semantic coverage. They encourage padding and still miss literal superclass errors and authority drift seen in the corpus (`ai-agent-skills/kg-output/incident-report-2026-feb-apr-1.ttl`, `ai-agent-skills/kg-output/loops-to-graphs-semantic-web-context-claude_code-1.ttl`).
- Do not adopt a global DBpedia-first rule without evidence and ambiguity handling. The valuable part is category-specific, auditable selection—not blind authority substitution (`ai-agent-skills/document-to-kg-skill/SKILL.md`).
- Do not use the generated corpus as training gold or compliance fixtures without expected findings and contract-version labels; parseable does not mean semantically conformant (`ai-agent-skills/kg-output/README.md`, `ai-agent-skills/kg-output/incident-report-2026-feb-apr-1.ttl`).
- Do not make embeddings the canonical store. They are excellent derived retrieval indexes but weak authorities for identity, provenance, reversibility, and compliance—the exact concerns the useful portions of `ai-agent-skills/kg-generator/SKILL.md` and the DET lifecycle checklists make explicit.
