# Area B — coverage, independent Astra phase-2 audit

## Verdict

Keep the full hosted, fresh, run-bound coverage proof. Add a bounded **advisory direct-owner pre-publish ratchet**, with canonical runtimes and explicit incomplete results; do not put owners-plus-dependents coverage on the synchronous publish path. Prioritize overlapping independent artifact validation with producer work, then runtime parity and accurate attribution. File-related tests are useful diagnostics for the two long poles, not substitutes for package totals or the hosted census. None of these findings establishes the under-ten-minute p95 target today.

The operator must choose whether Coverage Regression is genuinely a merge requirement. Recommend making `Heavy / Coverage Regression` required, with skip-satisfied routing and the existing explicitly attributed S11 exception handled consistently. Removing it from Yeet's mergeable gate is a real reduction in pre-merge assurance, not a performance optimization. Keeping it running while ignoring its result saves no runner compute.

Scope: read-only research, no tests, coverage, installs, Git writes, baseline writes, or tracked edits. Assumed #3005, #3007 and #3014 merged for prospective estimates; their implementation is not proposed again. Did not inspect the parallel Opus seat's output.

### Ranking calculation and limits

Use fleet local volumes above, not fleet 2,579 as a weekly number. Let H = 707.7 weekly PR arrivals times the fraction that actually executes the comparator; that fraction is not established by the 100-run outcome list. Let P = 87.32 weekly publishes and V = 52.25 verifies. Score = `(H × hosted runner-minute savings + P × publish wall savings + V × verify wall savings) / risk weight`, with weights low=1, medium=3, high=9. This is the brief's mixed-resource prioritization heuristic; retain its components separately and never equate it with CPU or critical-path savings. Negative savings mean added work. Risk weights are explicit judgment, not empirical probabilities.

No positive measured weekly savings can be claimed. Ranked order below is a **conditional engineering ranking**, not a fabricated quantitative leaderboard: L3 is the only candidate for unconditional steady-state latency savings; L7/L5 tie at zero booked savings and precede negative-cost diagnostics because they are low-risk prerequisites. L1/L2 can rank higher only after avoided-red attribution is measured. L8 is a policy choice, excluded from optimization scoring.

Illustration, not forecast: H=400, L3 removes 2 min of serial tail but no execution work -> **800 critical-path min/week, zero runner-min savings, score zero**. If it adds 0.25 runner-min/run, score becomes -33.3. L1 adding one 26.7 s owner measurement per publish costs at least **38.86 local min/week**, plus setup/admission. Its break-even requires recovered avoided rerun minutes greater than that cost. The prior-pass 40-run sample (37 green, two cascades, one direct-owner red) is too small and selected to supply an avoidance probability. No assumption that advisory warnings automatically prevent pushes.

## Ranked levers

### 1. COV-L3 — overlap independently validated partitions; retain a complete final join

- **Area/grain:** coverage; package for complete unsplit packages, affected-set for final join and packages distributed over multiple partitions. A partition is a scheduling unit, not necessarily a valid ratchet unit.
- **Mechanism:** split `CoverageArtifacts.collect` into independently verified slice receipts and a final package merge/judge. Change `CiCoverage.ts`, artifact schemas and workflow DAG. Whole packages in mixed bins can be judged as soon as all their canonical invocations finish. LPS/repo-cli require **all** their shards before judging package/file totals. Never compare each shard's percentages to whole-package floors; merge raw maps first. Keep Labs and testOnly inventory validation.
- **Trust boundary:** a step in an untrusted producer cannot certify its own census. First safe slice: trusted validator emits early diagnostics while the existing final consumer independently repeats validation. Second slice removes duplicate work only with a sealed receipt from an independently trusted validator, bound to tested checkout SHA (including synthetic merge SHA), immutable event base, run, producer attempt, validator revision, partition assignment, runtime/provider, census and raw-map digest. Final join independently verifies complete expected assignments and absence of duplicate slices. An incremental artifact-arrival service would add considerable complexity; prefer explicit workflow partitions/validator DAG, not a second scheduler.
- **Reruns:** preserve newest valid producer attempt no later than consumer; invalid newer evidence fails closed. Recheck latest Labs job terminal state and carried-attempt timestamps. An old successful slice cannot mask a later failed producer. Missing partitions never trigger fallback tests.
- **Savings:** hosted runner-min **0 booked; may be negative** from extra setups/duplicate validation. Critical path **0–3 min/run hypothesis after #3007**, bounded by the measured remaining serial validation tail, only when coverage lies on that path. Currently non-required, so contribution to push→required-green is **zero unless producer jobs are lengthened** or policy L8 changes. Local publish/verify **0/0**. CPU approximately unchanged when moved, higher during shadow; no current CPU measurement. Basis: derived DAG opportunity plus guessed post-#3007 bound, not the old five-minute comparator headline.
- **Risk:** high (9): forged partial green, lost shards, rerun mixing, producer self-certification. Mitigation above; final context cannot turn green early.
- **Admission gate:** parity ledger on complete judgments and errors; mutation fixtures for missing/duplicate/future/stale attempts, poisoned census, mismatched instrumentation, failed Labs, testOnly omissions, carried attempts; A/B on identical heads after #3007 with setup/runner sum and p95 end-to-end. No optimization admission merely from a quiet observation window.
- **Prior art/dependencies:** combined producers, bounded artifact validation and independent discovery already shipped; #3007 four-wide work assumed landed. L4 producer-census cache remains rejected. Depends on trusted workflow receipt design; `heavy.yml@main` changes require staged rollout if touched.
- **PR slices:** M “Expose independent partition validation diagnostics” (`CoverageArtifacts*`, tests); L “Join trusted partition verdicts without duplicate validation” (`CiCoverage*`, workflow, receipt schemas/tests); M “Qualify equivalent final coverage context” (parity fixtures/runbook).
