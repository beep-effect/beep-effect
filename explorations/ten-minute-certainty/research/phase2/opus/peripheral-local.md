# Phase 2 audit, Opus 5.5 seat: L2: local and policy surfaces nobody mapped (yeet monitor polling, review window and merge-gate wall, lefthook, merged-preview and quarantine, git/worktree churn, attempt-journal retention, fleet hosts and agent seats in economics, Fallow lanes, changesets/commitlint)

Produced 2026-11-09 by a `claude-opus-5-5` (medium) auditor from the shared brief and the phase 1 inventory; read-only. Paths are repository-relative or `~`.

## Verdict

This area adds almost nothing to the critical path of a PR run, and it cannot move ruling 1's push-to-required-green p95. The only hosted runner-minute lever is folding the 3-minute non-required Fallow job into another job, which saves about 75 s of setup per run. The real cost here is unattributed background load from detached `yeet monitor --until-ready` services. There have been 257 monitor jobs on 105 PRs since 11-02, and 56 of those PRs had two or more monitors (one had 16). Right now 4 run concurrently, 3 of them on PR #3007 from the same lane. Each burns about 82-105 CPU-s per hour and holds 215-310 MB RSS (600-700 MB peak). Each polls every ~30-37 s, including during base-conflict or required-red states that only a push can change. Each poll recomputes 20 lane digests and makes about 4 GraphQL and 5-8 REST calls. The user's shared REST budget stood at 2,184 of 5,000 used about 40 minutes into its window, and an earlier lane-timings census already aborted on this rate limit. The 20-minute review window is non-compute wall: it bound 11 of the last 30 merged PRs, adding about 12-20 min after the last check finished. In 5 of those 30 PRs, a merge-from-main was the final push and re-armed the window. Changing that is an operator policy decision, not efficiency work. Lefthook, changesets/commitlint, head-install and local Fallow costs each measure in seconds and are below the noise floor. The exception is the instrumentation-catalog hook, which reparses all of repo-cli (997 files, 20 MB) on each of about 73 repo-cli commits a day. Economics coverage has measurement gaps: T3 lanes, residue archives and remote hosts are missing, and no seat or host field exists. These gaps keep the 10-minute claim from being proved fleet-wide.
