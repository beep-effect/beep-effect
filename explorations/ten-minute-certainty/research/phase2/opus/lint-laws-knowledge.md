# Phase 2 audit, Opus 5.5 seat: E: lint, laws, policy seals, JSDoc inventory/ratchet, schema catalog, knowledge gates (Heavy / Lint Policy, JSDoc Ratchet, local cheap-gates policy lanes)

Produced 2026-11-09 by a `claude-opus-5-5` (medium) auditor from the shared brief and the phase 1 inventory; read-only. Paths are repository-relative or `~`.

## Verdict

Yes. Heavy / Lint Policy can get under 10 min, and the local cheap tier under 2 min, without touching proof depth. Nothing has to be narrowed for that. Across 86 PR jobs over 44.5 h (11-07 20:03Z to 11-09 16:33Z), Lint Policy ran p50 668 s and p95 919 s. The lane splits into four ordered stages. The medium stage takes 383-396 s. The knowledge doctor then runs alone as its own serial stage for p50 205 s. It is currently report-only ("promoted kinds: none"), reads only its own git archives, and is serial only because #1752 predates abreast stages. Moving it abreast of medium inside the existing width budget (L4) removes about 3.4 min from every run. That is the largest lever. The second is the JSDoc inventory, at 152-233 s. It is computed twice per hosted run and again in two local lanes. noResolve (L1, measured 152.6 to 21.8 task-s with identical signatures) shrinks it everywhere. Dropping the copy in non-required Lint Policy (L2) removes a duplicate that the required JSDoc Ratchet context already proves. The double miss is a timing race on main pushes plus a read-only cache on PRs, not a backend difference. Narrowing the escalation triggers (L3) is worth much less than the inventory assumed. Escalation happened on 31 of 59 PRs, not 7 of 9, but changed-scope lanes are only about 60 s faster than full ones (p50 616 vs 676 s). The width-1 unscoped state graph (about 340 s serial: semantic-delta 161, opaque-roundtrips 102, refs-check 32) and the doctor dominate. After L4+L1+L2 I project about 6.5 min full scope and about 8 min changed scope (p90 about 9.8 min). Re-splitting the state width (L4b) is needed for p95 margin. PR C's algorithmic fixes (L9), together with L1, bring the local cheap-gates critical path from p50 240 s to roughly 100-120 s.
