# Phase 2 audit, Opus 5.5 seat: A: yeet publish / verify / final-check / ready

Produced 2026-11-09 by a `claude-opus-5-5` (medium) auditor from the shared brief and the phase 1 inventory; read-only. Paths are repository-relative or `~`.

## Verdict

Publish can get under 10 minutes locally, at p50 and at p95. Verify can get there at p50 for changes that do not reach law-practice-server, but not at p95 until the coverage and test seats shrink that package's affected cost. Three things dominate publish. First, whole-repo cheap-tier scanners that miss Turbo on any .ts edit (on a miss: jsdoc-ratchet:regenerated averages 205 s, effect-imports 82 s, effect-vitest 84 s, schema-first 62 s). Second, admission wait (publish wait p95 277 s, max 644 s over 165 admissions in 2.8 days). Third, the cheap tier holds a 3-token lease while its measured peak is 22 GiB p50. Verify is dominated by something the inventory did not name: the local pre-push tier runs the affected unit tests twice, once as quality:test-unit (p50 678 s) and once as the exclusive quality:coverage lane (p50 692 s). Hosted CI already runs this as a single producer plus a compare-only consumer. The minimal sound publish gate per change kind: docs/goals-only keeps the #3011 selector. A package-local TS edit gets package-grain jsdoc (after the lint seat's noResolve fix) and file-grain effect-imports, and these escalate to repo grain on any shared input (foundation, policy-pack, root config, lockfile). schema-first and effect-vitest stay repo-grain and get faster through algorithmic fixes, because their input closure is unproven (rejection on record). Root, tooling and lockfile changes run the full tier. This is sound because publish only predicts. Hosted Lint Policy and JSDoc Ratchet stay repo-grain and authoritative, so a missed red costs one hosted red, never a stale green on main. The C4.2 ProofLedger flip clears its bar (325/200 attempts, 151/10 branches, 0 disagreements since the exact ruling-83 merge instant, 2026-11-03T03:10:29Z), but it saves far less than the inventory implies: 121 lane-minutes over 6 days, on lanes that were already Turbo-cache hits (2-33 s each). Reusing hosted receipts in final-check (Y7) is blocked mainly by timing, not by the parity-ledger rule: in 24 recent receipts, no hosted Heavy job had finished green before final-check started.
