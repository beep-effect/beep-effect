# deps-toolchain: merged levers and skeptic verdicts

### Verdict (both seats reconciled)

Both seats agree that area H (dependencies, install, toolchain) is a compute lever, not a latency lever. Measured setup (checkout plus install) is 53-57 runner-minutes per green PR run, 14-19% of hosted job wall and about 7% on EC2. It is a fixed 30-75 s per job that runs in parallel, so removing it moves push-to-all-required-green p95 by about 1 minute at most and cannot reach the 10-minute target alone. Hosted frozen installs measured p50 34 s, p90 47 s, range 15-62 s (n=91, Opus) and medians 38-40 s (Astra), against 9-10 s on EC2. The inventory's 31 s ssh2 tax is one outlier warning in about 115 logs; both seats confirm this. The merged ranking follows minutes saved per week divided by trust risk, with about 500 full-setup PR-run equivalents a week (about 700 starts) and a fleet rate of about 87 publishes and 52 verifies a week. The explicit trustedDependencies allowlist (DEPS-01) ranks first. Running Heavy Admission and PR Size on the compiled CI binary (DEPS-02) ranks second because it is the only lever with a measured critical-path gain (about 0.7 min per run on the Change Profile -> Admission -> Heavy chain). The treeless checkout (DEPS-03, owned by the hosted-ci seat) ranks third: its pool is large, but its savings are a guess. Astra's top-3 levers L1 (scoped install) and L4 (root tool hash isolation) are kept but ranked 6 and 7, not dropped. The deciding evidence is Opus's bun.lock closure measurement: root plus repo-cli is 1,687 of the 2,336 installed packages, and package.json:401-402 makes the root postinstall and prepare need infra/@pulumi/gharunners and the CLI. Together these cap the scoped-install saving at about one third of extract time, roughly 1 min per run against Astra's 8-minute guess. L4 has no steady-state hosted saving, because the remote cache absorbs a re-key after the first run and local yeet already forces Turbo on lockfile changes (Planner.ts:944-953). EC2 placement of short lanes (DEPS-04) is high trust risk because of the accepted runner-trust-boundary replay residual, and it depends on an operator money decision. Native-payload trimming (DEPS-05) must be done by ownership, not with blanket --omit-optional. The AMI fast path (DEPS-08) bakes only the Bun binary and keys on the lockfile hash, so it saves about 1 s; retire it. Compiler-pass consolidation (DEPS-09, Astra only) is kept at the bottom because Opus measured Turbo check misses at a mean of 0.4 s. The economics attribution fix (DEPS-10) saves no minutes but is a prerequisite for any first-red or wave-order claim (Economics.ts:1154-1161 falls back to failedStepId without checking failureKind). Neither seat found a missed red or a stale green in this area, so only the top 5 are marked refute-worthy.
