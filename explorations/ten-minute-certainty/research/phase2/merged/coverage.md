# coverage: merged levers and skeptic verdicts

### Verdict (both seats reconciled)

Both seats agree that coverage cannot help the 10-minute required-context p95 today. Coverage Regression is not a required context: ruleset 10240248 lists 15 contexts and coverage is not one of them. Push to coverage verdict measured p50 29.0 min and p90 40.6 min, and that wait is set by the slowest Test Unit shard, not by the comparator. Hosted coverage stays fresh, run-bound and comparator-validated, and both seats reject result caching, producer-sealed census and depth cuts. The main local problem is trust, not speed. Final-check coverage is skipped in 101 of 159 receipts. When it runs, 26 of 58 runs fail, against 1 genuine hosted ratchet red in 43 comparator runs. The causes are Node 24 locally against hosted Node 22.23.4, missing attribution, and a confirmed judge gap. Tasks.ts:4837 calls compareCoverageRegressionBaseline without a base, so a bare `bun run coverage` falls back to baseOnlyComparison against the working-copy baseline (CoverageRegression.ts:2386-2390) with no self-judge. That is a local stale green and is merged as COVE-02.

Each seat's top-3 levers are kept. Opus's COV-L1, L5 and L8 are COVE-01, COVE-04 and COVE-02. Astra's L3, L7 and L5 are COVE-07, COVE-03 and COVE-04. Astra's L3 ranks low here because of its high trust risk and negative runner savings, not because it was dropped. Opus's COV-L3a, the comparator concurrency raise, is mostly #3007 content. The #3007 diff raises discovery (CiCoverage.ts:384) and the outer artifact collect (CoverageArtifacts.ts:942) from 2 to 4, so only the residual inner concurrency-1 sites survive, as COVE-06, with savings unbooked until a post-#3007 profile. The advisory direct-owner ratchet (COVE-01) has the only positive hosted estimate: about 1,900 check.yml runner-min a week of avoided re-runs. That figure is derived from one direct-owner red in 43 comparator runs, so treat it as a hypothesis until the parity ledger counts real avoidances. It costs about 0.5-2 local min per admitted publish. The operator still has to decide whether coverage becomes required (A), stops gating mergeable (B), or stays as it is (C). Option A today would make the required-path target unreachable. Option B is a real loss of assurance.
