# hosted-ci: merged levers and skeptic verdicts

### Verdict (both seats reconciled)

Both seats agree that the required-context p95 target is not met and that #3005/#3007 alone will not meet it. Opus measured the Test Unit context going green 11.0, 12.9 and 11.2 min after push on #3007-layout runs with no heavy queue. Astra derived the same miss from a 650 s Unit shard plus serial Change Profile, pickup and aggregate time. The two binding terms are (1) the Test Unit push-to-context envelope and (2) queueing: heavy-pool wait on 11-09 PR runs was p75 36, p90 51 and p95 84 min at cap 2, where the non-required 12-minute Lint Policy competes with required Check, Docgen and Doctest. Standard-hosted jobs also sit at the Team ceiling of 60; Opus measured 59 concurrent in the sample, and Astra treats 60 as a verified default with any override unverified. Property Laws (p95 23 min) and Lint Policy are not required contexts. Yeet still waits on them by default, so they decide push-to-mergeable whenever they run past the 20-minute review window. They are reported apart from the required target. Neither seat found a lever that cuts runner-minutes and required latency together at large scale. The two biggest compute removals are the empty-partition planner (HOST-07, about -9 runner-min per code run) and the JSDoc/Shadcn dedupe (HOST-03). The biggest latency moves are the Test Unit envelope plus the research-library-views fix (HOST-08) and getting Lint Policy off the cap-2 pool (HOST-02), and HOST-02 costs hosted minutes. Main needs a correctness repair that both seats rank independently of savings (HOST-01). check.yml:12-14 groups runs per ref, so a newer push replaces a pending one, and in-progress runs were cancelled by hand. Only 3 green and 7 red verdicts came from 58 main runs. The repair costs more compute (hosted roughly x2 per day), which is the price of the every-commit ruling. Astra's top 3 are all kept. Its C1 provisioning trim becomes HOST-10, ranked 7th because its savings are a guess (1.5-3 runner-min per run) and it does not touch the required path. Its C2 escalation narrowing becomes HOST-11, ranked 9th because it carries medium stale-green risk and overlaps the lint-laws area. Its C3 doctor overlap is merged into HOST-04. Opus's top 3 are kept: C1 (split into HOST-03 and HOST-04), C2 (HOST-05) and C3 (HOST-02). Even after HOST-02, HOST-03, HOST-06 and HOST-09, peak heavy demand stays near the cap-2 supply. So the 10-minute required p95 at peak is not guaranteed without capacity, which is the operator's money lever and is not proposed here.
