# lint-laws-knowledge: merged levers and skeptic verdicts

### Verdict (both seats reconciled)

Both seats agree on what is wrong in this area. The JSDoc inventory (152-233 s per miss) runs twice per hosted run, once in the required JSDoc Ratchet context and once in the non-required Heavy / Lint Policy. On PRs both copies miss by construction, because both jobs hold read-only remote cache (check.yml r2 read keys on non-push events). On main the two jobs race. Both seats rejected a backend mismatch as the cause. The knowledge doctor (p50 205 s, report-only today) runs as its own serial stage after medium in both full and changed scope (Tasks.ts knowledgeDoctorPolicySteps appended as a separate policyStage), with no data dependency on medium.

The seats ranked these differently mainly because they counted runner-minutes differently. Astra counted CPU, so it scored the doctor overlap (L4) at zero runner-min and ranked it last. Opus counted job-occupied runner time on the self-hosted m7i.2xlarge heavy runners. I side with Opus: a runner-minute is billed by job wall, so overlapping a 205 s serial stage frees about 3.4 heavy runner-min per Lint Policy run, about 1,100 per week. Overlapping does not save CPU, and Astra is right on that point.

This area can bring Heavy / Lint Policy p95 (919 s measured over 86 PR jobs) under 10 min with L1 + L4, plus L4b for margin, and can bring local cheap-gates toward 2 min with L1 + L9. It cannot by itself move the required-context p95. Lint Policy is not in standards/branch-protection-contexts.json. JSDoc Ratchet is required but is not the run pole, so area E's required-path gain is only L1's roughly 2.8 min off the JSDoc Ratchet lane. Every lever keeps proof depth: L2 removes an exact duplicate whose producer of record stays the required context (its lane gate is docs_only only, a superset of heavy admission), and L1 and L9 are admitted only on byte-identical parity ledgers.

No seat found a soundness hole (no missed red or stale green). The deprecation-annotation trigger, which tests head text rather than an added tag, over-escalates. That costs time but does not hide a red. refuteWorthy is therefore set on the top five only.

Top-3 handling: Astra's top three (L1, L9, L2) and Opus's top three (L4, L1, L2) are all kept. Astra-only levers L5, L6 and L7 are kept at the bottom of the ranking. Opus explicitly rejected L5 and L6. Opus's measured Turbo planning overhead (0.1-0.3 s per launch after #3014) cuts L7's savings to near zero. The Shadcn inner-copy part of Astra's L10 was dropped: the task is 6 s, and P35's replay contract makes it not worth route risk. Merged ranking: LINT-01 L1 > LINT-02 L4 > LINT-03 L4b > LINT-04 L9 > LINT-05 L2 > LINT-06 L10 > LINT-07 L3 > the rest.
