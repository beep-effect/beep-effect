# scheduler: merged levers and skeptic verdicts

### Verdict (both seats reconciled)

Both seats agree that extending QualityScheduler cannot move the hosted target. Every lever here is local only, with 0 hosted runner-minutes and 0 critical-path minutes per PR run. The scheduler is host-wide, not per checkout: one uid journal shows 200 admissions in 2.81 days (about 500 a week) and 100.3 wait-minutes, so about 250 wait-minutes a week is the ceiling on what any queue lever can recover on this host. Median wait is 0.04 s, so savings sit only in the tail (p95 178-277 s, max 644 s). Astra used per-checkout volumes (14.15 publishes and 11.82 verifies a week) and so understated the host-wide pool. Opus did not convert its estimates into weekly totals. I re-ranked on the host-wide journal figures. Both seats map their ids to the same eight mechanisms, but the numbers are permuted (Opus G-L1 is the schema, Astra G-L1 is footprint charging), so seat ids are kept per lever. Opus's schema-first G-L1 matches the first two slices of Astra's G-L4 and is kept as its own enabling lever (SCHE-08). The top savings lever is lane or package units resized inside one lease (SCHE-01). Source settles the ruling-35 dispute in Opus's favour: RunScope.enterRunScope keys the scope by ticket nonce and reuses it on retry, so resizing under the same nonce keeps one proof in one cgroup. Astra's lifecycle risks (early release with live descendants, nested waits) still need mutation fixtures. Next comes admitting final-check, package-verify and ad-hoc heavy commands (SCHE-02). It gets zero direct credit from both seats, but it is the only lever that targets OOM-kill reruns: one coverage rerun costs 20-40 min, though how often that happens is a guess. After those come footprint charging, the CPU dimension and actionable backpressure. Neither seat found a verdict soundness hole (a missed red or a stale green): the scheduler orders work and never changes what runs. So refuteWorthy covers only the top five. Retention, schema, calibration and quarantine cleanup save nothing directly but are prerequisites, so they are kept as measurement and trust levers. Each seat's top-3 levers are all kept: Astra's G-L4, G-L5 and G-L1 are SCHE-01, SCHE-04 and SCHE-03; Opus's G-L8, G-L1 and G-L3 are SCHE-09, SCHE-08 and SCHE-02. The 10-minute local verify/publish target is not reachable through admission alone: pre-push p50 is 14.5 min and final-check coverage p50 is 20 min. Those are execution-cost problems for other areas.
