# PAP2PAT: Benchmarking Outline-Guided Long-Text Patent Generation with Patent-Paper Pairs

## Problem & positioning

- Patent drafting is expensive and expertise-heavy, while patent descriptions account for 91.8% of PAP2PAT patent tokens and remain less studied than claims or abstracts.
- Confidential invention reports obstruct open research; pre-publication papers can serve as realistic public invention specifications when paired to patents on the same invention.
- Average reference descriptions are about 18,000 tokens and some exceed 180,000, beyond reliable single-call generation length.
- Existing text-similarity metrics inadequately separate technical coverage, factual support, patent style, coherence, and pathological repetition in long documents.
- The work contributes an open patent-paper benchmark, outline-controlled generation, long-document evaluation metrics, and a small patent-attorney case study.

## Approach / architecture

- PAP2PAT starts from 6.7 million USPTO applications dated 2005 through April 2024 and queries SemOpenAlex via SPARQL for candidate papers.
- Matching filters author/date candidates from 930,000 to 100,000 by term overlap, 21,000 by distinctiveness, and 1,800 by permissive license.
- Papers must be dated from one year before to two years after the patent filing; matching also uses author aliases, IDF-weighted title/abstract term overlap, and competing-match margins.
- Full texts become a nested JSON schema of section title, paragraphs, and subsections from USPTO/PubMed XML or GROBID PDF extraction.
- Font features reconstruct paper hierarchy; an LLM repairs unreliable patent heading levels; Pandoc converts MathML to LaTeX; metadata sections are filtered.
- Llama-3 70B generates short, medium, and long reference-derived outlines averaging 37, 74, and 150 bullets; real use assumes an attorney supplies the outline.
- COPGEN chunks the outline, retrieves relevant paper paragraphs with BM25, includes the abstract and headings, and generates chunks in parallel.
- Each chunk sees its current outline, prior chunk outlines for global context, and selected paper context; concatenation removes duplicate boundary headings.
- Default allocation is 2k instruction, 3k paper, and 2k output tokens per chunk; reducing output allocation to 400 increases chunk count until corpus-average length matches references.

## Ontology & KG modeling contributions

- The benchmark's core entity relation is a one-to-one `Patent Application ↔ Research Paper` pair intended to denote the same invention.
- Candidate identity evidence includes inventor/author surface-name overlap and SemOpenAlex aliases; no shared cross-corpus person identifier exists.
- Pair provenance can be reconstructed from filing/publication dates, title/abstract overlap scores, candidate-group distinctiveness, and redistribution license.
- Documents share a recursive section/paragraph/subsection structure, enabling hierarchy-aware retrieval and generation rather than flat token windows.
- Outlines are hierarchical control artifacts with section headings, bullet content targets, and original-section character budgets.
- The matching pipeline uses SemOpenAlex's graph and SPARQL for discovery, but the released task is document generation rather than an ontology or knowledge graph.
- No formal invention identity class, claim/component ontology, patent-family relation, CPC semantics, provenance ontology, axioms, or entity-resolution model is proposed.
- The useful modeling pattern is therefore an evidence-qualified candidate link plus hierarchical document spans—not promotion of a heuristic pair to unquestioned identity.

## Evaluation & results

- The final corpus has 1,813 pairs: 1,000 train, 242 validation, 500 test, plus a 71-pair 2024 non-contaminated test subset.
- Across all pairs, patents average `17.9k ± 14.7k` tokens and papers `8.1k ± 4.3k`; only 8.3% of 4-grams overlap in the main analysis.
- First-author review of 60 random pairs finds 55/60 (91.7%) where the paper's core contribution matches the invention; five have a better related paper or patent match.
- SCALE samples 10 hypothesis sentences, retrieves five premise chunks per sentence with BM25, and reports NLI support for coverage (`Gen→Ref`) and factuality (`Ref→Gen`, `Ref+Pap→Gen`).
- Single-call Mixtral and Qwen outputs are 3.2k and 2.8k tokens, only 17.7% and 15.6% of reference length.
- Default COPGEN Qwen2-72B outputs 8.1k tokens with coverage 44.1, `Ref→Gen` factuality 62.5, and `Ref+Pap→Gen` factuality 67.9.
- Length-calibrated Qwen2-72B reaches 18.1k tokens, BERTScore 71.7, ROUGE-L 50.8, coverage 46.8, and factuality 59.7/65.3.
- Qwen's detailed outlines improve coverage, factuality, and BERTScore without longer output; BM25 context selection approaches the BM25-oracle trend.
- Fine-tuned Llama-3 8B emits 27.5k tokens and has repetition rate 53.7 with 29.3% of windows above 80; repetition removal reduces it to 17.3k tokens and improves ROUGE-L by 6.8 points.
- Fine-tuning improves style by more than 20 points but lowers factuality by more than 10 points; standard similarity metrics reward the stylistic match and repetition.
- Two AI patent attorneys provide 15 evaluations over 10 samples at about 30 minutes each and predict substantial time savings in 8 cases.
- Experts identify insufficient non-limiting patent language and inadequate enabling detail as the two main obstacles.

## Limitations (authors' own + yours)

- The authors test open-weight models only and do not study automatic outline generation as part of the user-facing task.
- The expert study has two attorneys, 15 judgments, one technical field, and no inter-rater or blinded-comparison analysis.
- Benchmark outlines are generated from target patents, leaking target structure and summarized content that a real attorney must create without that reference.
- Pair validation is performed by the first author alone; 8.3% of sampled links are known imperfect matches, and surface-name matching can conflate people.
- Restricting papers to licenses permitting redistribution and commercial use changes domain and venue composition.
- Section 3.3 reports 8.3% shared 4-grams, while Appendix A.4 reports 2.1% without explaining the different value.
- SCALE is an approximate system-level metric: only 10 hypothesis sentences and top-five retrieved chunks are judged, so unsupported details elsewhere can escape evaluation.
- Reference patents and papers are not complete factual ground truth; patents can contain broad prophetic embodiments while papers omit patent-specific applications.
- The length/coverage/factuality trade-off shows that matching reference length can amplify unsupported content.
- High DiscoScore does not establish cross-section consistency, claim support, enablement, written description, novelty, or prosecution fitness.
- Only descriptions are generated; legally central claims and their dependency on description support are outside the task.
- The ethics statement rejects full automation and patent trolling, but the pipeline has no technical safeguards enforcing professional oversight.

## Beep-relevance hooks

- **patent tooling (agentic-cad-patent-tooling):** take attorney-authored hierarchical outlines plus per-section budgets as explicit drafting controls; reject generation that lacks a source-paper span map for each technical assertion.
- **patent tooling (uspto-patent-driver-depth):** reuse USPTO hierarchy repair, metadata filtering, formula conversion, and recursive section JSON for reliable description ingestion before any generation.
- **epistemic slice:** represent patent-paper identity as a scored claim with author/date/term/license evidence and adjudication state; do not treat the heuristic one-to-one link as certain.
- **OIP solo-practice workflows:** surface the coverage-versus-factuality trade-off and require attorney review for non-limiting language, enablement detail, and unsupported embodiments at section granularity.
- **semantic-foundation (SKOS/@beep/ontology):** model document section types, outline nodes, evidence spans, patent families, people, and invention concepts explicitly; the paper's nested JSON is useful structure but not an ontology.
- **agent memory (Cognee):** retain outline, retrieval query, selected paragraphs, generated chunk, model/configuration, and review outcome as derivation provenance so later revisions remain auditable.

## Extraction confidence

- High. `pdftotext -layout` recovered all 31 pages, main tables, equations, appendices, and printed ACL page numbers.
- Two-column reading order occasionally interleaves adjacent sections, but headings and table rows remain recoverable from layout.
- Plot coordinates and some formula typography were noisy; no values were inferred from plots when prose or tables did not provide them.
