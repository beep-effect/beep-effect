## What this repo actually is

`llm4oe-slr` is the reproducibility bundle for a systematic literature review of LLM-supported ontology engineering, not an ontology, knowledge graph, LLM implementation, patent corpus, or executable review pipeline.
The inspected snapshot is commit `e13b49e267f221c2f48ba7c44e8e0bc6c28490fc`. Its load-bearing artifacts are Excel evidence tables: one denormalized extraction table, four RQ-specific projections, a four-column experiment-resource registry, and screening workbooks; the remaining source is draw.io/Plotly figure material.

`Data Extraction/Full_Data_Extraction_from_36_papers.xlsx` has 49 nonempty study/activity rows, 36 unique opaque paper IDs, and 27 populated columns `A:AA` spanning paper metadata and RQ1–RQ4 evidence. Every `(paper ID, harmonized OE activity)` pair is unique, so its practical grain is **paper × activity**, even though the README calls the rows “studies”; there is no distinct study/experiment identifier for multiple variants of the same task.
The years represented are 2023 (6 rows / 6 papers), 2024 (35 / 24), and 2025 (8 / 6).

The workbook's physical layout exposes a version seam: rows 2–42 contain the older 41-row/30-paper corpus, rows 43–999 are hidden and blank, and the eight 2025 activity rows are stored at rows 1000–1007.
`Data Extraction/Data_Extraction_for_RQ2.xlsx` is an exact column projection of only rows 2–42: 41 activities from 30 papers. It omits all eight later activities and all six later papers. RQ1, RQ3, and RQ4 are likewise exact projections of that same 41-row prefix, not projections of the advertised full table.

The source's own counts preserve two generations of review state. `Paper Selection/Search Results.xlsx` contains 11,985 raw records, 5,275 deduplicated records, 204 title-screened records, and an older 30-paper/41-activity extraction.
`Paper Selection/Search Results-2025.xlsx` adds 3,703 raw records, 1,904 deduplicated records, 67 title-screened records, eight peer-reviewed candidates, and six represented paper IDs expanded to eight activity rows.
Those rows support the current 15,688 raw → 7,179 deduplicated → 271 title-screened → 36 included papers → 49 activity rows story, but `Figures/LLM4OE_SLR_search_pipeline.html` also retains diagrams ending at 30 and 34 papers.
The README's opening sentence still says 34 papers/46 tasks while its repository-structure section says 36/49.

## Extractable gold

### 1. A concrete ontology-engineering task hierarchy with an auditable harmonization map

The raw activity (`H`) and harmonized activity (`I`) columns in the full workbook yield this actual hierarchy:

| Phase | Harmonized activity | Rows | Raw labels absorbed |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Requirements specification | Functional requirements writing | 9 | `Functional requirements writing` (2), `CQ reverse engineering` (5), `CQs writing` (2) |
| Requirements specification | Requirement formalization | 3 | `Requirement formalization` (3) |
| Ontology development | Conceptualization | 11 | `Conceptualization` (11) |
| Ontology development | Encoding | 11 | `Encoding` (11) |
| Ontology development | Ontology matching | 5 | `Ontology matching` (5) |
| Ontology development | Ontology evaluation | 4 | `Ontology evaluation` (4) |
| Ontology publication | Ontology documentation | 5 | `Ontology documentation` (5) |
| Ontology maintenance | Bug issues | 1 | `Bug issues` (1) |

The phase totals are therefore requirements 12, development 31, publication 5, and maintenance 1. The
`Figures/Distribution_LLM_supported_tasks.py` script independently encodes `total = 49` and the same four phase totals;
`Figures/Taxonomic framework of LLM-supported ontology engineering tasks.xml` expands the leaves with per-study model,
prompt, role, input, output, experiment, dataset, and human-participation labels.
That draw.io XML is a presentation graph, not OWL/SKOS, but it is a useful source for a typed concept scheme and mappings.

The portable representation is not the delimiter-bearing string `"phase--activity"`. Use stable `OePhase` and `OeActivity`
concepts plus an evidence-bearing mapping from each source label to its harmonized
concept; in particular, preserve that “CQ reverse engineering” was collapsed into functional-requirements writing.

### 2. A systematic-review evidence graph hiding inside the 27-column table

`Full_Data_Extraction_from_36_papers.xlsx` supplies a compact relational shape:

```text
Paper -> ActivityAssertion(raw label, harmonized activity, author definition)
      -> LlmIntervention(role, model, prompt technique, input artifact, output artifact)
      -> HumanLoop(present, role, task)
      -> Experiment(link, dataset, dataset type)
      -> Evaluation(description, method type, metrics, human evaluator)
      -> ApplicationDomain
```

This decomposition is concrete rather than aspirational: the exact RQ2 columns are LLM role/model/prompt/input/output
followed by human-in-loop flag/role/task, while RQ3 adds experiment state/link, dataset/link/type, method description/type,
metrics, and human-evaluator state (`Data Extraction/Full_Data_Extraction_from_36_papers.xlsx:A1:AA1`).
It is an excellent schema seed for evidence import because it separates what the model consumes and emits from who reviews it.

The row-level artifact vocabulary is broad but recurrent. Inputs include natural-language text, competency questions,
ontologies/axioms, triples, concepts/terms/relationships,
knowledge graphs, JSON, schemas, and retrieved chunks.
Outputs include CQs, ontologies/RDF/axioms, concepts and relations, mappings, SPARQL queries, documentation/definitions,
binary classifications, JSON/YAML/schema, and question-answer JSONL.
Port these as source-scoped artifact-type concepts; do not preserve misspellings such as `Tripes`, `Omtology`, or
`Nature language text` as canonical labels.

The prompt cells provide a reusable multi-valued technique vocabulary. A case/punctuation-normalized scan of all 49 rows
finds zero-shot in 29, few-shot in 12, one-shot in 2, role-based in 14,
template-based in 15, iterative refinement in 14, CoT in 6, GoT in 1, fine-tuning in 6, and decomposition in 1.
These counts overlap because one intervention may combine techniques.
The cells also show why a normalized join table is needed: `Role-based`/`Role based`, truncated `Iterative refinemen`,
model bundles in a single cell, and `N/A`/blank/not-mentioned are all distinct raw values.

Human participation is already two different relations and should stay that way. The design-time human-loop field has 42
`no`, six `yes`, and one step-qualified `Step 8: yes`; the evaluation-human field
has 35 `no`, 13 yes/yes-with-description rows, and one mixed `no / yes / no` multi-task row.
Model `HumanLoopStage`, `HumanRole`, and `HumanAction` separately rather than coercing those cells to one boolean.

### 3. An evaluation ontology seed grounded in reported practice

The experiment-state field is a useful four-state warning against boolean collapse: 35 rows say `yes`, seven `no`, four
`yes, but it is a test`, and three `NA` after case normalization.
That should become `reported experiment`, `test/demo only`, `reported absent`, and `unknown`, with the source cell retained.

The metric cells expose several evaluation families worth taking:

- lexical/semantic similarity: cosine similarity, Sentence-BERT thresholds, BERTScore, and BLEU (entered once as `blue`);
- classification/set agreement: accuracy, precision, recall, F1/F-score, relaxed precision, majority vote, and ICC;
- ranking/matching: MAP@k, Hits, MRR/RR, and `InrAny@K`/`InrAll@K` as source-authored labels needing definition;
- structural/logical: Tree Edit Distance, logical consistency, hierarchy formation, HermiT-based checks, OOPS, and OQuaRE;
- ontology quality/effort: completeness, conciseness, concept/relation/property counts, time, size, and effort saving;
- human judgment: expert review, curator/editor scoring, user satisfaction, feedback, and domain-expert correlation.

The strongest portable lesson is the separation the workbook lacks internally. Several evaluation-description cells mix
protocol, metric, benchmark, observed score, and conclusion, while metric cells
sometimes contain prose or assessor details.
A Beep port should model `EvaluationProtocol`, `GroundTruth`, `MetricDefinition`, `MetricObservation`, `Evaluator`, and
`ReportedConclusion` as different evidence nodes, never treat a named metric as proof that it was correctly computed.

### 4. A 41-entry benchmark-resource registry that needs typing and provenance

`Experiment Datasets/full_experiment_datasets_summary.xlsx` contains 41 unique rows with exactly four fields:

```text
Acronym Name | Full Name | URL | Domain
```

Its inventory includes African Wildlife, CaSk, CL, CSO, DemCare, DOREMUS, EMAPA, ENVO, FIBO, FOODON, GO, HP, MONDO,
MP, MusicMeta, NORIA-O, OAIE, OBA, OBI, Odeuropa, OntoDM, OpenXOntology, OPC-UA, Polifonia, SAREF, UBERON, UFO, UMLS,
VICINITY, WDV, Pizza, NCIT, DOID, SNOMED CT, FMA, MEDCIN, SNOMEDCT US, Schema.org, Video Game Ontology,
MM-S14-Disease/CPP, and Wine Ontology.

The 26 exact domain labels are AI, Anatomy, Arts, Attributes, Benchmark, Biology, Business, Computer Science,
Cultural Heritage, Data Science, Disease, Ecology, Entertainment, Environment, Food, Foundational, Healthcare,
Industrial, IoT, Medicine, Methodology, Music, Oncology, Phenotype, Robotics, and Web.
Medicine has five entries, Anatomy four, Food three; Business, Disease, IoT, Music, Phenotype, and Web have two each.

This is valuable seed data but not yet a dataset catalog. The rows conflate ontologies, terminology systems, vocabulary
sites, benchmark initiatives, and one explicitly named dataset;
there is no resource kind, release/version, format, digest, license, access condition, provenance, or study foreign key.
The full workbook instead embeds dataset names and URLs in multiline free text, so study-to-resource joins are not lossless.
The target schema should split `BenchmarkResource` from `StudyResourceUse` and record use role (input, gold standard,
prompt context, matching source/target, or evaluation corpus).

Two direct integrity fixtures are worth retaining: the registry spells the alignment initiative acronym `OAIE` while its URL
uses `oaei`, and its Pizza URL contains `pizza-/pizza.owl` while the full extraction uses `pizza/pizza.owl`.
All 41 URLs are unique, but four are plain HTTP and none was content-addressed or availability-checked by this repository.

### 5. Workbook lineage itself is a reusable ingestion test case

The review artifacts demonstrate append/projection drift that a schema-first importer should detect automatically:

```text
old extraction: 30 papers / 41 activity rows
2025 addition:   6 papers /  8 activity rows
full workbook:  36 papers / 49 activity rows
RQ1–RQ4 files:  30 papers / 41 activity rows each
```

Require a declared workbook release, stable paper and study IDs, contiguous logical rows, uniqueness constraints, projection
cardinality checks, controlled missingness, and source-cell provenance.
Treat README/figure/workbook counts as separate claims; do not silently select the most convenient number when they disagree.

## Port discipline notes

The root `LICENSE` declares CC BY 4.0: the repository material may be shared and adapted, including commercially, provided
appropriate credit is given, the license is linked, and modifications are indicated.
Porting the task taxonomy, table schemas, curated registry rows, figure-derived labels, or transformation code is therefore
allowed with attribution to `llm4oe-slr`, its source/DOI, and CC BY 4.0, plus a clear change notice.

Do **not** infer that the 41 externally linked ontologies, terminology systems, datasets, papers, model assets, or website
contents are CC-BY-4.0 merely because their names and URLs occur in a CC-licensed spreadsheet.
Their upstream licenses, access terms, attribution requirements, versions, and redistribution rights must be checked separately;
none of those remote payloads should be vendored from this registry alone.
Likewise, bulk free-text cells that reproduce paper descriptions/results deserve underlying-paper citation and rights review;
prefer the normalized facts and short attributed evidence snippets over copying entire prose fields.

## Beep-relevance hooks

- **semantic-foundation SKOS / `@beep/ontology`:** seed source-scoped schemes for OE phase/activity, prompt technique,
  artifact type, evaluation family, and application domain; represent raw→harmonized task decisions as reviewable mappings.
- **epistemic slice:** ingest each workbook cell and each README/figure count as a provenance-bearing claim so 30/34/36 papers,
  41/46/49 activities, `test only`, absent, and unknown remain distinguishable instead of being overwritten.
- **patent tooling:** reuse the paper→task→intervention→evaluation graph to benchmark LLM-assisted claim/CPC ontology work;
  add patent-specific inputs, gold standards, office/jurisdiction, temporal split, and expert-review evidence rather than
  pretending this repository contributes patent data.
- **gov-legal data drivers:** turn the four-column registry into typed, versioned `BenchmarkResource` drivers with license,
  digest, format, retrieval policy, and `StudyResourceUse` joins before any external ontology or legal vocabulary is acquired.
- **OIP practice:** make evaluation reports preserve protocol, dataset release, prompt/model identity, metric definition,
  observation, human adjudication, and limitations; the workbook's mixed prose/metric cells are an adversarial import fixture.

## Extraction confidence

**High for local structure, counts, exact labels, projection drift, hierarchy totals, and registry contents.** All three
requested `.xlsx` targets were readable and passed ZIP integrity checks; their OOXML worksheets, shared strings,
styles, hidden rows, hyperlinks, formulas, and relationships were inspected directly with Python's standard library.
The targets contain no formulas, and the stated row/ID/category counts were recomputed rather than copied from the README.
RQ1/RQ3/RQ4, both screening workbooks, the Plotly script, draw.io XML/HTML, README, and license were cross-checked.

**Medium for the authors' intended study semantics and evaluation taxonomy.** The extraction cells are source-authored free
text with spelling drift, mixed granularity, and no codebook or study key;
the underlying 36 papers were not bundled and their claims were not independently re-evaluated.

**Low for remote resource availability, licensing, benchmark quality, and reported model performance.** No linked ontology,
dataset, repository, or paper was fetched or executed, and the local registry supplies no version/digest/license.
There is no local patent ontology, legal corpus, SPARQL/SHACL artifact, model output, or machine-verifiable result to mine.
