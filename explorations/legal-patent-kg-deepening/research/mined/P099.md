# Zep: A Temporal Knowledge Graph Architecture for Agent Memory

## Problem & positioning

- Static-document RAG does not represent continuously changing conversations, business records, or relationships needed by long-lived agents.
- Full conversation histories are expensive and eventually exceed effective context use even when they fit nominal model windows.
- Zep is presented as a production memory-layer service whose core, Graphiti, incrementally builds a temporally aware KG from messages, text, and JSON.
- Its stated goals are non-lossy source retention, evolving current and historical facts, multi-mode retrieval, low response latency, and scalable memory APIs.
- The paper positions Zep against MemGPT on Deep Memory Retrieval and against full-context baselines on LongMemEval.
- It is a vendor-authored first-version arXiv paper; results assess the authors' hosted production system and should be read with that conflict and reproducibility boundary in view.

## Approach / architecture

- Graph construction ingests an episode plus its reference timestamp and, for messages, the previous four messages as extraction context.
- Entity extraction always includes the speaker, applies a reflection pass for coverage, and creates a name, summary, and 1,024-dimensional name embedding.
- Candidate entity resolution combines cosine vector search with full-text search over existing names and summaries, then asks an LLM to decide duplication and update the canonical name/summary.
- Facts are extracted only between identified entities, assigned free-form all-caps relation types, embedded, and deduplicated against edges between the same entity pair.
- Graph writes use predefined Cypher rather than LLM-generated queries to constrain schema shape and reduce query hallucination.
- Temporal extraction resolves absolute and relative dates against the episode reference time; later contradictory edges may close earlier facts' valid-time intervals.
- Community detection uses label propagation; a new node joins the plurality community of its neighbors and triggers summary updates, with periodic full refreshes required.
- Retrieval composes `search → rerank → context construction` over semantic edges, entity nodes, and community nodes.
- Candidate generation combines cosine similarity, BM25 full-text search, and graph breadth-first search; recent episodes can seed local traversal.
- Reranking options include reciprocal-rank fusion, maximal marginal relevance, mention frequency, graph distance from a centroid, and an expensive LLM cross-encoder.
- The context constructor emits fact text with valid ranges, entity names/summaries, and community summaries for response generation.

## Ontology & KG modeling contributions

- The graph `G = (N, E, φ)` has three tiers: episode subgraph `Ge`, semantic entity subgraph `Gs`, and community subgraph `Gc`.
- Episode nodes preserve raw message, text, or JSON input; episode-to-entity edges connect evidence to extracted semantic entities.
- Semantic entity nodes hold resolved entities; semantic edges hold relationship facts, predicate text, embeddings, and temporal metadata.
- Community nodes cluster strongly connected entities and carry map-reduce-style summaries; community-to-entity edges record membership.
- Episodes and semantic edges maintain bidirectional indices, enabling a fact to trace back to sources and an episode to enumerate derived entities and facts.
- Repeated binary facts across different entity pairs approximate a multi-entity fact or hyperedge, though the paper does not define a first-class event node.
- The bitemporal model separates world/valid time `T` from ingestion/transaction time `T′`.
- Four edge timestamps are stored: transaction-created, transaction-expired, valid-at, and invalid-at.
- A newly detected, temporally overlapping contradiction sets the old edge's invalid-at to the new edge's valid-at; transaction order prioritizes newer input.
- The extraction prompt explicitly forbids date nodes and instead attaches temporal meaning to fact edges.
- This is schema-light KG construction: relation labels are open-ended strings, and the authors identify domain ontologies as future work.
- The architecture's strongest transferable pattern is the separation of immutable-ish episodes from revisable semantic projections, with traceable derivation between them.

## Evaluation & results

- DMR contains 500 conversations, each with 5 sessions and up to 12 messages per session, plus one memory question/answer pair.
- With GPT-4 Turbo, recursive summarization scores 35.3%, conversation summaries 78.6%, MemGPT 93.4%, full conversation 94.4%, and Zep 94.8%.
- With GPT-4o-mini, conversation summaries score 88.0%, full conversation 98.0%, and Zep 98.2%.
- The authors correctly qualify DMR as weak: at most 60 messages fit easily in current context windows, questions are single-fact retrieval, and some wording is ambiguous.
- LongMemEvals conversations average about 115,000 tokens and span six question types, including multi-session, knowledge-update, preference, and temporal reasoning.
- Zep retrieves an average context of 1,600 tokens versus 115,000 for the full-context baseline.
- With GPT-4o-mini, full context scores 55.4% at 31.3 seconds; Zep scores 63.8% at 3.20 seconds.
- With GPT-4o, full context scores 60.2% at 28.9 seconds; Zep scores 71.2% at 2.58 seconds.
- The paper reports relative accuracy improvements of 15.2% and 18.5%, plus approximately 90% latency reduction.
- GPT-4o temporal reasoning improves from 45.1% to 62.4%, multi-session from 44.3% to 57.9%, and knowledge-update from 78.2% to 83.3%.
- Counter-results matter: single-session-assistant falls from 94.6% to 80.4% with GPT-4o and from 81.8% to 75.0% with GPT-4o-mini.
- GPT-4o judges answers; graph construction uses GPT-4o-mini, response generation uses GPT-4o or GPT-4o-mini, and BGE-m3 handles embeddings/reranking.

## Limitations (authors' own + yours)

- The authors identify weak memory benchmarks, no benchmark for blending conversation with structured business data, and insufficient production cost/scalability evaluation.
- They could not obtain successful MemGPT LongMemEval responses through an archival-history workaround, so no direct LongMemEval competitor result is available.
- Experiments exercise only a subset of Zep search functionality and do not evaluate the claimed episode-to-fact citation traversal directly.
- The paper promises public notebooks but the PDF alone does not provide independent reproduction artifacts, raw run counts, uncertainty intervals, ingestion cost, or KG construction accuracy.
- GPT-4o-based judging and vendor-selected configurations can introduce correlated evaluator and reporting bias.
- Baseline timing excludes the network path imposed on Zep, yet graph ingestion time and LLM extraction cost are also absent; end-to-end economics remain unknown.
- New-information-wins invalidation is not epistemically safe: a later assertion can be false, lower-authority, or merely scope-misaligned with an earlier fact.
- Closing an old valid-time interval at a new edge's valid-at conflates contradiction detection with adjudication and can manufacture chronology when dates are uncertain.
- Free-form relation types lack domain/range, identity, cardinality, negation, authority, confidence, and source-span constraints.
- Entity resolution can merge distinct people or organizations; edge deduplication limited to an entity pair cannot resolve equivalent event structures across aliases or n-ary contexts.
- Incremental plurality community assignment drifts from a full label-propagation solution, and generated summaries add another lossy, model-dependent layer.
- The paper reports retrieval-answer quality, not temporal extraction accuracy, contradiction precision/recall, provenance completeness, or deletion/privacy behavior.

## Beep-relevance hooks

- `agent memory (Cognee)`: take the three-tier episode/entity/community separation and hybrid retrieval composition; preserve raw episodes as the audit base and treat all summaries and edges as regenerable projections.
- `epistemic-bitemporal-edge-core`: adopt distinct valid and transaction intervals, but reject automatic new-wins invalidation; record competing assertions additively with authority, confidence, and adjudication state.
- `epistemic-contradiction-triage`: use temporal overlap only to generate a contradiction candidate, then align subject, predicate, object, scope, and evidence before supersession.
- `epistemic slice / evidence spans`: deepen episode-level back-links to exact character spans and extraction-run provenance so every answer fact can quote and audit its source.
- `semantic-foundation (SKOS/@beep/ontology)`: constrain entity and relation types through registered concepts and SHACL/domain rules; reject open-ended all-caps predicates as canonical ontology terms.
- `OIP solo-practice workflows`: retrieve current and historical client/matter facts through bitemporal filters, while requiring human resolution for conflicting dates, parties, instructions, and legal positions.
