# Modeling the Diachronic Evolution of Legal Norms: An LRMoo-Based, Component-Level, Event-Centric Approach to Legal Knowledge Graphs

## Problem & positioning

- Reliable legal AI needs a formal history of each norm and provision from which the exact text applicable at a date can be reconstructed.
- Akoma Ntoso can annotate active and passive textual modifications, but its amendment records remain document-embedded rather than globally identified causal nodes.
- LexML can address a versioned provision and records events in document metadata, yet active and passive records require heuristic correlation rather than shared event identity.
- ELI supplies temporal/language URI components and metadata, but remains document-centric and usually expresses amendment as a binary law-to-law relation.
- The paper is a materially revised version of P054: it moves Temporal Versions from `F2 Expression` to distinct `F1 Work` instances and makes legal time and termination events explicit.

## Approach / architecture

- A single immutable Concept (`F1 Work`) anchors the continuing identity of the norm.
- Each Temporal Version (TV) is a distinct `F1 Work` because an amendment changes legal force and creates a new normative entity.
- TVs share the Concept through `R10 is member of` and form a succession chain through `R2 is derivative of`, qualified by `R2.1 has type` as Temporal Succession.
- Each Language Version (LV) is an `F2 Expression` that `R3i realises` its TV.
- As an `E33 Linguistic Object`, an LV carries `P72 has language` and `P190 has symbolic content`.
- A wording amendment is a single event jointly typed `F27 Work Creation` and `E64 End of Existence`.
- The event uses the authoritative amending provision with `P16`, terminates the prior TV with `P93`, and creates the successor with `R16`.
- Pure insertion uses only `F27`; pure repeal only `E64`; wording replacement combines both types.
- `P2`, `P14`, and `P4` capture event nature, actor, and publication/recording time.
- Version validity `[tstart, tend)` is event-sourced from its creation and termination rather than stored as an unexplained snapshot attribute.

## Ontology & KG modeling contributions

- Transaction time is independent of valid time: `P4 has time-span` records publication/recording, while a model extension carries legal validity.
- Valid time is further decomposed into entry into the legal order and applicability to governed facts, covering vacatio legis, retroactivity, tax anteriority, and lex mitior.
- A Component Concept (CC) is an `F1 Work`; component concepts nest through `R67 has part`.
- Each Component Temporal Version (CTV) is an `F1 Work`, belongs to its CC via `R10`, and succeeds its predecessor via `R2`.
- Each Component Language Version (CLV) is an `F2 Expression` realizing a CTV through `R3i`; translations use `R76 is derivative of`.
- Concrete expression trees use `R5 has component`, keeping conceptual membership, Work composition, realization, and textual incorporation distinct.
- `P9 consists of` decomposes the macro legislative event into concurrent provision-level micro-events.
- Each micro-event has a dogmatic operation type: insertion, repeal, or wording amendment, with operation-specific creation and termination consequences.
- The event is n-ary rather than a bare `amends` edge: it binds authoritative instruction, target predecessor, successor, actor, time, and legal effect.
- Globally reifying one event allows the amending and amended norms to reference the same causal entity rather than duplicate document-scoped records.

## Evaluation & results

- The Brazilian Constitution case traces the whole-norm change caused by Constitutional Amendment 1/1992 and the Article 6 caput change caused by Amendment 26/2000.
- The examples show stable Concept/CC URNs, dated TV/CTV URNs, language-qualified LV/CLV URNs, predecessor links, and event inputs/outputs.
- The paper supplies no implemented OWL ontology, populated LKG, reasoner validation, corpus statistics, reconstruction benchmark, accuracy result, latency result, or practitioner evaluation.
- Its claimed exact reconstruction and auditable history are architectural properties awaiting the authors' proposed implementation and benchmarks.

## Limitations (authors' own + yours)

- The authors leave OWL publication, large-scale graph population, historical legislation parsing, point-in-time tooling, and deterministic benchmarks to future work.
- They explicitly acknowledge that reliably extracting each micro-event from unstructured legislative text is a significant practical challenge.
- The valid-time properties are extensions beyond native LRMoo, but their vocabulary, cardinalities, and axioms are not formally specified here.
- Joint `F27`/`E64` typing is plausible for state transition, yet no reasoner proof demonstrates consistency with all imported LRMoo/CIDOC constraints.
- Treating every changed temporal state as a new Work improves legal-force identity but raises difficult identity questions for correction, consolidation, unofficial text, split/merge, and annulment.
- Transaction time is described as publication/recording time, although ingestion into a particular KG may occur later; system time needs its own immutable interval.
- A single deterministic state may hide concurrent official versions, source disagreement, contested applicability, judicial reinterpretation, or delayed discovery.
- No source-span or document-fragment evidence model connects graph events to exact authoritative language.
- The paper presents no migration rule from the earlier P054 `F2`-TV model, so adopters must explicitly choose rather than mix their incompatible class assignments and properties.

## Beep-relevance hooks

- **epistemic slice:** take the explicit separation of transaction, validity, entry-into-force, and applicability times; add recorded-at system time so late corrections do not rewrite prior knowledge states.
- **epistemic slice:** model insert/repeal/replace as typed, evidence-backed claim events with predecessor and successor edges; use contradictions for competing applicability or source assertions instead of forcing one state.
- **semantic-foundation (SKOS/@beep/ontology):** take the four-level distinction among conceptual membership, Work partonomy, Work realization, and Expression composition; enforce it with schemas and relation-domain tests.
- **OIP solo-practice workflows:** derive an “as-of facts date” legal text separately from “known as-of research date,” crucial for retroactivity, vacatio legis, and corrected official records.
- **agent memory (Cognee):** adopt event-sourced version lifecycles and immutable successor nodes; reject last-write-wins mutation of statutes or provision memories.
- **patent tooling (uspto-patent-driver-depth):** reuse the n-ary event pattern for prosecution actions—source office action/response, affected claim version, resulting claim version, legal effect, mailed date, and recorded date.
