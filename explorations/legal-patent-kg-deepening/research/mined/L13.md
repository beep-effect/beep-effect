# FOPNet: A Comprehensive Functional Semantic Knowledge Graph for Deep Technical Analysis in Patents

## Problem & positioning

Keyword, citation, and classification methods miss contextual and functional patent semantics.
Earlier FOP work is constrained by scarce STS datasets, limited evaluation, and pipelines in which ontology design, extraction, completion, and correction remain disconnected.
FOPNet aims to unify those stages while retaining interpretable patent representations.

## Approach

The pipeline defines a TRIZ-derived functional ontology, preprocesses patent text, extracts entities and relations, normalizes and links them, completes and corrects the graph, and learns representations.
It combines syntactic trigger rules, fine-tuned NER, supervised relation classification, ontology normalization, instance extension, KGE completion, pretrained patent embeddings, K-means-derived weighting, similarity thresholds, and learned reranking.
Outputs are normalized Function–Object–Property triples in reusable CSV/JSON forms; optional LLM interpretation or reranking remains downstream.
Two USPTO-derived STS benchmarks use examination decisions and PTAB appeals: a binary similarity set and ranked retrieval set.

## Ontology & KG modeling contributions

Core types include `Component`, `Function`, `Object`, and `Property`.
The ontology explicitly models hierarchy and synonymy, separating intended technical purpose, target artifact/entity, and attributes of functions or objects.
Compared with SAO, the claimed gain is finer granularity and explicit properties; compared with generic PatentKG work, the focus is function-level semantics and instance extension.
The capture says the functional ontology is released, but does not expose its URI, complete relation inventory, axioms, license, or artifact link.
Knowledge completion proposes missing links; quality correction and validation must therefore remain distinguishable from source-extracted facts.

## Evaluation & results

The abstract reports 43% better retrieval accuracy than keyword baselines and 26% over standard document embeddings.
K-means-weighted vector similarity improves 32% over unweighted baselines; a knowledge-similarity threshold of 0.4–0.6 best separates similar from dissimilar patents.
The best ablated configuration combines pretrained-patent FOP embeddings, clustering weights, thresholds, and semantic extensions.
It reduces mean relevant-patent rank from 5.7 to 2.7 and places a relevant result in the top three for all reported test cases.
The visible entity-recognition snippet reports three weighted aggregate scores of 0.85, 0.89, and 0.87, but the captured sentence does not safely map each value to a metric.

## Limitations

The full text, tables, benchmark sizes/year ranges, ontology artifact, and supplementary material are paywalled in this capture.
The abstract's percentage improvements cannot be tied to exact metric denominators or confidence intervals here.
USPTO examination and PTAB-derived relevance labels may not transfer to infringement, validity, or cross-jurisdiction retrieval.
KGE completion, semantic extension, and LLM interpretation can introduce unsupported edges unless provenance and review state are explicit.

- Preserve the two benchmark task types and report Recall@K, MRR, and NDCG separately.
- Treat similarity thresholds and reranker versions as experiment configuration, not ontology truth.
- Link every functional assertion back to patent section and text span before using it in CAD or legal workflows.
