# FIBO Legal Core Ontology

## Problem & positioning

This FIBO module supplies high-level legal concepts for reuse by other FIBO ontologies.
It deliberately abstracts over public and private legal instruments: `law` can ground statutes and company by-laws, while `constitution` can ground state constitutions and constitutive corporate instruments.

## Approach

The captured ontology listing exposes a stable ontology IRI, a versioned IRI, the QName `fibo-fnd-law-cor:`, release maturity, and RDF/XML download support.
Its compact vocabulary is intended to be imported rather than to model an entire legal domain alone.

## Ontology & KG modeling contributions

The four internal classes are `Constitution`, `CourtOfLaw`, `Law`, and `StatuteLaw`.
The two internal object properties are `hasInForce` and its inverse-facing relation `isInForceIn`.
Imported concepts include `Collection`, `FormalOrganization`, and `Jurisdiction`; the imported `governs` property connects this module to regulatory-agency semantics.
The listing therefore offers a small legal-instrument and institutional spine, with explicit reuse of OMG Commons concepts.

## Limitations

The capture is a vocabulary listing, not the expanded class/property definitions or axioms.
It does not expose duties, permissions, legal relations, claims, events, provenance, or temporal amendment semantics beyond the two in-force relations.
The page alone cannot establish the complete import closure or reasoning behavior.

## Beep-relevance hooks

- Reuse the instrument hierarchy and jurisdiction link as an external-alignment layer, not as Beep's complete legal domain model.
- Preserve ontology IRI, versioned IRI, and release maturity as source/provenance metadata.
- Map `hasInForce` assertions to time-qualified claims rather than timeless booleans when integrating legal events.
- Keep imported FIBO/OMG terms distinct from locally owned canonical entities.
