# UFO: Unified Foundational Ontology

## Problem & positioning

UFO is presented as a foundational ontology for conceptual modeling, assembled over roughly two decades from formal ontology, cognitive science, linguistics, and philosophical logic.
Its target is a “Calculus of Content” for exposing the ontological commitments of representation artifacts and supporting domain understanding, problem solving, and meaning negotiation.
The paper positions UFO as descriptive and cognitively informed but realist: multiple worldviews can be viable, while consistency within a worldview and safe interoperability between worldviews remain engineering goals.
It responds to deficiencies the authors found in Bunge–Wand–Weber, GFO, and DOLCE for conceptual modeling, especially weak treatment of reified relationships, types, roles, attributes, and particularized relational properties.
UFO is paired with OntoUML, whose stereotypes operationalize UFO distinctions for concrete conceptual models.
This paper is a broad state-of-the-ontology account, formalization, and worked-example collection rather than a legal or patent ontology.

## Approach / architecture

UFO is a four-category ontology: it recognizes substantial individuals, substantial types, particularized properties or moments, and their types.
Its three fragments are UFO-A for endurants, UFO-B for perdurants, and UFO-C for social and intentional entities built on the first two.
The paper concentrates on UFO-A and selected UFO-B categories, using first-order modal logic QS5 with Barcan and converse Barcan formulas and a fixed possibilist domain.
Types and individuals share the first-order domain and are connected by an instantiation relation; types may themselves instantiate higher-order types.
The axiomatization is also represented in TPTP and was submitted to multiple automated provers for consistency and satisfiability checks.
Worked OntoUML cases cover composition versus constitution, role dependence, qualitative property change, event/process change, and anticipated concept evolution.
Model simulation is used to exhibit possible populations satisfying the logical theory, including evolving marriage concepts at multiple classification levels.
An OntoUML-as-a-Service infrastructure is described separately: JSON Schema serialization, a TypeScript model library, HTTP model-intelligence services, and tool integration.

## Ontology & KG modeling contributions

The top concrete split is `Endurant` versus `Perdurant`: an endurant is wholly present through time and may change while retaining identity; a perdurant unfolds through temporal parts and is modally fragile.
Every endurant necessarily instantiates exactly one `Kind`, which supplies its principle of individuation, identity, and persistence.
`Sortal` covers a kind or a specialization of one kind; rigid specializations are `SubKind`, intrinsically contingent anti-rigid sortals are `Phase`, and relationally contingent ones are `Role`.
`NonSortal` types span multiple kinds and divide into rigid `Category`, anti-rigid `PhaseMixin` and `RoleMixin`, and semi-rigid `Mixin`.
Endurants partition into independent `Substantial` and existentially dependent `Moment`; substantials further partition into `Object`, `Collective`, and `Quantity`.
Moments partition into `Relator` and `IntrinsicMoment`; intrinsic moments partition into `Mode` and `Quality`.
`inheresIn(moment, bearer)` implies existential dependence and a unique bearer; recursive `momentOf` supports moments of moments and a unique ultimate non-moment bearer.
An externally dependent mode inheres in one bearer while depending on another disjoint entity; commitments and vulnerabilities are representative modes.
A `QuaIndividual` sums externally dependent modes sharing bearer, external dependence source, and founding event, such as one spouse qua spouse.
A `Relator` is a sum of mutually dependent qua individuals with a common foundation; marriages, contracts, enrollments, employments, and mandates are examples.
`mediates(relator, endurant)` links a relator to each bearer of its component qua individuals, and every relator mediates at least two distinct endurants.
Externally dependent modes and relators are proposed as truth-makers for one-sided and multi-sided material relations, respectively.
Mereology includes part, proper-part, overlap, strong supplementation, composition-specific functional dependence, and differentiated unity conditions.
`constitutedBy` connects distinct entities of different kinds without collapsing identity; constitution is asymmetric and can change for endurants.
`characterization(type, momentType)` requires instances of the moment type to inhere in instances of the characterized type; qualities additionally map uniquely to qualia in quality structures.
Quality structures divide into dimensions and multidimensional domains, with value membership, subtype-related subspaces, distance, and metric constraints.
`foundedBy` connects externally dependent modes and relators to a unique founding perdurant; `manifests` connects perdurants to endurants.
The `lifeOf` an endurant is the mereological sum of events manifesting it; adding a manifestation changes that life aggregate, not an already-complete event.
Higher-order `categorizes(type, baseType)` supports anticipated concept evolution: changing first-order marriage types specialize a stable `ConjugalRelationship` relator kind.
For legal KGs, the crucial pattern is to reify a contract or legal relation as a relator with role-bearing participants, internal claims/commitments, founding events, and temporally varying qualities.

## Evaluation & results

The paper provides 104 numbered axioms through `a104`, plus later case-specific axioms, definitions, and theorems; it does not report a benchmark score for the theory.
The TPTP theory was submitted to multiple automated provers, but the paper does not name the provers, state runtimes, or tabulate consistency or satisfiability outcomes.
Five FOUST-derived cases demonstrate expressiveness for composition/constitution, roles, property change, event change, and concept evolution.
The concept-evolution simulation shows a model accommodating monogamous heterosexual and same-sex conjugal-relationship types under shared invariants.
The usage survey lists applications across many domains, including legal issues, smart contracts, trust, services, risk, and economic exchange, but it is evidence of adoption rather than a controlled evaluation here.
The paper cites an external OntoUML-versus-EER experiment with 100 participants in two countries as evidence of model-quality improvement without added production effort.
No KG construction task, query benchmark, legal-expert evaluation, inter-annotator study, or patent-domain evaluation is performed.

## Limitations (authors' own + yours)

The formalization focuses on UFO-A and only selected UFO-B categories; UFO-C social, intentional, normative, and communicative machinery is not developed here.
The authors explicitly state that UFO has no definition of function, despite modeling dispositions, capabilities, and functional complexes.
The fixed-domain QS5 possibilist commitment and the claim that perdurants exist only in the past are strong metaphysical choices that may not fit operational event stores.
The paper reports prover submission without reproducible prover identities or results, weakening the claimed formal verification evidence.
Many worked models are demonstrations of satisfiable structures, not evidence that domain experts agree with the ontological analysis.
Relators capture dependence and truthmaking but do not themselves provide deontic force, legal authority, validity, defeasibility, jurisdiction, or Hohfeldian opposition/correlation axioms.
The change account separates mutable endurants from completed perdurants, but a production KG still needs explicit valid-time and transaction-time representations.
The higher-order theory exceeds straightforward RDF/OWL-DL expressivity; a deployment must distinguish conceptual truth from tractable graph constraints.
The adoption and 100-participant claims are summarized from other studies, with no data or analysis reproduced in this paper.

## Beep-relevance hooks

- `semantic-foundation / SKOS and @beep/ontology`: take Kind, Role, Phase, Relator, and event distinctions as modeling diagnostics, but keep SKOS concept-scheme organization separate from UFO’s stronger modal and identity commitments.
- `epistemic slice`: represent claims, evidence-bearing assertions, and contradictions as dependent entities with explicit bearers and foundations; combine `foundedBy`/`manifests` provenance with bitemporal valid-time and transaction-time edges rather than treating events as mutable records.
- `patent tooling / uspto-patent-driver-depth`: model an application, patent, claim, assignment, prosecution event, inventor role, and assignee role with identity-preserving kinds and reified relators; reject naked binary party links where the relation has dates, terms, or evidence.
- `patent tooling / agentic-cad-patent-tooling`: use qualities and quality structures for measured design parameters and use dispositions/functions only where their manifestation conditions are explicit; do not infer function semantics from the paper because UFO leaves function undefined.
- `OIP solo-practice workflows`: reify engagements, representations, deadlines, filings, and attorney-client commitments as relators founded by acts, allowing role changes without changing the underlying person or organization identity.
- `agent memory / Cognee`: retain stable entity kinds separately from contingent roles, phases, assertions, and event manifestations so memory consolidation does not merge a person with a temporary legal or agent role.
