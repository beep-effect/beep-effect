# Interactive Methods in Teaching of the Elective Discipline “Fundamentals of Patent Law” as an Element of the Legal Education of Future Doctors

## Problem & positioning

- Medical research produces inventions and other intellectual outputs, but future doctors may lack the legal and practical skills to protect, transfer, search, and commercialize them.
- The course must make a document-heavy subject—laws, regulations, applications, and patent information—engaging for science students during pandemic-era distance learning.
- The paper presents the elective “Fundamentals of Patent Law” for second-year master's students in specialty 222 “Medicine” at Bogomolets National Medical University.
- Its intended outcomes combine knowledge of Ukrainian and international intellectual-property regulation with practical patent-search and application-drafting skills.
- It positions interactive learning, particularly Quizizz tests and games, as a way to combine instruction, immediate feedback, discussion, motivation, and competency formation.
- The contribution is a curriculum and experience report, not a controlled study of learning efficacy or an ontology/KG system.

## Approach / architecture

- The authors used comparative and systematic review of scientific and pedagogical literature, pedagogical observation, systematization of teaching experience, and interactive tests and games.
- The 2021–2022 course allocated 120 hours (4.0 ECTS): 10 lecture hours, 10 practical-class hours, and 100 hours of independent work.
- Thirty-six enrolled students attended scheduled online lectures and practical classes through Google Meet.
- The Moodle-based LIKAR_NMU platform held lectures, self-study material, links to games and tests, and motivational videos.
- Quizizz supplied approximately ten interactive tests or games in each lesson; question content and game form were tied to the official curriculum.
- Viber groups supported discussion prompted by interactive testing.
- A teacher followed a lesson card to activate tests, games, and videos at planned points, monitor actions and phase transitions, and intervene with corrections.
- Flipped lessons, spiral learning, situational tasks, case-method activities, videos, and podcasts supplemented the interactive quizzes.
- Ten curriculum units progress from IP-system basics through copyright, inventions, designs, trademarks, medicinal products, patent information, medical patent research, agreements, commercialization, and international protection.
- Assessment was a differential assessment; the paper does not state its rubric, score distribution, or relation to the interactive activities.

## Ontology & KG modeling contributions

- No formal ontology, axioms, knowledge graph, controlled vocabulary, or machine-readable curriculum is produced.
- The curriculum nevertheless exposes a candidate concept scheme with top concepts `IntellectualPropertyRight`, `ProtectedObject`, `RightHolder`, `Agreement`, `PatentDocument`, `PatentInformationSource`, and `CommercializationMethod`.
- Protected-object types include invention, utility model, industrial design, trademark, copyright work, related-right object, production secret, and medicine or manufacturing process.
- Medical-invention subject matter is subdivided into device, substance, microorganism strain, plant or animal cell culture, process/method, new use of a known product or method, and group of inventions.
- Patentability concepts include novelty, inventive level, and industrial applicability; application concepts include description and claims, including claim purpose, requirements, and structure.
- Candidate relations include `application seeksProtectionFor object`, `claim defines invention`, `right heldBy authorOrOwner`, `agreement transfersOrLicenses right`, and `search uses database`.
- Further useful relations are `document classifiedBy IPC`, `commercializationMethod exploits right`, `law governs activity`, and `competency evidencedBy task`.
- The course distinguishes primary and secondary documentary sources and attributes completeness and veracity to sources, but gives no formal evidence or provenance model.
- Its ten-unit sequence is a pedagogical taxonomy rather than a defensible ontology: categories mix legal objects, activities, institutions, documents, and learning outcomes.
- A KG implementation would need jurisdiction, effective-time, authority, and source-span qualifiers because the course explicitly mixes Ukrainian law, international instruments, and practical procedures.

## Evaluation & results

- The reported cohort contains 36 students in the 2021–2022 academic year.
- The authors report high activity, many clarifying questions, and discussion of practical examples in every session, but provide no counts or observation protocol.
- Students reportedly learned Ukrainian patent laws and Cabinet resolutions, application procedures, invention criteria, patent information, search types, official publications, and research methodology.
- Reported practical proficiencies include using IP laws, drafting descriptions and claims, searching Ukrainian and foreign databases, identifying patentable subject matter, and drafting invention or utility-model applications from a sample.
- Students reportedly described the course as useful and informative and highlighted legal sources, filing rules, database searching, and author/patent-owner rights.
- No pre/post test, control group, baseline, completion rate, score distribution, effect size, statistical test, retention measure, or validated survey instrument is reported.
- The conclusion that competencies were “effectively mastered” is therefore supported only by instructor observation and unspecified student feedback.

## Limitations (authors' own + yours)

- The authors do not state substantive study limitations; they present the intervention positively.
- A single 36-student cohort at one Ukrainian medical university cannot establish transferability to other institutions, jurisdictions, or professional stages.
- There is no comparator against lectures, non-gamified quizzes, or another online-learning design.
- Neither the approximately ten activities per lesson nor their individual questions, difficulty, feedback rules, or scoring logic are documented for replication.
- The paper does not distinguish engagement from doctrinal accuracy, durable learning, search competence, or application-drafting quality.
- Self-report and teacher observation are vulnerable to novelty, social-desirability, and confirmation effects.
- The teacher-controlled phase architecture may increase feedback but also makes it unclear which component—Quizizz, discussion, cases, flipped lessons, spiral repetition, or videos—produced any outcome.
- Jurisdiction-specific content can become stale; laws, filing procedures, databases, and international practice require versioned curriculum maintenance.
- The statement that correct medicine patenting “saves people's lives” is motivational rather than an evaluated causal result.
- No accessibility, digital-divide, privacy, platform-dependence, or academic-integrity analysis is supplied for the remote tools.
- No ontology evaluation is possible because the paper offers only curricular groupings and prose concepts.

## Beep-relevance hooks

- **semantic-foundation (SKOS/@beep/ontology):** Turn the ten curriculum units into a versioned SKOS teaching scheme, but separate legal objects, actions, sources, institutions, and competencies instead of preserving the paper's mixed hierarchy.
- **epistemic slice:** Attach every teachable legal proposition and quiz answer to jurisdiction, authority, valid time, curriculum version, and exact evidence span; reject static answer keys for mutable law.
- **patent tooling (uspto-patent-driver-depth):** Reuse the competency progression—subject-matter identification, classification, database search, analog finding, and claim/application drafting—as task-level acceptance criteria for guided tooling.
- **patent tooling (agentic-cad-patent-tooling):** Adopt scenario quizzes and immediate explanatory feedback for invention/patentability training, but require recorded answers and cited patent evidence before treating performance as mastery.
- **OIP solo-practice workflows:** Use short interactive case checks to teach intake and issue spotting across right type, jurisdiction, owner, filing path, agreement, and commercialization route; keep lawyer review for conclusions.
- **agent memory (Cognee):** Store question, answer, governing authority, curriculum version, observed error pattern, and remediation as separate entities so repeated misconceptions can drive later training without freezing outdated law.
