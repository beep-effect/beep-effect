# Events as Entities in Ontology-Driven Conceptual Modeling

## Problem & positioning

OntoUML historically emphasized structural domain aspects—endurant types and their relations—through UFO-A.
The paper addresses the missing representation of event types and event relations in structural conceptual models.

## Approach

The authors draw on UFO-B's ontological distinctions and axiomatization of events.
They extend the OntoUML UML-profile vocabulary with constructs and modeling guidelines for events.

## Ontology & KG modeling contributions

Events are modeled as entities rather than reduced to state changes or relationship annotations.
The extension connects event types and event relations to the same ontology-driven conceptual-modeling discipline already used for endurants.
This provides a foundation for representing legal acts, filings, grants, amendments, decisions, and violations as first-class occurrences.
The specific stereotypes, axioms, and event-relation taxonomy are not visible in the preview.
