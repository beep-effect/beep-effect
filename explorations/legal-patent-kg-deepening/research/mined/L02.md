# UFO-L Project: A Core Legal Reference Ontology

## Problem & positioning

The project argues that many legal models inherit monadic deontic logic's weak support for relations between parties.
It also positions computer-science-led models as missing concepts from contemporary legal theory, especially power and legal liberty.

## Approach

UFO-L grounds its legal concepts in Hohfeld's fundamental legal concepts, Alexy's relational theory of constitutional rights, and UFO's notion of a relator.
The page presents the ontology through a publication lineage spanning legal relations, service contracts, judicial decision-making, and later legal-theory analysis.

## Ontology & KG modeling contributions

The stated domain includes rights and duties, no-rights and permissions, powers and liabilities, disabilities and immunities, and liberties.
Legal relations are represented as relational entities rather than isolated unary modalities.
The relator foundation makes contracts and other multi-party legal settings first-class modeling targets.
The publication list shows progressive application to service-contract enterprise architecture and modeling patterns for legal relations.

## Beep-relevance hooks

- Model Hohfeldian correlatives as typed relational schemas with explicit bearer and counterparty roles.
- Separate permission/no-right and power/liability instead of collapsing every legal relation into obligation.
