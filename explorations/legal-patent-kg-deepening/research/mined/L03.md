# UFO-L: A Core Ontology of Legal Concepts Built from a Legal Relations Perspective

## Problem & positioning

The Ph.D. proposal identifies a gap between computational legal conceptualizations and legal theory.
It targets reusable concepts such as legal norm, legal fact, and legal relation, while arguing that principle-insensitive models fail to reflect contemporary legal practice.

## Approach

The proposal combines legal theories with a foundational ontology.
It selects Alexy's Theory of Fundamental Rights, shifts focus from legal norms to legal relations, and proposes UFO-L as a new layer of UFO.
Its stated research questions ask whether ontologies effectively represent the contemporary legal world relationally and what modeling benefits a legal core ontology provides.

## Ontology & KG modeling contributions

UFO-L is positioned as a generic legal-concept layer grounded in selected legal theories.
The subjectivist orientation makes relations among legal subjects primary rather than treating norms as the sole organizing unit.
The references locate the proposal among FOLaw, LRI-Core, LKIF-Core, Hohfeld, LegalRuleML, legal-RDF, and earlier legal top ontologies.
