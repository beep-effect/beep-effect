# Effective Identification of Technological Opportunities for Radical Inventions Using International Patent Classification: Application of Patent Data Mining

## Problem & positioning

- Radical-invention opportunities are harder to identify than incremental improvements because they depend on distinctly different technologies and difficult unresolved problems.
- Prior patent-opportunity work uses citations, morphology, semantic maps, and text mining but underuses the expert-designed hierarchy and functional/structural knowledge in IPC symbols.
- The paper defines a candidate radical opportunity as a patent combining high value of technological novelty (VON) with a high-difficulty R&D theme (VOD).
- VON is inferred from when an IPC symbol first appears in a corpus and how far it lies from previously observed IPC symbols.
- VOD is proxied by how many IPC technologies connect to a text-derived R&D theme, interpreting cross-domain integration as problem difficulty.
- A two-axis IPC-based map ranks existing patents and highlights blank regions where novel technologies might address difficult themes.
- A DJI unmanned-aerial-vehicle case study demonstrates feasibility and generates example opportunity proposals; it does not validate prediction against future radical inventions.

## Approach / architecture

- Retrieve granted invention patents and collect filing dates, IPC symbols, and description sections; the authors recommend background sections as statements of existing technical problems.
- Build an IPC-by-date count matrix, retaining the first appearance year for every IPC symbol in the selected corpus.
- Construct a technology-change map whose nodes are IPC symbols first present in the initial year or newly appearing later.
- Assign pairwise IPC difference 2 when symbols differ only at the last letter, 3 when they share a section/chapter-level prefix as described, and 4 when chapter symbols are inconsistent.
- Compute novelty as first-appearance time rank plus the minimum distance to an earlier IPC symbol; later and more structurally distant symbols score higher.
- Preprocess background text with a user dictionary, stop-word removal, segmentation, and part-of-speech handling using `jieba`/NLTK.
- Extract discriminating terms with TF-IDF, represent each patent as a normalized feature vector, and form cosine-distance matrices.
- Choose K through an LDA-perplexity heuristic, run K-means, and visualize with multidimensional scaling; human interpretation consolidates clusters into R&D themes.
- Build an IPC–R&D-theme matrix and bipartite network; degree centrality of each theme node becomes its VOD.
- Place IPC technologies on the x-axis by VON and themes on the y-axis by VOD; patents at intersections farther from the origin are proposed radical-invention candidates.
- Blank high-VON/high-VOD regions are treated as prompts for feasibility analysis and technology recombination.

## Ontology & KG modeling contributions

- IPC symbols form a pre-existing hierarchical concept system whose labels encode combinations of purpose, function, structure, material, method, operation, and control knowledge.
- The temporal technology-change graph has `IPCConcept` nodes, `firstAppearedIn` links to filing-year buckets, and weighted `differsFrom` edges derived from hierarchy distance.
- The IPC/date matrix records corpus-local observation counts, not the universal birth date of a technology; provenance to corpus and query is essential.
- Text-derived `R&DTheme` nodes summarize technical-problem clusters such as flight control, shooting/image processing, telecontrol/communication, energy, and transmission.
- The IPC–theme network is bipartite: an IPC concept connects to a theme when patents in that theme bear the classification.
- Theme degree centrality is treated as a difficulty score because more connected IPC domains imply more cross-domain technology used to solve the problem.
- A patent links one or more IPC concepts, one text cluster/theme, filing time, applicant, and source background passages; these are the minimum graph entities needed to reproduce VON/VOD.
- The coordinate map is a derived analytic view rather than an ontology: `(VON(IPC), VOD(theme))` locates patents and exposes unoccupied combinations.
- Human regrouping from 12 K-means clusters to 6 functional themes is an ontology-learning step and should be preserved as a reviewable mapping with curator provenance.
- The model contains no logical axioms establishing radicality; high VON/VOD is a heuristic candidate classification.
- The paper's raw IPC prefix-distance rules are a coarse taxonomy metric and should not be reified as semantic equivalence or technological distance without validation.

## Evaluation & results

- An initial Patsnap query collected 19,235 granted UAV patents filed from 2000 through 12 November 2019 for landscape context.
- DJI was selected because its patents and reported product/market history make it a plausible radical-invention case.
- The focused SIPO query collected 720 DJI UAV invention patents through 22 June 2022; their filing dates, IPC symbols, and description sections form the case corpus.
- DJI applications in the corpus rise from 1, 0, 3, and 3 in 2010–2013 to 50 in 2014, 56 in 2015, 197 in 2016, and 250 in 2017.
- The authors warn that apparent declines after 2017 reflect grant/publication lag and incomplete recent data rather than demonstrated innovation decline.
- Low-frequency IPC symbols associated with only one patent are excluded as noise based partly on domain judgment.
- VON scores in the displayed DJI table range from 2 for G01C, through 6 and 7 for intermediate entrants, to 8 for later/distant symbols including G06F, G06T, H04N, and B64C.
- TextRank extracts at most 200 verbs or nouns from each background; TF-IDF document-frequency bounds are set to 10% and 90%.
- The perplexity heuristic selects 12 K-means clusters, which are manually consolidated into 6 R&D themes.
- Theme degree centralities are 29 for flight control, 27 for customer/manipulation “others,” 26 for shooting/image execution, 20 for communication, 16 for energy, and 14 for transmission.
- The high-VON/high-VOD region yields 8 listed core control-system patents and 12 listed execution-system patents.
- Suggested blank-space opportunities include G02B optical/AR guidance for flight, H04B synchronous multi-camera 3D capture, G06Q flight analytics/forecasting, and G11B aided shooting/editing.
- One G02B opportunity is retrospectively associated with a later published DJI headset/video-glasses patent, but no systematic precision, recall, ranking baseline, expert agreement, market outcome, or prospective hit rate is reported.

## Limitations (authors' own + yours)

- The authors state that IPC hierarchy difference is only an approximation for sophisticated cutting-edge technology and propose semantic analysis of IPC titles.
- Background-section keyword extraction contains noise; proposed future methods include subject-action-object extraction, dependency parsing, structural topic models, and deep learning.
- They propose adding multiple input criteria and post-analysis such as MANOVA rather than relying only on VON/VOD.
- Generalizability is untested because the detailed case covers one Chinese firm's UAV patents; the authors call for other UAV firms, semiconductors, and drug patents.
- Corpus-local first appearance confounds technological novelty with query design, applicant portfolio history, filing coverage, classification practice, and grant lag.
- The hard-coded IPC distances 2/3/4 are ordinal judgments without empirical calibration and ignore polyhierarchy, symbol revisions, CPC detail, and semantic closeness across branches.
- Degree centrality measures breadth of classification linkage, not inherent engineering difficulty; common, broadly classified themes can appear difficult by construction.
- K-means assumes Euclidean cluster structure, depends on initialization and K, while LDA perplexity is not a direct validation criterion for K-means partitions.
- Manual consolidation of 12 clusters into 6 themes is under-specified and lacks multiple annotators or agreement statistics.
- Excluding IPC symbols seen once may delete exactly the rare, novel combinations the method seeks.
- “Farther from origin” ranks patents but supplies no threshold or calibrated probability of radicality.
- Proposed future opportunities receive qualitative feasibility narratives, not prior-art, enablement, commercial, safety, or freedom-to-operate validation.

## Beep-relevance hooks

- `patent tooling / uspto-patent-driver-depth`: build a versioned IPC/CPC hierarchy and patent-classification event graph, but calculate first appearance per explicit corpus/query and expose classification/grant lag.
- `agentic-cad-patent-tooling`: use high-VON/high-theme-centrality blank cells as ideation prompts, then require technical feasibility, source-span evidence, prior-art search, and claim-support gates before drafting.
- `semantic-foundation (SKOS/@beep/ontology)`: represent IPC symbols as an external hierarchical concept scheme with mappings into curated functional concepts; reject fixed prefix distance as authoritative semantic distance.
- `epistemic slice / claims and evidence spans`: retain VON, VOD, cluster membership, and “radical opportunity” as versioned analytic claims with corpus, parameters, curator decisions, and supporting patent-background spans.
- `epistemic contradiction triage`: compare alternative novelty metrics, clusterings, and expert theme assignments as competing analyses rather than overwriting a single score.
- `OIP solo-practice workflows`: offer portfolio landscapes and whitespace maps for attorney/client review, while clearly separating technology adjacency from patentability, infringement risk, and freedom to operate.
