# Capture

## 2026-09-03

SuperGrok Heavy includes Grok Bot, and current monthly use is far below the
allowance. There are already two hosted bots worth turning into disciplined
automation: the nightly research routine and professional-desktop QA.

The seed ideas were documentation enhancement, knowledge and documentation
staleness detection, and a "beep style & law enhancer." The earlier
[`beep-mode`](../beep-mode/) exploration, which distilled the pstack agent
operating model, looked like the nearest precedent for authority, evidence,
model roles, and promotion.

The question is not how to spend unused tokens. It is how to turn hosted
judgment into bounded proposals while preserving local checkout proof,
credential isolation, deterministic publication, and human merge authority.
