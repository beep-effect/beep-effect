## §2 Cross-lane conflicts

- **Active simulation versus static embedding (a vs b):** lane (a)'s bundle proof shows an active d3 force engine with configured forces and cooldown; lane (b)'s runtime global scan infers “static, precomputed embedding” from baked xyz and lack of exposed globals. The inference is contradicted. The safe contract is “baked xyz enters a d3-backed component”; whether the product should freeze or simulate remains a design choice.
- **2D/3D depth ownership (a vs b/final demo):** lane (a) says dropping below three dimensions deletes `z/vz` and returning to 3D regenerates depth. The final graph has z on all nodes and the runtime pass observed identical z restoration. Specify non-destructive flatten/restore unless a prototype deliberately chooses re-layout.
- **Dark background (a vs b):** lane (a)'s bundle fallback is `#000000`; lane (b)'s direct WebGL clear-color read is `#111111`. This is an override/state distinction, but the clean-room visual target must use `#111111`, not call black the observed value.
- **Dimming attribution (a vs b):** numeric 0.10 ordinary/0.35 group constants are bundle-confirmed. Lane (b)'s sampled 0.35 split occurred during automatic topic highlighting; it does not confirm manual-click selection. Values agree; state attribution does not.
- **Edge numbers (a vs b):** geometry type agrees strongly (tube/ribbon triangles). Curvature 0.25, rotation 0.25, radial resolution 4, width curve, and ~0.563 global opacity come only from static analysis; lane (b) explicitly did not measure those numerically.
- **Label density at extreme zoom (the two browser passes):** `interaction-notes.md:38-45` reports zero labels at the farthest phase, while `behavior-notes.md:18-27` says topic callouts remained visible at its farthest manual zoom. Because both passes shared one changing tab and automatic filtering, this disagreement must remain a state-dependent observation, not a fixed threshold.
- **2D toggle query side effects (the two browser passes):** `behavior-notes.md:77-99` saw edge/label URL presets change but no obvious flattening; `interaction-notes.md:51-60` directly sampled z flatten/restore. Shared-tab interference explains the URL observation; only the direct z/scene identity measurement should define the dimensional behavior.
- **Versions/compatibility (d vs f):** Three/R3F/drei/React versions agree. The material conflict is lane (d)'s Reagraph semver conclusion: lane (f) confirms repo Three 0.185.1, while Reagraph's `^0.184.0` excludes it. Other candidate compatibility remains manifest-level until installation/dedupe proof.

## §6 Net assessment

**Yes, with fixes.** The primary evidence is unusually strong for visual fingerprinting: bundle constants, two independent browser passes, scene/material introspection, WebGL draw-mode capture, final graph data, and live repo manifests agree on the renderer family, sprites, tube edges, palette, importance metric, and most numeric styling.

The design gate must not run as though every conclusion is settled. Reagraph's compatibility result is wrong; static-versus-simulated layout and z-toggle behavior need correction; source/license ledgers are stale; selection attribution and all target-scale performance remain unconfirmed. After items 1-9 are corrected, the corpus is trustworthy enough to choose a prototype stack and clean-room style spec, with item 10 retained as the acceptance gate rather than assumed evidence.
