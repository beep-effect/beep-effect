Research date: 2026-08-25.

Packet context (not independently verified on the public web): `explorations/lejeune-bolt-agentic-demo/CAPTURE.md` records a 2026-08-25 conversation with Jackson LeJeune, an employee in sales / marketing / logistics and son of an executive. The lunch pitch next week is framed around veterans retiring, Microsoft 365 as the system of record, and a demo that ingests office data into a fastener knowledge graph. This file is the public-web half of that brief. Packet-only claims are marked **PACKET / UNVERIFIED**.

Do not confuse **LeJeune Bolt Company** (Burnsville fastener distributor, this subject) with **LeJeune Steel Company** (Minneapolis structural fabricator, APi Group, separate firm that split off in 1977). Both appear on Twin Cities steel jobs. Mixing them invents a customer relationship that the public record does not support.

## Company profile

LeJeune Bolt Company is a privately held Minnesota structural fastener distributor. Public self-description: supplier of structural bolts, installation tools, concrete anchors, stud welding, and general-line fasteners, and exclusive source of the patented TNA (Torque + Angle) fastening system using ASTM F3148 144 ksi fixed-spline assemblies. Tagline on the homepage: "Big Bolts. Big Projects. Big, Big Service." ([lejeunebolt.com](https://lejeunebolt.com/))

**Legal / SAM.** SAM.gov lists legal name LEJEUNE BOLT CO, Burnsville MN, Unique Entity ID W3HMKMJB7DA7, CAGE 1LMB9, incorporated in Minnesota, entity structure corporate (not tax exempt), business types for-profit and Subchapter S corporation, also self-certified small disadvantaged business. Registered 2002-04-02. Business start date 1977-01-03. Officers named: Karla Berg (Controller), Jeff Greene (President), Chad Larson (title blank on the aggregator page). Snapshot accessed 2026-08-25 listed status Expired, with activation 2025-08-29 and expiration 2026-08-27. Treat SAM status as time-sensitive. ([opengovus.com/sam-entity/W3HMKMJB7DA7](https://opengovus.com/sam-entity/W3HMKMJB7DA7))

**Locations.** Headquarters: 3500 West Highway 13, Burnsville, MN 55337. Western Region: 3655 Placentia Ct, Chino, CA 91710. Main 952.890.7700, toll-free 800.USA.BOLT (872.2658), Western 909.529.7577 / 877.684.4091. Hours stated as 8:00 am to 5:00 pm CT (MN) and PST (CA), weekdays. ([lejeunebolt.com](https://lejeunebolt.com/); [shortspansteelbridges.org supplier page](https://www.shortspansteelbridges.org/suppliers/lejeune-bolt-company/))

**Founding.** The live homepage says the firm began in a small warehouse in Burnsville in 1976. The 2016 brochure says a small garage in Minneapolis in 1977. LinkedIn and SAM use 1977.
