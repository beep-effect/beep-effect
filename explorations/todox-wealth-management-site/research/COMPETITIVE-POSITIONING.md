# Competitive positioning corpus

Captured 2026-08-27 with `firecrawl-competitive-intel`: 30 searches, 21
maps, 140 successful primary-page scrapes, two documented scrape failures, and
423 provenance records. Public marketing is treated as a vendor claim, not
independent capability proof.

Todox is a vision-only concept. Every open position below is a hypothesis,
not a shipped differentiator or proof that no competitor has the capability.

notetaker or copilot category.

## Open positioning hypotheses

These are the strongest concepts not found as named, complete product objects
in the reviewed public sources:

1. **Bounded model-call admission.** Before a model call, firm policy evaluates
   purpose, data class, approved model, permitted tool, credential class, and a
   spend ceiling.
2. **Versioned source-span evidence.** Candidate meeting-prep assertions point
   to stable spans and retain the exact source version.
3. **Supersession.** A new instruction or fact identifies what it replaces and
   preserves the earlier evidence and interpretation history.
4. **Action authorization as a record.** A named approver, scoped action,
   optional dual control, and a refusal to write to a system of record without
   authorization.
5. **Execution ledger.** Model, tool, credential class, cost, actor, approver,
   time, source, and disposition stay with the work product.
6. **Infrastructure around the installed stack.** Evidence and control for the
   notetakers, CRMs, planning tools, and approved models a firm already uses,
   rather than another capture bot.
7. **Advisor-specific proof surface.** A source-linked client index becomes a
   reviewable meeting-preparation packet without autonomous advice, trading, or
   money movement.

The compact territory is `control, evidence, and ledgers`. It remains a
research hypothesis until Todox implements and proves each mechanism.

## Target-account collision

Public account research adds two material constraints:

- A joint 2026 vendor/customer release says Mariner is undertaking a five-year
  AI-workforce program spanning onboarding, account opening, compliance review,
  client reporting, billing, prospect onboarding, and cross-serve work. Todox
  cannot arrive as generic back-office automation or another “AI workforce.”
- AdvicePeriod's public technology story already includes estate-document AI
  through Vanilla, an API/integration posture, and a technology-forward brand.
  Todox cannot arrive as document OCR, planning analysis, or innovation theater.

The plausible non-overlap is meeting-to-meeting client-context continuity with
source provenance and scoped authorization. That is a discovery question, not
a confirmed internal gap.

## Account-tailored discovery questions

- What advisor work remains unserved after the announced AI-workforce scope?
- Where does meeting preparation obtain current household context today?
- When a source changes, can a reviewer see what it superseded?
- Which actions require named approval, dual control, or a system-of-record
  write boundary?
- Can the firm reconstruct the source, model, tool, credential class, cost,
  actor, and reviewer behind a work product?
- Where do acquired teams lose local client knowledge during handoffs?
- Which model may receive which client-data class, for which purpose, under
  whose policy?
- What would prove value without inventing a time-saved or ROI figure?

## Claim guardrails

Do not claim that Todox uniquely owns or currently provides:

- local or on-prem deployment;
- citations, sourced answers, or explainability;
- human review or approval;
- MCP or integrations;
- privacy-first or no-recording architecture;
- an AI OS, copilot, or autonomous workforce;
- a security certification or zero-retention policy;
- a named customer, AUM figure, adoption figure, time saving, or ROI;
- autonomous financial advice, trades, money movement, tax conclusions, or
  unsupervised client communication.

Do not use `only`, `first`, or `the AI OS for wealth`. Until product proof
exists, express the seven open territories as design intent or questions a
private walkthrough can examine.

## Research limits

- Public pages only; no authenticated vendor dashboards.
- A missing public page is not proof of a missing capability.
- Marketing certifications and results were not independently audited.
- Several trust reports were gated and not read.
- Some pricing evidence lives in dated press or comparison pages and may be
  stale.
- Vendor nomenclature changes quickly; re-run primary pages before a sales
  conversation that depends on a specific feature or price.
