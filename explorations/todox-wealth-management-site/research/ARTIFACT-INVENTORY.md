# Historical artifact inventory

The two public Claude artifacts are useful demonstrations, not product proof or
visual authority. Both use synthetic people, households, firms, accounts,
balances, events, metrics, dates, and outcomes.

## Artifact `94890cda`: knowledge-graph demonstration

The artifact explores a conceptual ontology spanning households, clients,
accounts, positions, obligations, life events, documents, meetings, and agent
memory. Useful narrative and interaction ingredients include:

- scenario selection;
- relationship traversal;
- evidence inspection;
- episodic, semantic, and procedural memory layers;
- a visible action and evidence trace.

Restrictions:

- `Meridian` is a stale artifact-only demonstration name, not the product.
- Animated or unhydrated counters are not claims.
- “Watch an agent think” must become a visible action-and-evidence trace, not a
  promise to expose hidden model reasoning.
- Permanent-memory, complete-audit, and universal-traceability language is
  aspiration, not proof.

## Artifact `6f0c3839`: client-orrery demonstration

The artifact explores a spatial household/context view, scenario flags,
entity drill-down, memory categories, an execution timeline, and a
meeting-preparation narrative.

Restrictions:

- All household and financial details are fiction.
- It is not evidence for a graph-heavy, dark, neon, orbital, or dashboard-led
  marketing direction.
- Its performance figures, entity counts, and compliance language cannot be
  reused as customer evidence.

## Safe material for Fable to reinterpret

- A source-to-claim-to-task-to-review sequence.
- A source-linked household or client index.
- A meeting-preparation assertion that opens its supporting source.
- Visible differences among current, superseded, candidate, and accepted
  information.
- An activity trace naming actor, source, time, tool or model, and review state.
- Scenario switching among meeting preparation, liquidity, and review, while
  keeping the public narrative focused on client index to meeting preparation.

## Demonstration labeling

Any later site rendition must label demonstration records as synthetic wherever
a visitor could mistake them for client data, an operating customer, or a
measured outcome. Fable may write new synthetic material at production
fidelity, but it must not convert the historical artifact into a testimonial,
case study, or deployed-product screenshot.
