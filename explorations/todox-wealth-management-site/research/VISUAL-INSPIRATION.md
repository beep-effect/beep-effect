## Nonbinding inspiration families

### 1. Cream editorial atelier

Evidence: Mariner's cream room, Dimensional's paper and landscape pacing, and
Bessemer's editorial scale.

Possible ingredients: warm paper, charcoal type, one mineral signal, a single
display stress, small-radius controls, and still-life evidence rather than a
yacht or boardroom. The open territory drops Mariner's teal and people and
Dimensional's landscape.

### 2. Numbered fiduciary English

Evidence: AdvicePeriod's moral sequence, Credo's numbered governance features,
and Bessemer's report numbering.

Possible ingredients: short sentences that read aloud, numbers as procedural
spine, hairline illustration, and restrained signal color. It stops being open
territory when orange line art, quote marks, or the three AdvicePeriod
objectives return.

### 3. Family-office ledger, recast as an invention

No captured reference is actually a ledger. The nearest ingredients are
Mariner's forms and cream interiors, Bessemer's square paper tiles,
Dimensional's footnotes, and Tailscale's work surface.

If Fable explores this family, treat it as an original invention: tabular
numerals, modest titles, hairline rules, and squared paper. Avoid “old money”
costume, leather, marble, and cream-plus-teal advisor branding.

### 4. Financial-science paper

Evidence: Dimensional's claim/method/footnote rhythm, Bessemer's report genre,
and Stardog's explanatory diagrams.

Possible ingredients: cool paper, a restrained serif/sans contrast, real
source spans, plots that contain defensible synthetic data, square controls,
and prominent qualifications. Do not borrow investment-performance authority
or render empty decorative evidence wells.

### 5. Privilege and security desk

Evidence: 1Password's dark control surface, Tailscale's light operator desk,
and Credo's policy objects.

Possible ingredients: access, policy, and review as tangible artifacts; a real
synthetic console or registry; and a light-desk alternative to cyber-neon. The
dark version has high clone and command-center risk.

### 6. Origins and evidence on the table

Evidence: Stardog's labeled diagrams, Dimensional's footnotes, Bessemer's
report objects, Credo's registry, and Temporal's timeline.

Possible ingredients: a source span, policy, actor, date, disposition, and
timeline presented as objects a visitor can inspect. This is the closest
family to the product story and the easiest to ruin with glowing nodes or an
AI-brain metaphor.

### 7. Durable work as furniture

Evidence: Temporal's failure-recovery story, Tailscale's quiet work surface,
1Password's control language, and AdvicePeriod's operating promises.

Possible ingredients: work that remains inspectable when a step fails, a
packet or timeline instead of a chat transcript, and a workhorse type voice.
The narrative need not inherit Temporal's dark, neon visual world.

## Cross-family tensions for Fable

- Cream plus people photography becomes Mariner too quickly.
- White plus orange numbered line art becomes AdvicePeriod.
- Landscape plus chart becomes Dimensional.
- Dark plus floating rounded product chrome becomes 1Password or generic AI.
- Lime plus nodes becomes Stardog; rainbow plus timeline becomes Temporal.
- A ledger can feel like useful work or “old money” costume.
- A product screenshot can prove mechanism or turn the brand into a dashboard.
- “Knowledge graph” can describe a model while visually collapsing into an
  unearned glowing-network claim.

## Shared anti-references

- Generic dark neon, glowing graphs, particle fields, glass panels, AI brains,
  orbit diagrams, and ominous command centers.
- Sailboats, golden retrievers, lake-as-wealth shorthand, marble lobbies, and
  generic smiling-advisor photography.
- Dominant chat bubbles or meeting-recorder UI.
- Unearned SOC 2, regulator, analyst, award, customer-logo, or performance
  bands.
- Cloned Mariner or AdvicePeriod branding.
- Any third-party screenshot or artwork presented as Todox product material.
