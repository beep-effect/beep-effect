## Mechanism the site may explain

The honest product mechanism is:

1. Keep source artifacts canonical and identify stable source spans.
2. Let an agent propose candidate claims, tasks, and drafts.
3. Attach evidence, actor, model/tool usage, and activity provenance.
4. Route professional judgment and client-facing work through advisor or
   policy review.
5. Record the scoped disposition without pretending approval makes the
   underlying proposition true.
6. Build bounded context and optional actions from accepted runtime state.
7. Leave CRM, custodian, email, calendar, document, billing, and other external
   platforms authoritative for the data they own.

This follows the runtime contract in
[`goals/agentic-professional-runtime/SPEC.md`](../../../goals/agentic-professional-runtime/SPEC.md).

## Safe public claims

These are mechanism or vision statements. They still need careful tense and
should not be presented as customer results.

- Todox is a concept for a firm-controlled agent runtime around professional
  work, not another meeting recorder.
- The product direction centers source-linked client context, candidate work,
  human review, action boundaries, and auditable activity.
- The flagship scenario turns source material into a living client index and
  a reviewable meeting-preparation packet.
- The proposed runtime is designed to leave existing systems of record
  authoritative. Todox would coordinate context, proposed work, review, and
  provenance around them.
- Its proposed authority model pairs firm-controlled data and actions with
  policy-approved model calls that receive bounded context and leave an
  activity record naming the model, tool, credential class, actor, and
  approval.
- Demonstrations use synthetic data unless a future source explicitly says
  otherwise.

## Claims that need a qualifier

| Candidate phrase | Required treatment |
| --- | --- |
| "Built for wealth management" | Say "a wealth-management product direction" or "a concept shaped around advisor work." |
| "Explainable" | Define it as source provenance, action trace, actor, time, model/tool use, and review disposition. Never promise hidden chain-of-thought. |
| "Local-first" | Define firm-controlled data and action authority plus bounded, approved model calls. Do not say fully offline. |
| "Knowledge graph" | Use only for the proposed model or synthetic demonstration. Do not claim production scale, integrity, or completeness. |
| "Evidence-backed" | Tie it to exact source spans in the deterministic fixture or state it as a product principle. |
| "Integrates with your stack" | Recast as "designed to sit around existing systems of record" until named connectors are built and proven. |
| "Compliance-ready" | Replace with concrete review, policy, and audit concepts. Do not imply a certification or legal conclusion. |

## Forbidden public claims

- Either researched firm or either user-supplied individual is a customer,
  partner, endorser, pilot participant, or design partner.
- Todox is deployed, production-ready, generally available, SOC 2 certified,
  SEC compliant, FINRA compliant, or approved by a regulator.
- Todox autonomously gives financial advice, recommends trades, executes
  trades, moves money, files regulated material, or sends binding client
  communications without review.
- Todox has zero hallucinations, proves every fact true, exposes model
  chain-of-thought, or makes a graph intrinsically trustworthy.
- Nothing leaves the device, every model runs locally, or the system is fully
  offline.
- Named CRM, custodian, email, calendar, document, or planning integrations
  work unless a later implementation packet proves them.
- The historical Claude artifacts contain real households, client metrics,
  balances, adoption data, or customer outcomes.

## Synthetic demonstration inventory

The canonical synthetic wealth fixture is safe to reinterpret as visibly
labeled demonstration material:

- a household cash need with a date;
- a client assumption about a taxable account;
- tax sensitivity expressed by the client;
- tasks to review liquidity, schedule a call, and confirm that no movement
  occurs before advisor review;
- a client-facing acknowledgement draft;
- a pending approval gate with source-span evidence;
- a bounded context packet that excludes custodian state, money movement, and
  an accepted recommendation.

The names and values in the fixture and Claude artifacts remain synthetic.
Fable may author new synthetic content at production fidelity, but it must label
the demonstration wherever a visitor could mistake it for a customer record.
