## Product and audience

This is a public category site for sophisticated wealth-management buyers who
already have CRMs, planning tools, document systems, compliance processes,
meeting technology, and an AI roadmap. It was researched against one related
account family with two distinct public voices, but the public site must name
or imply neither account and neither supplied individual.

Todox is a vision-only wealth-management concept. Repository evidence includes
a deterministic synthetic fixture and proposed runtime contracts; it does not
prove a deployed wealth product, live integrations, customer adoption, or
measured outcomes.

The primary action is **Request a private walkthrough**. Do not invent a paid
pilot, product signup, pricing plan, trial, customer logo, testimonial, or
deployment claim.

## Flagship narrative

Center one story: a living, source-linked client index becomes a reviewable
meeting-preparation packet.

The product proof may show, using original synthetic data:

- an approved source and exact supporting span;
- a new candidate fact, task, or preparation item;
- current versus superseded client intent;
- accept, edit, or reject review by an advisor or compliance role;
- a meeting-preparation assertion opening its source;
- an activity receipt naming source, actor, model or tool, policy, time, and
  review state;
- an uncertain or wrong model output remaining a candidate rather than silently
  entering the record.

Review records a scoped human disposition; it does not make a proposition
universally true. Existing systems of record remain authoritative for their
own data. The story ends before a client send, autonomous recommendation,
trade, money movement, tax conclusion, or system-of-record write.

## Positioning territory

Explore how to make **control, evidence, and ledgers** tangible:

- firm-controlled data and action authority;
- policy-approved model calls with bounded context;
- versioned source spans and supersession;
- named, scoped action authorization;
- an execution record that stays attached to the work;
- infrastructure around the firm's existing tools instead of another capture
  bot.

These are intended mechanisms and hypotheses, not proven unique capabilities.
Do not lead with abstract labels such as local-first, explainable, agentic,
knowledge graph, or human in the loop unless the page immediately shows the
specific behavior.

## Competitive constraints

The market already occupies:

- meeting capture, notes, summaries, CRM writeback, and follow-up;
- AI OS, copilot, agentic workforce, and time-saved promises;
- privacy-first, no recording, integrations, citations, and audit logs;
- human review, MCP, explainability, customer-cloud, on-prem, and customer-key
  language.

Do not claim Todox is the only or first provider of any of those. Do not frame
the page as “more than a notetaker”; competitors already use that move. Make
the difference legible through the chosen narrative and product behavior.

## Claim gate

Every factual public assertion must resolve to a record in `CLAIMS.jsonl` with
`publicEligible: true`, and its caveat must survive the rewrite. A claim can
still be unsuitable if the source record restricts its use.

Use concept language where required: “designed to,” “the proposed runtime,”
“could,” “the concept,” or an explicit synthetic-demonstration label.

Never claim:

- deployed, generally available, production-ready, or pilot-ready;
- use, approval, endorsement, or validation by a named wealth firm or person;
- live CRM, custodian, email, calendar, document, portfolio, or MCP
  integrations;
- autonomous advice, tax conclusions, trading, money movement, or unsupervised
  client communication;
- SOC 2 or other certification, regulator approval, examination readiness,
  zero retention, or absolute privacy;
- no hallucinations, every fact is true, every answer is cited, full
  explainability, or nothing leaves the device;
- a time-saving, AUM, adoption, performance, ROI, or customer-result metric.

All demonstrations must be visibly synthetic wherever a reasonable visitor
could mistake them for client data, a live system, or a measured result.
