different, and what to do — without the page ever claiming deployment.

## 3. Selected direction: Terminal of Record

Human-locked 2026-08-27. Alternates on record: The Working Paper (Impeccable's
pick — audit working papers on eye-ease columnar green), The Provenance

  phosphor on near-black CRT ground for the live mechanism; tractor-feed

  disclosures. One world, two materials, natural light/dark pacing. Light

  the discipline that keeps it out of "AI command center" territory.
