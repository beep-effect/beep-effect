# Advisor, executive, and client voice corpus

This file extracts jobs, tensions, and language patterns from public sources.
It does not manufacture a Todox customer story. All named-account evidence is
sales-only; Fable receives de-identified market tensions, not endorsements.

## Evidence status

- Public advisor stories exist primarily on Mariner's recruiting site and are
  marked for investment-professional use.
- Public executive comments exist in newsroom, interview, podcast, and joint
  vendor/customer material.
- AdvicePeriod publishes named ultra-affluent client testimonials with an
  explicit disclaimer.
- No named end-client testimonial hub was found on the Mariner corporate site.
- No public attributable voice was found for either user-supplied individual.

## Advisor story records

### Building the specialist bench

- **Context:** An advisor spent years assembling tax, estate, investment, and
  marketing support before joining a scaled firm.
- **Job:** Give clients coordinated expertise without rebuilding every
  specialty inside the practice.
- **Friction:** Operating leverage and transition visibility.
- **Evidence:** [Mariner transition story](https://joinmariner.com/success-stories/the-mariner-difference-transitioning-with-confidence/).
- **Status:** First-party recruiting story; investment-professional use only.
- **Todox use:** Sales discovery about source-backed handoffs. Not public copy
  and not proof that Todox solves the transition.

### Transition work needs shared status

- **Context:** An advisor transition required visibility into each client's
  status when the normal transfer path was unavailable and staffing changed.
- **Job:** Keep the work, origin, owner, and next decision visible through a
  handoff.
- **Friction:** Client-by-client tracking under operational stress.
- **Evidence:** Same Mariner transition story.
- **Status:** Sales-only advisor evidence.
- **Todox use:** A source-linked work-origin hypothesis, not a testimonial.

### Scale without rebuilding the wheel

- **Context:** An advisory firm sought operational, compliance, marketing,
  estate, tax, and investment support as it grew and planned succession.
- **Job:** Preserve client service while adding specialist capacity.
- **Friction:** The difference between scale and a disconnected vendor stack.
- **Evidence:** [Ocean Heights story](https://joinmariner.com/success-stories/ocean-heights-advisors-strategic-succession-to-mariner/).
- **Status:** First-party recruiting story; sales-only.
- **Todox use:** Discovery about continuity across specialists and systems.

### Expertise a small team cannot carry alone

- **Context:** An independent practice values access to a larger specialist
  bench.
- **Job:** Bring broader expertise into the client relationship without
  surrendering the advisor's role.
- **Evidence:** [Wealth With Options story](https://joinmariner.com/success-stories/pathways-to-success-wealth-with-options/).
- **Status:** First-party recruiting story; sales-only.
- **Todox use:** Supports a “context for judgment” question, not an automation
  claim.

## Executive voice records

### Capacity is the declared strategic problem

- **Context:** A joint 2026 announcement frames the traditional growth model as
  adding people as client count grows.
- **Job:** Move repeatable work away from associates so people can spend time on
  relationships and advice.
- **Evidence:** [Joint Mariner/Humanity Labs announcement](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260714748378/en/Mariner-and-Humanity-Labs-Announce-Five-Year-Partnership-to-Leverage-an-AI-Workforce-of-700-FTEs).
- **Status:** Joint vendor/customer source claim, not independent outcome proof.
- **Todox use:** Forces a non-overlapping pitch. Generic automation is already
  claimed.

### Quality assurance remains human work

- **Context:** The same announcement describes new roles directing and checking
  an AI workforce.
- **Job:** Preserve accountability while changing who or what performs
  repeatable work.
- **Status:** Source claim; sales-only.
- **Todox use:** Discovery about named review, policy, and activity receipts.

### Follow-up and integrations are old, persistent themes

- **Context:** A public advisor interview described the craft as enjoyable and
  the follow-up work as painful, while treating APIs as the connective tissue
  between systems.
- **Evidence:** [Kitces Financial Advisor Success interview](https://www.kitces.com/blog/steve-lockshin-advice-period-podcast-financial-advisor-serial-entrepreneur-ultra-hnw-clients/).
- **Status:** Opened interview, historical context.
- **Todox use:** A discovery prompt. It does not prove the same problem remains
  unsolved after current tooling and partnerships.

## End-client story patterns

AdvicePeriod's [ultra-affluent page](https://www.adviceperiod.com/ultra-affluent/)
publishes named current-client testimonials. The page says the clients were not
compensated, may not represent other clients, and do not guarantee future
results. Preserve that context whenever reviewing the source.

The de-identified patterns are:

- major family-office decisions cross investment, estate, tax, and lifestyle
  boundaries;
- proactive detail can matter as much as investment oversight;
- business sales and family changes create simultaneous planning, tax, estate,
  and investment work;
- one household may expect one call to coordinate real estate, investments,
  estate matters, reporting, and bill pay;
- large families, operating businesses, and many entities intensify task and
  information ownership.

These patterns may inform discovery and synthetic scenarios. They are not
Todox results, representative market statistics, or permission to reuse names,
quotes, photographs, or stories on the site.

## De-identified market tensions for Fable

- Advisors want specialist leverage without losing responsibility for the
  relationship.
- Complex households create context across people, entities, documents, goals,
  and outstanding work.
- A task title is not enough; delegates need the source and reason the work
  exists.
- Transitions and acquisitions increase the cost of undocumented local
  knowledge.
- Follow-up and reconstruction can be less valuable than judgment but consume
  the same professional attention.
- Security and supervision are product behavior, not a badge row.
- A new AI product must fit around an installed AI, CRM, planning, document,
  and operations stack.
