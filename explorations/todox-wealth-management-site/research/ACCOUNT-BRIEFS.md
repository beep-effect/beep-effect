## Relationship model

AdvicePeriod is not best described as a separate current RIA or simply as a
“child company.” Current first-party disclosure describes AdvicePeriod as a
business name and brand used by Mariner, LLC and Mariner Platform Solutions,
LLC. Regulatory records also show the historical AdvicePeriod, LLC registration
ended in 2022.

The account should therefore be approached as one organizational family with
different public voices, entity and employment models, and buying centers:

- Mariner speaks as an integrated national wealth firm with a broad specialist
  bench, an acquisition engine, and centralized operating scale.
- AdvicePeriod retains a boutique, ultra-affluent, technology-forward voice and
  a Los Angeles operating context.
- Mariner Platform Solutions supports an independent-contractor path alongside
  the employee-model adviser path.

Do not flatten those relationships into one legal entity or assume one buyer
controls every surface.

## Account-specific conversation frame

### Opening premise

The organization does not need another claim that AI can summarize a meeting
or automate back-office work. The narrower question is whether an advisor can
inspect the source, history, policy, and authorization behind the context that
survives from one client interaction to the next.

### Discovery sequence

1. What work remains on the advisor's desk after the announced AI-workforce
   program and existing specialist tools?
2. How is a client or household brief assembled before a meeting today?
3. Where is outstanding work tracked when it originates in email, a document,
   a prior meeting, or a specialist handoff?
4. When evidence conflicts or a client's intent changes, how does the firm
   preserve what was superseded?
5. What must a reviewer see before a candidate fact, task, draft, or external
   action becomes usable?
6. Which system remains authoritative for each record, and how are write
   permissions granted?
7. Which model may receive which data class for which purpose?
8. What evidence would justify one controlled proof without inventing an ROI
   target?

### Plausible first proof, still a hypothesis

A synthetic, read-only client-index-to-meeting-prep workflow:

- approved source artifacts only;
- stable source spans and versions;
- candidate facts, work, and draft questions;
- current versus superseded intent;
- named review disposition;
- no system-of-record write, money movement, advice, or client send;
- an execution receipt naming model, tool, actor, policy, and cost where used.

This is a discovery artifact, not a pilot offer or claim of readiness.
