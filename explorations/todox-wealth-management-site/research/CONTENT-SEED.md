## Plain-language position

Todox.ai is a product direction for a firm-controlled runtime around advisor
work. It is meant to turn approved source material into candidate claims,
tasks, drafts, and a reviewable meeting-preparation packet while existing
systems remain authoritative for the records they own.

The useful distinction is not “more AI.” It is controlled continuity:

- source spans instead of untraceable summary prose;
- candidates instead of silent writes;
- advisor or policy review before consequential use;
- current and superseded intent instead of overwrite;
- actor, source, model or tool, time, and disposition in the activity record;
- firm-controlled data and action authority with bounded, approved model calls.

3. The system marks money movement, advice, and client communication as review

## Audience lenses

### Advisor

Job: arrive knowing what changed, what matters, and where each point came from.

Language candidates:

- Spend the first minute with the client, not reconstructing the file.
- Open the sentence behind the task.
- See what is in your court before it becomes an inbox search.

### Operations leader

Job: maintain continuity, clean handoffs, and visible work state across people
and systems.

Language candidates:

- Give the delegate the origin of the work, not a copied task title.
- Keep duplicate and superseded work visible instead of silently merging it.
- See where the workflow stopped and what decision it needs.

### Compliance or supervision

Job: reconstruct what was asserted, evidenced, proposed, reviewed, and
authorized.

Language candidates:

- Review the source and disposition together.
- Preserve the difference between evidence, inference, and approval.
- Trace the action without claiming access to hidden model reasoning.

Do not say `compliant`, `examination-ready`, or name a regulatory standard as a
Todox capability.

### Executive or innovation leader

Job: introduce useful model assistance without surrendering firm authority or
replacing the core stack.

Language candidates:

- Keep systems of record in place. Add a controlled runtime around the work.
- Define which model receives which context under which policy.
- Start with one reviewable workflow, not an enterprise transformation claim.

## Objection-response bank

### “Is this another meeting recorder?”

> No. The concept begins with source-linked client context before the meeting
> and preserves reviewable work after it. Recording may be one future source;
> it is not the product category.

### “Are you replacing our CRM or custodian?”

> No. Those systems remain authoritative for the records they own. Todox is
> being shaped to coordinate context, proposed work, review, and provenance
> around them.

### “Does local-first mean nothing leaves the machine?”

> No absolute claim. The design goal is firm-controlled data and action
> authority, with bounded context sent only to approved models under policy.

### “Does the advisor have to trust a black-box answer?”

> The target experience keeps the source span, candidate state, actor, tool or
> model, time, and review disposition inspectable. It does not promise hidden
> chain-of-thought or that a citation makes the underlying claim true.

### “Can it act for the advisor?”

> Only within a future firm-defined policy and authorization boundary.
> Professional advice, recommendations, money movement, trades, tax decisions,
> and unsupervised client communications remain outside the demonstrated
> workflow.

### “Which integrations work today?”

> The current wealth demonstration is a synthetic contract, not a deployed
> connector suite. Discuss desired systems in a private walkthrough; do not
> publish integration logos or availability claims yet.
