# Research — Todox.ai wealth-management sales site

Captured 2026-08-27. This pass assembled a source-backed creative and sales
corpus for Claude Fable. It did not design or implement the site.

## Executive synthesis

The useful opening is not another wealth notetaker, copilot, AI operating
system, document analyzer, or generic automation workforce. Those positions
are already crowded, and the researched account family has publicly announced
or discussed programs in several of them.

The narrower Todox concept is a firm-controlled runtime for work that must
remain inspectable across client interactions:

```text
approved source + stable source span
  -> candidate client context and work
  -> advisor or compliance review
  -> scoped disposition and authorization
  -> reviewable meeting-preparation packet
  -> activity record with source, actor, model or tool, policy, and time
```

This is a product direction, not a shipped capability statement. The
repository's executable wealth evidence is a deterministic synthetic fixture,
while law remains the sole active vertical. That distinction governs every
copy candidate in this packet.

## Jobs and tensions worth carrying forward

The public and private sources converge on a useful set of questions without
proving that any named firm currently fails at them:

- How does an advisor assemble current household context before a meeting?
- Can a delegate see the source and reason behind an outstanding task?
- What changed since the last interaction, and what did the new information
  supersede?
- Which system is authoritative for each record?
- Which candidate facts, drafts, and actions require a named reviewer?
- Which model may receive which client-data class, for which purpose, under
  which policy?
- Can the firm reconstruct the source, actor, model or tool, credential class,
  approval, disposition, time, and cost behind a work product?
- Does local knowledge survive advisor absence, specialist handoffs,
  acquisition integration, and firm transitions?

De-identified advisor, executive, and client-language patterns are in

published evidence from a scenario hypothesis.

## Competitive landscape

Twenty direct and adjacent vendors were reviewed across advisor meeting AI,
planning and analysis, CRM or platform AI, document operations, and private or
customer-controlled AI. The full comparison and dated pricing snapshot are in
[`COMPETITIVE-POSITIONING.md`](./research/COMPETITIVE-POSITIONING.md).

The following language is already saturated and cannot carry the position by
itself:

- AI OS, copilot, or agentic workforce;
- meeting capture, notes, CRM writeback, and follow-up automation;
- privacy-first, no recording, integrations, or time saved;
- human in the loop, citations, audit logs, MCP, or explainability;
- local, on-prem, customer-controlled, or customer-key deployment.

Three close collisions matter most:

- Zocks already publishes transcript references, MCP feature gates, and audit
  logs.
- Practifi already markets write-and-cite behavior, team controls, agent
  governance, and MCP on a wealth CRM substrate.
- Clarista already claims customer-cloud or on-prem deployment, customer keys,
  governed MCP, lineage, audit, and sourced or reproducible answers.

The researched account family also has an announced multi-year AI-workforce
program covering broad operating workflows and a public estate-document AI
story. Todox therefore cannot arrive as generic back-office automation,
document OCR, or innovation theater.

## Differentiation hypotheses

The combined corpus leaves a possible position around **control, evidence, and
ledgers**. Its parts remain hypotheses until the product implements and proves
them:

1. policy admission before a model call, including purpose, data class, model,
   tool, credential class, and spend boundary;
2. versioned source-span evidence attached to candidate work;
3. explicit supersession rather than silent overwrite;
4. action authorization as a named, scoped record;
5. an execution ledger attached to the work product;
6. infrastructure around the firm's installed tools, not another capture bot;
7. an advisor-specific proof surface: a source-linked client index becoming a
   reviewable meeting-preparation packet.

Neither “local-first” nor “explainable” is self-explanatory. In this packet,
local-first means firm-controlled data and action authority with bounded,
policy-approved model calls. Explainable means source provenance, actor and
action history, model or tool attribution, review state, and supersession. It
never means exposing hidden chain-of-thought or guaranteeing truth.

## Repository capability truth

Repository authority outranks historical demos, private notes, and competitor
language.

### Existing evidence

- One active agentic professional-runtime specification.
- A deterministic, package-contract-tested synthetic wealth fixture that
  produces candidate claims, tasks, a draft, a pending approval gate, and a
  bounded context packet.
- Approval and autonomy policy documents.
- A proposed wealth data-model overlay including meeting preparation and
  supersession.
- Live graph and provenance pieces alongside material specified and net-new
  graph work.
- An existing, minimal Next.js `apps/todox` workspace.

### What that evidence does not prove

- A deployed or generally available wealth product.
- Live CRM, email, calendar, custodian, document, portfolio, or MCP
  integrations.
- A production knowledge graph, GraphRAG system, or complete explainability
  engine.
- Autonomous advice, trades, money movement, tax conclusions, or unsupervised
  client communication.
- Compliance certification, customer adoption, a paid pilot, performance, or
  ROI.

## Research outcome

The evidence supports one focused later narrative: a synthetic living client
index becomes a reviewable meeting-preparation packet, with visible source,
change, candidate, review, authorization, and activity states. The primary
action can invite a private walkthrough of the concept.

It does not yet support an implementation goal. Claude Fable must first run
Impeccable `init`, curate original candidate directions during `shape`, and
return the human-locked direction and resulting brief for confirmation. Copy
reconciliation and implementation preparation follow during `decompose`.

## Remaining unknowns

- Which Fable-curated visual and verbal direction the human will lock.
- Which product mechanisms will be implemented before any public present-tense

- Which buyer and deployment boundary a real discovery conversation validates.

Those are downstream discovery and product questions. They do not justify more
