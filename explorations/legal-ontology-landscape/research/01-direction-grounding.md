## Where the project is heading

The direction is a legal system of record where schema-first domain records, evidence spans, provenance, and EventLog-style authority remain the source of truth, while graph views are rebuildable read models. The IP-law KG packet originally proposed a 7-ontology OWL survey, Effect Schema node/edge definitions, FalkorDB/Cypher storage, and seed data for patent, trademark, and copyright scenarios (`goals/ip-law-knowledge-graph/SPEC.md`), but its own grounding note warns that FalkorDB must be reconciled with the rule that claim + evidence + provenance + lifecycle are authoritative and graph views are projections (`goals/ip-law-knowledge-graph/research/ontology-grounding-corpus.md`). BEEPGRAPH resolves that direction as an Effect-native, ontology-guided, provenance-first extraction spine whose accepted outputs project into graph/retrieval shells, with EventLog mutation history and evidence/provenance as authority (`docs/BEEPGRAPH_ARCHITECTURE.md`).

The newer legal-document-intake packet makes this concrete for the practice: first DMS is Box, local vault is canonical, sync is one-way push, taxonomy is a repo-owned SKOS-style seed with concept IRIs aligned to FOLIO where available, and KG storage is a Postgres/PGlite projection rather than FalkorDB or any dedicated graph DB in v1 (`goals/legal-document-intake/SPEC.md`). Its acceptance target is not an abstract ontology demo: filed documents must yield span-grounded extractions, admitted claims must carry DMS links and source span provenance, and natural-language questions must open the source document at the highlighted span (`goals/legal-document-intake/SPEC.md`).

For ontology research, this means FOLIO is the legal-practice backbone, not the whole IP layer: the grounding corpus says FOLIO supplies broad matters, actors, and documents vocabulary but is shallow on patent/trademark practice areas (`goals/ip-law-knowledge-graph/research/ontology-grounding-corpus.md`). The exploration capture adds the operational target: this research is aimed at the Aug 5 `oip-web` first-user metric, with third-party ontology assets kept under the packet's gitignored vendor area and committed artifacts limited to manifests, scripts, and reports (`explorations/legal-ontology-landscape/CAPTURE.md`). Later phases should therefore choose vocabularies by competency question, align DMS/file/email metadata and IP doctrine to the system of record, and treat RDF/OWL/SHACL outputs as projections, validation aids, or agent grounding surfaces rather than a replacement authority (`explorations/legal-ontology-landscape/research/00-source-brief.md`; `docs/BEEPGRAPH_ARCHITECTURE.md`).

## Competency questions

1. [P1][P2] For each matter, what office-action response deadlines are open, extended, final, or missed, and which application, office-action document, rule/source event, and docket record support the date?
2. [P1] Which email threads, letters, attachments, and portal downloads belong to a given client, matter, patent application, trademark application, or office-action response, and what DMS links prove the linkage?
3. [P1] Where should this incoming document file in the local vault and Box mirror taxonomy, and which taxonomy concept, matter, client, document type, and lifecycle status justified that path?
4. [P2] What is the chain of title for an IP asset across assignments, mergers, security interests, and recorded releases, and which recorded instruments or correspondence prove each ownership change?
5. [P2] Which inventors, applicants, assignees, owners, attorneys, law-firm contacts, examiners, and clients are the same real-world party across matters, and which identifiers or evidence justify the merge or separation?
6. [P2] Which prior-art references recur across matters or office actions, which claims and rejection statutes do they support, and have they already been distinguished in another matter?
7. [P2] For patent matters, what maintenance-fee, annuity, IDS, RCE, appeal, continuation, and national-stage docket obligations exist, and what authority or source document created each obligation?
8. [P2] For trademark matters, what application, office-action, statement-of-use, opposition, section 8/9/15, renewal, and maintenance obligations exist; current domain vocabulary must explicitly account for the missing `TrademarkAsset` entity (`packages/law-practice/domain/src/entities/index.ts`).
9. [P2] Which goods/services and mark records use Nice, Vienna, or other trademark classifications, and how should those classifications attach once a `TrademarkAsset` model exists (`packages/law-practice/domain/src/entities/index.ts`)?
10. [P2] Which patent assets, applications, claims, and references carry IPC or CPC classifications, what scheme edition do those codes come from, and what broader/narrower lookup should the attorney see?
11. [P1] Is this document a draft, redline, filed copy, received copy, client-provided copy, or extracted child artifact, and how does it version against earlier copies in the vault and Box mirror?
12. [P3] Which extracted facts have confidence values, exact evidence spans, matched text, source document coordinates, and claim-gate verdicts, and which facts failed because their spans were unaligned?
13. [P3] From an office-action review packet, which claims, rejection grounds, cited prior art, and applicant distinctions were extracted, and which source spans anchor each law-practice entity?
14. [P2][P3] Which claim limitations are argued as missing from a cited reference, and which office-action response passages support the distinction against the rejected claim?
15. [P1] Which source files have digest, origin path, salvage provenance, recycle-bin restoration, extraction manifest, and Box sync state sufficient to audit custody from raw file to filed DMS location?
16. [P4] Can every graph edge used by search or agent answers be rebuilt from accepted claims, EventLog/provenance records, DMS links, and evidence spans rather than from a graph store as authority?
17. [P4] Which questions should be answered through Postgres/PGlite projection, which through RDF/SPARQL sidecars, which through bounded SHACL validation, and which require a real semantic engine not yet wired?
18. [P2] Which legal roles are enduring party identities versus time-bounded roles, such as inventor, applicant, assignee, owner, client contact, signer, prosecutor, examiner, licensee, or licensor?
19. [P1][P2] Which incoming documents are privileged correspondence, office correspondence, filed prosecution papers, assignment records, disclosure materials, prior art, or billing/admin records, and what retention or access policy follows from that class?
20. [P4] For each accepted external ontology or vocabulary term, should the project adopt it directly, slice it, map to it, or reject it, and what competency question and repo authority record justify that decision?

## Known gaps

- No `TrademarkAsset` entity exists in the law-practice entity export surface, while the IP-law KG spec requires trademark seed scenarios and trademark node types; trademark docketing and Nice-classification CQs therefore require vocabulary and domain-model work before implementation (`packages/law-practice/domain/src/entities/index.ts`; `goals/ip-law-knowledge-graph/SPEC.md`).
- SPARQL is not wired to a real engine: the semantic-web package exposes a SPARQL request/result/service contract and an `UnsupportedSparqlQueryServiceLive` layer that fails with `unimplemented` (`packages/foundation/capability/semantic-web/src/services/sparql-query.ts`).
- SHACL is bounded, not a full external engine: the contract documents a bounded subset, and the live adapter checks targetClass, minCount, maxCount, and datatype in local code (`packages/foundation/capability/semantic-web/src/services/shacl-validation.ts`; `packages/foundation/capability/semantic-web/src/adapters/shacl-engine.ts`).
- Storage doctrine is split across packets: the IP-law KG spec still names FalkorDB storage and Cypher as implementation choices, while legal-document-intake resolves KG storage as a Postgres/PGlite projection and excludes dedicated graph DBs for v1 (`goals/ip-law-knowledge-graph/SPEC.md`; `goals/legal-document-intake/SPEC.md`; `docs/BEEPGRAPH_ARCHITECTURE.md`).
- The `documents` product slice that should own Document, FilingDecision, SyncState, and IntakeBatch language is net-new, so the DMS ontology cannot assume an existing document-domain authority yet (`goals/legal-document-intake/SPEC.md`).
- `@beep/ontology` is currently FOLIO/OpenAPI-model oriented, while BEEPGRAPH lists Effect Schema ontology authoring/projection as specced or blocked rather than live; later phases should build on the FOLIO models without mistaking them for a complete ontology authoring substrate (`packages/foundation/modeling/ontology/src/Ontology.models.ts`; `docs/BEEPGRAPH_ARCHITECTURE.md`).
- GraphRAG explainability DAGs, Knowledge Cores, separated graph/document embeddings, and the EO-style monoid-fold extraction kernel are named as net-new grafts in BEEPGRAPH, not live repo capabilities (`docs/BEEPGRAPH_ARCHITECTURE.md`).
- Full corpus ingestion is out of scope for the IP-law KG spec, while legal-document-intake requires filed documents to be parsed, extracted, admitted through the librarian/critic/gate loop, and opened at exact spans; research phases must separate the existing corpus CLI from the future product intake surface (`goals/ip-law-knowledge-graph/SPEC.md`; `goals/legal-document-intake/SPEC.md`; `packages/tooling/tool/cli/src/commands/Corpus/Corpus.service.ts`).
