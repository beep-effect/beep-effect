- Across the three sampled journals (179 records), the only event kinds were `attempt-started` and `attempt-finished`; all envelopes were `yeet-attempt-journal/v1`, and sampled finishes embedded `yeet-verdict/v2`.
- Sampled finishes carried the claimed `elapsedMs`, `failedStepId`, `failureKind`, and verdict `mode`; sampled starts carried top-level `mode`, and paired modes agreed.
- The probe counts each sampled terminal attempt once: the three samples had no duplicate finished `attemptId` and no orphan finish, although five starts had no finish.

- The current exact lock sentence is detected in the lock-heavy sample; the warning above concerns its unversioned prose dependency, not a sampled false negative.
- All sampled timestamps used canonical UTC `Z` form, so the raw-string sort happened to agree with chronological order in this sample.
- The v0 all-mode choice, local-green tier boundary, and missing epoch qualification are disclosed at baseline:13-14 and :103-113; no separate finding was added for those documented divergences.
