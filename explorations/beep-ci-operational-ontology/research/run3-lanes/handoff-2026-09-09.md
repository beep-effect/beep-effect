## Gotchas learned tonight (all in memory)

- Lane briefs must require the cheap gates on new TS files: `beep lint schema-first` (schema-heavy
  specs need `S.toArbitrary` + `fcRuns`), `fallow audit --check`, `fallow health --check`.
- `--start-pr-early` refuses `--amend`; restage an unpushed commit with `git reset --soft HEAD~1`.
- Graft rewrites `.claude/settings.json` and `.claude/helpers/graft-*.cjs` in every lane
  worktree; `git checkout --` them before publishing. `-c mcp_servers.graft.enabled=false` is
  ineffective.
- Codex sandbox add-dirs show phantom `.agents/.codex/.git` entries; empty-target exports run from
  the Bash tool. A lane can exit mid-turn with empty stdout; `codex exec … resume <session id>`
  under a new unit continues it.
- Never gate a chain on `cmd | tail | grep -q`; capture the verdict and exit explicitly.
- Single-job reruns copy untouched jobs into a new attempt with new ids; rerun by the latest
  attempt's id, or `--failed` once when every red is an attributed flake.
