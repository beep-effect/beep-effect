# Review-validity audit — ontology-foundational-auditor run 2 (`beep-ci-ops`)

This report audits the adversary seat rather than re-running it. It covers all 45 review files and all 100 attacks whose recorded outcome is `landed`; survived attacks are not re-adjudicated. The review-verdict column reproduces the adversary's verdict and is not itself a replacement review disposition.

The audit applies the ratified run-1 precedents as binding constraints:

- measurements are recorded values and therefore information objects; a DTO-shaped carrier does not defeat an information-object proposal unless the proposal adds unsupported identity, issuance, custody, cross-record unity, or a world-side referent;
- `SeatRequest` is the queued-ticket record and `SeatGrant` is the lease record; a transition/lifecycle may relate them but may not merge their identities;
- `WorkUnitSpecification` and `WorkUnitExecution` remain distinct;
- a run-1 concession means undefendable from the current evidence, not false.

`DEMONSTRATED` means the stated counterexample actually defeats, or exposes an evidentiary gap in, the cited proposal chain. `INVALID` means the stated attack misreads the proposal, CQ, identity card, observation, or ratified taxonomy. No new attack is introduced here.
