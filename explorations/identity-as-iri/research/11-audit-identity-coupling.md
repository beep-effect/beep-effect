- The public barrel shape must preserve `export * from "./Id.ts"` and `export * from "./packages.ts"` so consumers can import core types/functions and generated composer constants from `@beep/identity`. (packages/foundation/modeling/identity/src/index.ts:31, packages/foundation/modeling/identity/src/index.ts:45)
- The package export map must preserve the root export, wildcard export, and `./packages` subpath because `@beep/identity` and `@beep/identity/packages` are both consumed. (packages/foundation/modeling/identity/package.json:35-40, packages/tooling/tool/cli/src/commands/CreatePackage/Handler.ts:14, packages/drivers/acp/src/AcpProtocol.service.ts:8)

(packages/foundation/modeling/identity/src/Id.ts:1201-1215, packages/foundation/modeling/identity/src/packages.ts:40)
