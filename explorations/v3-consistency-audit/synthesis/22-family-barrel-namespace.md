# 22 — Family cross-check: barrels & namespaces

Scope: `index.ts` per level, `export * as <Concept>` vs flat re-export, barrel completeness, `package.json` exports subpath grammar, `public/server/test` entrypoints. v3 = `~/YeeBois/projects/beep-effect4/packages/{iam,knowledge}` (10 packages, 1478 src files, 347 `index.ts`). v4 = `~/YeeBois/projects/beep-effect7/packages/{agents,architecture-lab,documents,epistemic,law-practice,ontology,shared,workspace}` (40 packages, 948 src files, 327 `index.ts`). Every count below was re-run for this file; commands are in the evidence columns. `node_modules`, `dist`, `coverage`, `.turbo` excluded throughout.

## TL;DR

1. v3's one real barrel law, an `index.ts` in almost every source directory (347/350) with `export * as <Concept>` at the tier barrel (84/84 entity folders), survives in v4 only at the concept level (253/254 concept dirs have `index.ts`). At the kind level v4 splits 19 namespace-only, 8 flat-only, 1 mixed, and 1 named-only, with no doctrine sentence choosing among them.
2. Where v4 is uniform in this family it is v4-only or doctrine-backed: explicit boundary subpaths (`/public` and `/server` 8/8; config five 3/3), no root `./*` wildcard in 38/40 packages, `./internal/*: null` in 33/40, and `.ts`/`.tsx` on 785/785 barrel specifiers. v3 used extensions on 194/1000 barrel specifiers, so the extension habit existed but was not uniform. The v3 `"./*": "./src/*.ts"` wildcard and its shim files must not be ported: `standards/architecture/DECISIONS.md` L408-432 makes roots and wildcards transitional rather than canonical.
3. No repo-wide hard check validates this family. `beep lint schema-topology` is pinned to `packages/foundation/modeling/schema`; `TsconfigSync` reads export maps to derive aliases but does not validate the architecture contract. The Architecture generator does supply defaults for `./internal/*: null`, role subpaths, root barrels, and `VERSION`. `standards/ARCHITECTURE.md` L1934 still lists export-map and import-boundary checks as future automation.
