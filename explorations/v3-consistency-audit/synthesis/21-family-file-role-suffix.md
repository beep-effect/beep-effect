# 21: family file-role-suffix

Cross-check of the `<Concept>.<role>.ts` taxonomy: per-tier suffix vocabulary,
folder/file prefix agreement, off-vocabulary suffixes, single-segment files.
v3 = `~/YeeBois/projects/beep-effect4/packages/{iam,knowledge}` (1478 src / 347
`index.ts`); v4 = `~/YeeBois/projects/beep-effect7/packages/{agents,architecture-lab,documents,epistemic,law-practice,ontology,shared,workspace}`

## TL;DR

- v3 had no written vocabulary but a copy-propagated one: 39/39 persisted concepts carry the same 7 semantic roles (`.model .entity .errors .http .rpc .tool .repo`), but only 38/39 folders carry the exact `<folder>/<folder>.<role>.ts` kit because `Agent/` contains `KnowledgeAgent.*`. Suffixed files agree with their folder 317/324 (the 7 misses are that one folder). Outside the domain tier v3 abandoned suffixes: knowledge server 113/135 unsuffixed, and 446 of iam client's 455 single-segment files use the fixed names `mod/contract/handler/layer/service.ts`.
- v4 wrote the vocabulary (ARCHITECTURE.md L969-1127, DECISIONS 2026-04-21) and code follows it structurally (prefix==folder 403/432 = 93.3%, `.model.ts` in 146/152 domain concept dirs, `.table.ts` 32/32 PascalCase, `.layer.ts` 21/22 tier-correct). Lexically, 335/432 = 77.5% of suffixed files use a tier-correct doctrine suffix, including four valid server `.<port-name>.ts` implementations. There are 67 distinct observed suffixes; 41 names are outside the doctrine vocabulary, and 97 files are nonconforming after wrong-tier uses are added. The largest repeat drifts (`.converters` 25, `.repository` 7 vs `.ports` 12, `.http/.rpc/.tools` handlers vs `-handlers`) are either undocumented habits or emitted by the canonical generator itself (`AcceptedProofManifest.ts` L293-445).
- Enforcement today is zero for slices: the only filename gate is `pascal-case-file` scoped to `packages/tooling/tool/cli/src` (Lint.command.ts L37, L275-283); `beep architecture check` compares one plan JSON to disk; 13-onboarding L155 points at a "hard-check lane in 07-non-slice-families.md" that contains no such lane (`rg hard-check 07-*` = 0).

## Notes

1. **v3 uniformity was tier-local, not slice-wide.** The 100% figures (frs-04, frs-05, frs-06, frs-07, frs-10) all live in the domain tier plus server `.repo.ts`; frs-01 is 97.8% because `Agent/KnowledgeAgent.*` breaks prefix agreement. The same two slices hold 608 single-segment files, a client tier that encodes the role in fixed filenames (`mod.ts`, `contract.ts`, `handler.ts`) instead of suffixes, and a server tier where 83.7% of knowledge files have no suffix. "v3 wins on uniformity" is true of the semantic domain 7-role kit and of `.repo/.table/.errors` spelling, not of the suffix family as a whole.
2. **The v3 7-role kit is scaffold output, not authored discipline.** knowledge's 14/22 empty `RpcGroup.make()` / `AiToolkit.make()` shells (frs-07) show the kit was generated and left in place. v4's "canonical when the role exists" (ARCHITECTURE.md L162-163) is a deliberate rejection of placeholder role files; a v4 gate should therefore check *spelling and tier of files that exist*, never presence of the full kit.
3. **v4 has two vocabularies for the same tier, and the generator votes for the wrong one.** Four contested spelling families (`.repository` vs `.ports`, `.use-cases` vs `.service`, `.http/.rpc/.tools` vs `-handlers` in server, `.view-model` / `.client`) account for 9 doctrine-divergent manifest paths at L293-639. Adding the lowercase config and tables composers at L507 and L551 makes 11 divergent manifest paths. Any vocabulary gate must be paired with a manifest fix or it will fail on every `beep architecture add concept` output.
4. **Off-vocabulary is mostly long tail.** The corrected fixed-plus-wildcard recount finds 41 off-vocabulary suffix names over 78 files; 35 of those names are singletons. Another 19 files use a known suffix in the wrong tier, bringing total nonconformance to 97/432. The repeatable off-vocabulary habits are `.converters` (25), `.repository` (7), `.use-cases` (4), `.models` (3), `.sparql` (2), and `.validation` (2); wrong-tier `.values` adds 4 files. The named-port wildcard covers four actual implementations, not `.fts`, `.host`, or `.emails`.
5. **Doctrine gaps this family exposes:** `.converters.ts` (25 files, 5 packages) has no doctrine row; the config concept vocabulary has no `.layer.ts` yet the lab ships one; per-concept `server.ts`/`worker.ts` shims occur 15 times without doctrine; 13-onboarding L155 cites a hard-check lane that does not exist; ARCHITECTURE L1926-1934 assigns lanes to 7 rule areas and none is role naming.
6. **Scout fact corrections (this family):** v3 `.contract` is 72 not 38; `.errors` 61 not 12; `.table` 41 not 23; `.repo` 78 not 76; `.model` 39 is missing from the scout list; "1163 no role infix" is 608 non-index single-segment files (955 including `index.ts`). v4 `.model` 148 not 118, `.values` 35 not 20, `.table` 32 not 16, `.converters` 25 not 17, `.layer` 22 not 15, `.errors` 21 not 11; "659 single-segment" is 189 non-index (516 with `index.ts`). Reader 11's "167 no suffix" for the knowledge server includes 54 `index.ts`; non-index it is 113/135.
7. **Not relitigated:** PascalCase concept stems (frs-19) and one-file-per-role (frs-09) are decided; v3's kebab `.table.ts` stems, kebab client/ui files and per-operation `.contract.ts` files are recorded as not-worth-porting.
