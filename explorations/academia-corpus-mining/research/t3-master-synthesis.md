# Master synthesis — Academia corpus mining (2026-07)

- **Date:** 2026-07-25  **Synthesist:** codex gpt-5.6-sol (max)
- **Input:** 7 cluster reports over 185 deep-read papers (of 443 triaged)

## Executive verdict

The corpus changes beep-effect less by selecting new technology than by imposing a stricter epistemic boundary. Exact artifacts, source spans, assertions, approval decisions, policy revisions, execution attempts, and observed outcomes can be authoritative records of what occurred or was recorded. Schema validity, retrieval rank, entailment, ontology consistency, attorney acceptance, and action authorization are separate predicates; none converts a contestable proposition into source truth. This requires correcting the “becomes a fact” and “authoritative runtime truth” language in `docs/product/prose-to-proof.md` and the professional-runtime approval policy.

The current narrow goals are largely validated. The bitemporal core, semantic-foundation M1, deterministic identity fold, candidate-only fusion core, exact verified-span substrate, local Office Action slice, and pre-prompt secret scrub should not be widened. The delta belongs mainly in explicit boundary fixtures and follow-ons: retained evidence plus preferred belief views, legal multitemporality, qualifier-aware claim-to-evidence assessment, visual/composite evidence anchors, ontology lifecycle QA, legal procedure and argumentation, and a trajectory-level agent control plane.

The evidence is strongest as architectural convergence and negative evidence: many implemented systems demonstrate ways that shape-valid, well-ranked, replayable, or human-accepted output can still be wrong. It is weak as production validation. No paper demonstrates the full target of privileged USPTO material, exact evidence, bitemporal correction, legal interpretation, attorney review, matter walls, and authorized multi-agent execution.

## The ten findings that matter most

1. **Verification, acceptance, authorization, and release must be independent verdicts.** **Evidence weight:** high—six-cluster convergence with formal boundaries and implemented failure cases, though little production legal validation. **Clusters:** memory-bitemporal; legal-ontology-design; legal-norms-reasoning; retrieval-citation-grounding; agent-metacognition-neurosymbolic; agent-security-orchestration. `36d82e899e75` (Cross: An OWL Wrapper for Reasoning on Relational) separates ontology consistency from source-record existence; `0421a1687b40` (ProVe) separates source support from truth; `703aea161905` (Argumentation and Standards of Proof) separates evidence from accepted conclusions; `aff0b53d4126` (AlignedCoT) shows correct answers with invalid reasoning; and `caefce8b35a2` (LLM Agents can Autonomously Exploit One-day Vulnerabilities) shows verified information can amplify harmful capability. **What to do:** model at least shape validity, anchor fidelity, claim-to-span stance, source authority/currentness, human disposition, action authorization, and release state separately. Correct product prose and approval policy before adding another publication gate.

2. **Exact episodic history and its semantic indexes are different memory layers.** **Evidence weight:** high architectural convergence; limited production and deletion-policy evidence. **Clusters:** memory-bitemporal; doc-structure-legal-nlp; agent-metacognition-neurosymbolic; agent-security-orchestration. `5c2aeef6919d` (Ontology Revision as Non-Prioritized Belief Revision) retains sourced assertions behind a disposable working view; `2ebef49a0b91` (Construction of Knowledge Graphs) requires provenance-preserving repair; `fc16fbd18148` (MemoryFlow) binds telemetry to exact versions; and `b9ac605c406e` (OTIP: A Falsifiable Anti-Goodhart Protocol) shows why hashes and summaries do not replace complete events. **What to do:** split the memory taxonomy’s episodic layer into an exact record corpus and bounded semantic/session projections. Apply compression, decay, consolidation, and score-driven pruning only to projections. Exact records may be removed only through explicit retention, access, legal-hold, or key-destruction policy with descendant invalidation—not semantic forgetting.

3. **A text-equal span is necessary but not a complete evidence model.** **Evidence weight:** high cross-paper convergence, but no USPTO Office Action validation. **Clusters:** retrieval-citation-grounding; doc-structure-legal-nlp; legal-norms-reasoning. `39933453659f` (DUDE), `517d7e937747` (A Framework for the Encoding of Multilayered Documents), and `3a636c39bbaa` (Kleister) show that tables, checkboxes, images, OCR, layout, and external values escape one character interval. `0421a1687b40` (ProVe) separately shows that a correctly located passage may not support the proposition. **What to do:** preserve the current UTF-16 raw-slice goal unchanged, then shape a typed artifact/page-region/composite-anchor extension carrying artifact revision, page, polygon, OCR-token mapping, engine version, and derivation lineage. Keep anchor fidelity distinct from field-role correctness, citation resolution, and claim support.

4. **The two-axis bitemporal core is necessary but not complete legal or operational time.** **Evidence weight:** high for distinguishing temporal meanings; medium for the complete legal model. **Clusters:** memory-bitemporal; legal-ontology-design; legal-norms-reasoning; retrieval-citation-grounding; agent-security-orchestration. `2ebef49a0b91` (Construction of Knowledge Graphs), `10828be135bf` (Finding fault), `73abf21862dc` (The adaptive nature of text-driven law), and `0421a1687b40` (ProVe) distinguish validity, transaction knowledge, release chronology, interpretation, and qualifier-sensitive claims. **What to do:** keep `goals/epistemic-bitemporal-edge-core` core-only. Document that `validAt` does not collapse applicability, efficacy, enforceability, or procedural effect, and `knownAt` does not mean observation, arrival, access, release, or model-trust time. Build legal multitemporality as a composed downstream layer.

5. **Stable identifiers do not settle semantic or real-world identity.** **Evidence weight:** high formal warning plus broad implementation evidence; sparse controlled identity evaluation. **Clusters:** memory-bitemporal; legal-ontology-design; legal-norms-reasoning; retrieval-citation-grounding; agent-metacognition-neurosymbolic. `d9e73d47d4a2` (Formal Foundations for RDF/S KB Evolution) exposes the unique-name assumption; `3c06bfc28d1d` (Classifications and the Law: Doctrinal Classifications vs. Computational Ontologies) and `2a9360ee7197` (Relating Knowledge Graphs To Logic, Language, and the World) reject timeless meaning; `e4c1e92b3477` (A Linked Term Bank of Copyright-Related Terms) contains a concrete hierarchy defect; and `d81e86e1d786` (IntKB) reports extensive entity ambiguity. **What to do:** keep `identity-iri-fold` syntactic and deterministic. Put equivalence, redirects, folds, and SKOS mappings in scoped, provenance-bearing, time-qualified, reversible records with negative fixtures proving that labels, translations, suffixes, shared predicates, and fuzzy scores cannot authorize identity.

6. **Legal semantics require cooperating modules, not one relation-centric or taxonomy-centric core.** **Evidence weight:** medium-high architectural convergence; weak comparative task evaluation. **Clusters:** legal-ontology-design; legal-norms-reasoning. `43f2c05c6d62` (A Comparison of Four Ontologies for the Design) finds procedure inadequately represented; `92e7a14f872b` (Empirically-Grounded Development of Legal Ontologies: a Socio-Legal Perspective) adds professional practice; `727fe68fabe6` (A Model of Legal Reasoning with Cases Incorporating) requires competing case theories; and `703aea161905` (Argumentation and Standards of Proof) requires proof-context evaluation. **What to do:** retain Hohfeldian relators as one legal-position module. Separately shape document/expression/interpretation/situation, norms and principles, procedure and practice, argumentation, and evaluation. Keep semantic-foundation M1 as the bounded SKOS registry/loader it already is.

7. **Contested evidence needs retained assertions plus context-indexed working views.** **Evidence weight:** high formal and conceptual support; little legal-scale evaluation. **Clusters:** memory-bitemporal; legal-ontology-design; legal-norms-reasoning; retrieval-citation-grounding. `5c2aeef6919d` (Ontology Revision as Non-Prioritized Belief Revision) permits inconsistent retained evidence and recoverable preferred views; `ad0622e993ee` (Reasoning About Reasoning in a Meta-Level Architecture) separates attributed and adopted belief; `f4ab0f7d6892` (Persuasion and Value in Legal Argument) and `90c15fe84620` (Persuasion in Practical Argument Using Value-based Argumentation Frameworks) make defeat and acceptance context-dependent. **What to do:** allow competing evidence-scoped assertions to coexist. Keep structural attacks immutable; derive defeats, preferred-view membership, and acceptance for a named evaluator, fact set, proof standard, value ordering, procedure, jurisdiction, and time. No-overlap should protect one logical lineage, not erase sourced disagreement.

8. **Retrieval quality is non-monotonic, and deterministic fusion proves mechanics rather than relevance.** **Evidence weight:** medium-high from controlled ablations and independent failure patterns; legal transfer remains unproved. **Clusters:** retrieval-citation-grounding; doc-structure-legal-nlp; legal-norms-reasoning; memory-bitemporal. `05afbbf3e1e9` (Should We Fine-Tune or RAG?) finds retrieved and oracle context can hurt; `0421a1687b40` (ProVe) exposes qualifier and natural-counterevidence failures; `3a636c39bbaa` (Kleister) exposes extraction and evidence-origin ambiguity; `4528245fb9e9` (Capturing Logical Structure) shows structural pruning can remove correct candidates; and `c8b554d0d336` (Argument Based Machine Learning Applied to Law) shows high aggregate accuracy can omit governing conditions. **What to do:** retain candidate-only RRF and literal priority, but add exact-but-irrelevant and wrong-entity boundary fixtures. Evaluate no-evidence/retrieved/oracle arms, noisy context, unseen evidence, natural contradictions, abstention, field/layout slices, exact-span/parent/document recall, and risk versus coverage.

9. **Agent safety is a property of the whole typed trajectory, including observed outcomes.** **Evidence weight:** medium—strong risk mechanisms and architectural convergence, weak end-to-end professional-runtime evidence. **Clusters:** agent-security-orchestration; agent-metacognition-neurosymbolic; legal-norms-reasoning. `caefce8b35a2` (LLM Agents can Autonomously Exploit One-day Vulnerabilities), `05d0a27d8629` (Secure-by-Default Guardrails for MCP-Based Tool Use), and `2ad7451c2819` (AGENT-FENCE) require principal-, resource-, sink-, capability-, and budget-aware control. `576ee59aaf2b` (Two Approaches to Implementing Metacognition) exposes monitor blindness, while `7ea169e822e0` (Toward Domain-Neutral Human-Level Metacognition) separates proposed recovery from observed success. **What to do:** add a typed `Observation → Proposal → Authorization → Attempt → Outcome → Disposition` lifecycle, causal ancestry, commitment revisions, budgets, egress checks, and decision-complete approval packets. Treat read-only access as potentially high risk when an egress sink exists.

10. **Validation, repair, and aggregate metrics need versioned operational governance.** **Evidence weight:** high formal and methodological support; limited legal-domain and scalability evidence. **Clusters:** memory-bitemporal; legal-ontology-design; doc-structure-legal-nlp; agent-metacognition-neurosymbolic; agent-security-orchestration. `d9e73d47d4a2` (Formal Foundations for RDF/S KB Evolution) shows repair can synthesize unsupported facts; `5943ec738200` (Declarative Repairing Policies for Curated KBs) retains exponential global search; `2b2bc2e2a40e` (Efficient Knowledge Graph Accuracy Evaluation) shows stale samples can preserve initial errors; `82458716569c` (VERSION ANALYSIS FOR FAULT DETECTION IN OWL ONTOLOGIES) adds release regression; `142d0583b149` (Performance Evaluation of Document Structure Extraction Algorithms) exposes component/pipeline gaps; and `0d06c1a2189a` (Prompt Engineering a Prompt Engineer) demonstrates metric-winning false shortcuts. **What to do:** version competency questions, samples, policies, reasoners, source snapshots, and verdict logic. Report asserted and entailed results, OWA and CWA outcomes, completeness/optimality status, oracle-conditioned versus full-pipeline metrics, coverage denominators, and refresh age.

## Cross-cluster patterns

### Independent convergence

- **Exact records below rebuildable semantics.** All seven clusters independently preserve source artifacts, exact spans, sourced assertions, decisions, messages, policy revisions, attempts, and outcomes beneath summaries, embeddings, ontologies, inferred closures, rankings, working views, and generated explanations.

- **Typed verdicts instead of one confidence or truth flag.** Anchor fidelity, provenance integrity, entailment, source authority, review disposition, temporal applicability, authorization, and release repeatedly fail independently. A scalar score or `verified` flag cannot safely collapse them.

- **Version and qualifier completeness are identity requirements.** Source version, jurisdiction, applicable time, procedural posture, actor role, polarity, scope, model/policy version, and coordinate space repeatedly determine whether two superficially identical records are actually the same assertion or evaluation.

- **Abstention and negative attempts are durable values.** `NO_CITATION`, insufficient evidence, ambiguity, unsupported logic, budget exhaustion, low-quality source, denied action, failed recovery, and rejected candidate should be recorded distinctly rather than collapsed into absence or deletion.

- **Human authority is scoped rather than magical.** Attorney review is consistently supported as the durable promotion and action seam, but the authoritative fact is that a named reviewer made a named decision for a purpose. Reviewer accuracy, disagreement, workload, and automation bias remain empirical questions.

- **Evaluation must follow the complete path.** Stage-local diagnostics are useful only when paired with end-to-end anchor recovery, semantic support, action success, abstention, coverage, latency, cost, and harm introduced by correction.

### Contradictions that must remain explicit

- **Retention versus forgetting and deletion.** `5c2aeef6919d` (Ontology Revision as Non-Prioritized Belief Revision) retains all sourced assertions, while memory-consolidation papers favor pruning and the security stream forbids durable raw-secret retention. The resolution is by storage class: retain in-policy exact evidence; prune projections; never persist prohibited secrets; apply explicit legal retention or key-destruction policy rather than semantic scores.

- **Exact text versus operative legal meaning.** Citation and document clusters make exact source bytes load-bearing, while `73abf21862dc` (The adaptive nature of text-driven law) and the legal-ontology cluster show that interpretation and institutional action also matter. Preserve source text and adopted interpretive or institutional acts as distinct exact records; treat their semantic consequences as scoped, contestable projections.

- **Open-world inference versus closed-world integrity.** `36d82e899e75` (Cross: An OWL Wrapper for Reasoning on Relational) demonstrates that an OWL model can satisfy a reference with a model-only witness, while SHACL and closed validation can detect missing records but cannot establish legal negation. Run and label both regimes; neither subsumes the other.

- **Deterministic replay versus order-independent acceptance.** `fc16fbd18148` (MemoryFlow) requires a reproducible event order, while `5c2aeef6919d` (Ontology Revision as Non-Prioritized Belief Revision) rejects arrival order as epistemic precedence. Preserve total order for history, but require permutation invariance where chronology has no substantive meaning.

- **Exact-literal priority versus exact-but-irrelevant evidence.** The current fusion goal intentionally gives exact phrases a deterministic candidate tier, while retrieval studies show exact co-occurrence can identify the wrong entity or relation. Candidate-only output reconciles the designs; literal priority must never imply support or authority.

- **More context versus selective context.** Some retrieval work favors always retrieving or consolidating evidence, while `05afbbf3e1e9` (Should We Fine-Tune or RAG?) and the security/orchestration cluster show that extra context can reduce quality or widen exposure. Keep no-context, retrieved, oracle, noisy, and selective arms rather than choosing a universal policy.

- **One supervisor versus one planner.** External metacognitive monitors offer separation but can be blind to omitted process state; integrated planners see more context but concentrate authority. Preserve logical monitoring/control contracts while leaving deployment topology open for repository-local comparison.

- **Complete audit versus confidentiality.** Memory and governance clusters seek complete exact telemetry, while secret scrubbing and matter walls prohibit raw-secret or cross-matter replication. Use scrubbed metadata-first events, protected source references, explicit coverage measures, and capability-checked access—not unrestricted prompt or payload logging.

- **Rich reasoning versus tractability.** Ontology and legal-reasoning papers favor expressive definitions, global repair, and argument evaluation, while reasoner studies show exponential search and degraded performance. Every operational profile needs a declared fragment, budget, completeness status, and escalation path.

- **Consensus versus professional authority.** Multi-agent and weighted-source systems use agreement as a signal, but copied sources, shared upstream errors, and agent unanimity cannot manufacture attorney authority or legal correctness.

## Delta vs the June-29 prior synthesis

### Genuinely new

- **A formal source-integrity counterexample.** `36d82e899e75` (Cross: An OWL Wrapper for Reasoning on Relational) shows precisely why open-world ontology consistency cannot certify authoritative record existence.

- **A layered verdict model.** The new corpus operationalizes the separation among exact anchor, proposition stance, source authority/currentness, reviewer disposition, action authorization, and release. The prior synthesis largely collapsed these into ClaimGate admission and verification status.

- **Retained inconsistent evidence plus recoverable preferred views.** `5c2aeef6919d` (Ontology Revision as Non-Prioritized Belief Revision) supplies conflict sets, view membership, rejection, and rescue without rewriting source assertions.

- **Exact-version memory operations.** `fc16fbd18148` (MemoryFlow) contributes distinct READ, USE, VERIFY, correction, supersedence, zombie-read, and observation-completeness obligations.

- **Documentary coordinate spaces beyond text.** `39933453659f` (DUDE) and `517d7e937747` (A Framework for the Encoding of Multilayered Documents) add page-space evidence, OCR lineage, multi-region anchors, overlapping views, and partial reading orders.

- **Ontology operations rather than ontology content alone.** Executable competency questions, asserted/entailed diagnostics, OWA/CWA separation, release regression, impact review, refreshable audits, and bounded repair are a substantial new operational layer.

- **Procedure, practice, and argumentation as independent modules.** `43f2c05c6d62` (A Comparison of Four Ontologies for the Design), `92e7a14f872b` (Empirically-Grounded Development of Legal Ontologies: a Socio-Legal Perspective), and `727fe68fabe6` (A Model of Legal Reasoning with Cases Incorporating) expose what a relator or taxonomy core does not cover.

- **Structural attack versus contextual defeat and acceptance.** The value-based argumentation papers make evaluator, audience, value order, fact set, proof standard, and time load-bearing rather than storing timeless accepted/rejected edges.

- **Trajectory-level agent governance.** Principal, capability, commitment revision, causal ancestry, retry budget, egress sink, model/prompt artifact, action attempt, observed outcome, and release state were largely absent from the prior ontology-centered synthesis.

- **Controlled retrieval evaluation.** No-evidence/retrieved/oracle arms, natural counterevidence, unseen-evidence partitions, noisy context, abstention calibration, field/layout slices, and exact-span/parent/document recall are new concrete prescriptions.

### Re-confirmed

- Exact source artifacts and immutable event records remain beneath semantic projections.
- Per-assertion provenance, source/version identity, append-only correction, and temporal qualification remain load-bearing.
- Schema-first modeling is appropriate for representation, validation shape, typed errors, and repo-owned vocabulary contracts.
- Legal ontology should be plural, modular, purpose-scoped, and aligned outward rather than copied wholesale.
- Stable identities must remain distinct from labels, mentions, aliases, and similarity.
- Graphs, inferred closures, retrieval packets, summaries, model state, and explanations remain rebuildable projections.
- Hybrid retrieval is preferable to a single channel, provided every channel remains candidate generation.
- Human review remains necessary for consequential legal interpretation and action.

### Contradicted or corrected

- **“The relation-centric camp won.”** Relators remain useful, but the corpus does not support them as the universal center of legal semantics. Procedure, principles, case theories, argument roles, and professional practice remain independent requirements.

- **ClaimGate admission creates an institutional fact.** Structural admission can establish repository workflow status. It cannot establish substantive truth, source support, legal correctness, or institutional authority absent a separately recorded authorized act.

- **The four-state lifecycle is a burden-of-proof ladder.** Lifecycle and proof standard can vary independently; evidentiary sufficiency requires claim-specific meta-level evaluation.

- **Schema is the single source of truth for types, persistence, and ontology.** Schema may own representation grammar and repo-owned vocabulary definitions. It does not own sourced facts, real-world identity, historical ontology releases, adopted interpretations, or task-specific legal meaning.

- **A stable IRI or `skos:exactMatch` provides straightforward equivalence.** Equivalence-like mappings require scope, source, jurisdiction, version, evidence, reviewer, intentional non-equivalences, and reversible revision.

- **A half-open character span is broadly sufficient evidence.** It is sufficient only for text-bearing evidence in a named coordinate space. Visual and spatial evidence requires a separately verified anchor type.

- **Grounded quote or SHACL conformance is analogous to admissibility.** Exact location and shape validity do not establish claim entailment, authority, or legal sufficiency.

- **Work/expression identity alone is the citation target.** Reproducible citation needs both a semantic target and an immutable item or manifestation plus exact span.

- **OWL/Oxigraph can be source of truth.** Graph stores and reasoners may be authoritative for their own stored schema or computation record, but derived legal semantics remain projections over exact sources and events.

- **UFO-L supplies controlled comprehension evidence.** The deep read of `04cbf7a69b5e` (UFO-L: A Core Ontology of Legal Concepts Built) identifies it as a proposal without completed experiments; this corpus cannot corroborate the prior empirical claim.

- **The verified publication gate already exists.** The current repo audit found no provenance-aware publication gate in the live ClaimGate/projection path. `@beep/ontology` now exists, while the prior `Ontology.create` roadmap and many code examples are superseded or defective.

## Per-stream impact

### Memory-architecture / No-Escape

**What changes:** clarify that the exact event/evidence corpus is outside the semantic-cache lifecycle even when semantic indexes are built over it. Separate schema-valid, provenance-valid, span-verified, entailment-checked, authority-assessed, accepted, and attorney-approved states. Add exact-version READ/USE/VERIFY telemetry and distinguish deterministic replay order from epistemic precedence.

**What is validated:** C3—semantic retrieval is a suggestion mechanism—is reinforced across all clusters. The bitemporal goal’s immutable payloads, half-open axes, durable rejection, atomic close-and-insert, lineage, and canonical as-of reads are supported by `2ebef49a0b91` (Construction of Knowledge Graphs), `5c2aeef6919d` (Ontology Revision as Non-Prioritized Belief Revision), and `10828be135bf` (Finding fault). The corpus does not independently validate the No-Escape theorem’s particular numeric thresholds.

**Concrete next steps:** extend `standards/memory-architecture/01-memory-layer-taxonomy.md`; add a boundary fixture in `goals/epistemic-bitemporal-edge-core` proving competing evidence-scoped lineages coexist; route preferred-view revision, legal multitemporality, exact-version telemetry, and write governance to follow-ons rather than widening the core.

### Semantic-foundation & identity

**What changes:** treat each ontology module as a purpose- and commitment-bearing artifact with jurisdiction, authority basis, logic profile, OWA/CWA assumptions, negation policy, version, competency questions, and applicability limits. Treat semantic mappings as explicit records outside the deterministic fold. Do not assume that M1 can grow directly into legal procedure or argumentation.

**What is validated:** the M1 repo-owned SKOS registry/loader, vetted external alignment, graph-as-projection boundary, no graph-store/SPARQL commitment, and deterministic tuple fold remain sound. `36d82e899e75` (Cross: An OWL Wrapper for Reasoning on Relational) strengthens the source boundary, while `e6bc0ec90155` (Formalising Ontologies and Their Relations) supports explicit shared models and mappings.

**Concrete next steps:** attach negative identity fixtures to `goals/identity-iri-fold`; attach executable competency questions and separately labeled asserted/entailed and OWA/CWA diagnostics to `goals/semantic-foundation` without widening M1; extend `explorations/legal-ontology-landscape` with module manifests; route lifecycle QA, curation governance, legal procedure, argumentation, and inference policy to separate explorations.

### Hybrid-retrieval & citation grounding

**What changes:** define the missing follow-on as `QualifierCompleteAssertion → anchored candidates → relevance → per-span stance → aggregate assessment → reviewer disposition`. Preserve `supports | refutes | insufficient-evidence | qualifier-mismatch` independently from authority and approval. Add semantic and immutable citation targets, natural counterevidence, abstention, source-dependence, and no/retrieved/oracle evaluation.

**What is validated:** the fusion core’s deterministic candidate-only output, exposed contributions, span preservation, and mandatory downstream gate are correct. The verified-span goal is stricter than the reviewed systems and should retain exact raw equality, local-only privileged text, source digest/version, ambiguity failure, and negative attempts. `0421a1687b40` (ProVe) provides the strongest direct support for separating anchor fidelity from proposition support.

**Concrete next steps:** make RRF explicitly non-epistemic in boundary tests; attach stage-preserving mention, anchor, identity-candidate, ambiguity, and argument-role records to the citation engine; extend the citation-grounding exploration for stance assessment; benchmark retrieval with noisy, absent, contradictory, superseded, unseen, entity-dense, and layout-sensitive evidence; shape composite visual anchors only after the locked text slice.

### Law-doc structure

**What changes:** treat raw text as one versioned coordinate space derived from an immutable parent artifact. Future structure should preserve source regions, OCR mappings, physical grouping, partial reading order, logical sections, functional/legal roles, and cross-references as separate views. A tree and flattened text remain consumer projections, not the only document structure.

**What is validated:** the current Office Action goal’s narrow paired rule, local deterministic execution, exact source identity, typed abstention, atomic pair emission, rule-version persistence, and no-LLM-escalation posture are well supported. `4528245fb9e9` (Capturing Logical Structure), `142d0583b149` (Performance Evaluation of Document Structure Extraction Algorithms), and `355af156cd8c` (Robust Document Image Understanding Technologies) reinforce stage attribution and source lineage.

**Concrete next steps:** add full-pipeline eligible/emitted/abstained/correct denominators and split/merge/miss/false-alarm diagnostics to the current goal; audit the file-processing manifest for artifact digest, producer version, coordinate mapping, quality, alternatives, and dependent revalidation; keep multi-view Office Action layout, parser selection, and partial-order structure in follow-on exploration and benchmark packets.

### Agent control plane & security

**What changes:** split content admission, evidence verification, human disposition, action authorization, and output release. Add typed capabilities, principal and matter scopes, resource/sink-aware read policy, commitment revisions, budgets, causal parents, outgoing-argument checks, post-action outcomes, and model/prompt artifact admission. Preserve logical monitor/controller separation while leaving external-supervisor versus integrated-planner deployment open.

**What is validated:** candidate-only writes, professional approval for consequential actions, local-first confidentiality, pre-prompt secret scrubbing, exact event history, and graph-as-projection remain sound. The correction is that `safeForPrompt` authorizes only prompt construction and attorney acceptance authorizes only a scoped runtime decision.

**Concrete next steps:** revise approval language; add expected effect, deadline, success/failure predicates, action attempt, and observed disposition to the runtime; prove `safeForPrompt` cannot satisfy an action-authorization type; extend the agent-governance exploration with trajectory envelopes, commitment versions, typed performatives, independent certification, budgets, and rejected-attempt history; separately shape model admission, sandboxing, and assurance regression.

## Honest limits

- The master synthesis consumes seven cluster reports and their inherited deep-read judgments; it does not independently reopen all 185 source papers.

- Selection was relevance-driven, not random. T1 routed 185 papers to deep read, 93 to “maybe,” and 165 to catalog-only. Misrouting can therefore hide both contrary evidence and useful niche mechanisms.

- The tier distribution—42 gold, 125 silver, 15 bronze, and 3 dross—measures repository usefulness, not methodological quality. Much of the gold evidence is architectural rather than empirical.

- Apparent corroboration is inflated by related publication lineages: LKIF, CLO/D&S, interactive ontology revision, value-based argumentation, metacognitive-control, neural-symbolic, OTIP, IDJ, Dolores, and other families reuse systems, examples, or formal cores.

- No paper validates the complete beep-effect target. Attorney studies, inter-annotator reliability, USPTO-specific retrieval and procedure, privileged matter walls, late-event concurrency, privacy-preserving deletion, adversarial poisoning, source dependence, production cost, and end-to-end action safety remain thin or absent.

- Many evaluations use synthetic negatives, tiny hand-modeled cases, random rather than document-family splits, oracle upstream components, positive-only samples, old OCR systems, general-domain corpora, or unreported thresholds. Their mechanisms are useful; their reported percentages and release thresholds are not portable.

- The corpus corroborates the No-Escape authority boundary but does not independently replicate its mathematical derivation or its specific competitor, dimensionality, compression, or phase-transition constants.

- Academia.edu files lack reliable canonical URLs and sometimes expose truncated titles. The twelve-character ids are corpus content-hash identifiers, not DOI or bibliographic authority.

- The June-29 reading map still holds an approximately 240-paper backlog: 97 legal-NLP/extraction-KG, 50 other-relevant, 22 methodology/tooling, 22 legal-core ontology, 15 DRM/licensing, 15 deontic/rules/contracts, 13 IP/patent ontology, and six thin-tail papers. This July corpus overlaps some themes but did not systematically discharge those entries. The 97-paper ingestion edge remains the largest likely source of additional empirical retrieval and extraction evidence.

- The routing priorities are synthesis judgments for align-stage review. No route, scope change, doctrine edit, or implementation is executed by this artifact.
