# Brief

## Problem

The repository and downloaded research accumulated overlapping claims, dated
release watches and cross-domain analogies without a complete local evidence map.
URLs alone hide cited versions, incomplete captures, publication failures and
repeated reports. A summary made before resolving those references could promote
unread evidence, recreate completed capabilities or turn a benchmark analogy into
an unsupported product claim.

## Appetite

One exhaustive pass over the frozen original intake: 143 files, including 142
readable documents and an empty control file; 18 nightly packets and 18 downloaded
reports. Acquire or explicitly dispose every reference before synthesis. Read all
input documents and inspect primary methods/limits for substantive conclusions.
Additional discovery is allowed only for recorded evidence gaps. Deliver the
maintained local library and a completely routed exploration, then stop before
implementing newly graduated goals. Follow-on appetites are bounded separately:
three implementation days for the OA fixture, two research days for remote skill
conformance, and small evidence amendments within existing owner phases.

## Solution sketch

The external library separates source identity, cited version/capture, artifact
bytes and citation occurrence. Immutable input/object storage and schema-first
Effect CLI commands produce reproducible, portable Markdown and static HTML
navigation. Every capture has retained provenance and a current readable or
reviewed disposition. Grok Build uses the actual deep-research workflow and X
Search; missing returned X bodies remain incomplete evidence.

Four reading ledgers reconcile all original documents, claims, sections and source
responsibilities. The resulting 461 findings separate attributed assertions,
verified observations and proposed applications. Primary evidence checks and a
fresh capability inventory favor existing components. Independent admission and
isolated ADHD frames select one ready/paused OA consumer goal and one parked remote
skill child; workflow, outbox, effectiveness and authority receive explicit
amendments/re-entry evidence. The routing catalog gives every other finding a
reason and concrete reconsideration trigger.

## Rabbit holes

- A successful acquisition request can contain a failed target, paywall intro,
  abstract, incomplete pagination or an interpretation. Validate the target and
  preserve human corrections instead of equating provider success with reading.
- Source existence is not semantic support; neither supplies attorney authority.
- Cancellation and absent observations do not prove remote effects were undone.
- Paper attack rates, synthetic tests and performance gains depend on their
  harnesses, denominators and versions. Transfer them only as bounded experiments.
- Current main moves during long research. Pin each capability check and reconcile
  new completed work before adding scope; preserve historical observations.
- Source selection, native host selection and production composition are different
  readiness gates. Park unresolved research instead of inventing an implementation.

## No-gos

No edits to original reports or the nightly single-writer ledger; no external repo
builds, scripts, recursive submodules or automatic LFS payloads; no default video
archiving; no new spending; no raw corpus/client/secret material in the public
repository; no new graph/store/runtime merely from market news; no legal holding
inferred from an unread order; no lifecycle reset of completed work; no execution
of newly graduated goal packets within this exploration.
