## D11 — Accounted evidence and readable evidence remain distinct

**Decision.** Phase 1 accepts either verified readable evidence or an explicit,
identity-bound reviewed disposition for every cited version. The review may be
unavailable, ambiguous, incomplete, or tool-blocked; internal, operational, and
non-reference records retain their own accounting. A raw failure does not count
as a review. Required route qualification and artifact integrity still must pass.

**Reason.** This is the approved intake boundary. The initial verifier accidentally
allowed only the unavailable/internal/non-reference subset, which would make
the explicitly incomplete X transport evidence impossible to account for. Source
reading admission remains stricter than intake accounting: no unread or
incomplete evidence can support an affirmative synthesis conclusion.

**Reversal.** Change the documented intake contract and its regression tests
together; never relabel unknown evidence as unavailable or readable to clear
a gate. The original reviewed receipts remain immutable.
