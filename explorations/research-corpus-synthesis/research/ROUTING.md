## Retention and validation boundary

Historical vendor/release/market assertions remain individual parked rows. Revisit requires the exact dated primary source and a named consumer comparison; acquisition, funding or publication alone does not justify a new store or runtime. Explicit reader coverage relations yield existing-coverage, not same-fact deduplication. Rejected scope/false-authority proposals retain their objections; superseded rows retain historical claims and current counterevidence rather than deleting originals.

Every row has a final disposition, actual retained artifact target, original reader proposal/reason, evidence IDs and a reconsideration/intake condition. Artifact targets fall back to this catalog while selected packets are not yet present; that fallback is disclosed rather than inventing a file. Source-level evidence bindings remain in each reader finding and the mechanically validated synthesis. Routing validation verifies coverage/links/bindings and preserved proposals; it cannot establish semantic truth or owner approval.
