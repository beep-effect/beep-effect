# Reading 3: evidence-bound reuse across legal work, skills and runtime

This reading covers all 43 assigned immutable documents: six complete nightly packets (28–31 August, 10 September, 2 October) and six downloaded reports (9, 10, 23, 29 September, 3 and 6 October). Every document was displayed and read in bounded chunks, including claims, refutations, run metadata, suggested actions and source indexes. The ledgers account for all 118 nightly claims, 416 Markdown sections and 405 assigned source responsibilities. Fifteen primary sources received selected method/results/limitations or repository-documentation reading, and one secondary article received context reading. One primary reading is supplementary to another reader's responsibility. These counts describe inspection, not independent benchmark reproduction or complete reading of every acquired body.

The 108 findings separate 23 cross-report mechanisms from dated source assertion branches and exact-URL duplicates. A readable acquired source with no deep inspection is explicitly `background-not-deep-read`. An incomplete source, X record or tool failure stays an `unread-gap`; local symbols and operational pointers are `non-evidence`. The five JSONL ledgers preserve exact identifiers and evidence hashes; the conclusions below reference their finding IDs.

## Primary evidence changes the reports' interpretation

**Advisory semantic audit is different from deterministic citation binding.** GANDR v1 commits most runs when a structural protocol passes, leaving the Critic advisory. Its authors explicitly do not attribute accuracy gains to the audit machinery; changing to a strict Critic gate degraded its particular benchmark. That cannot justify bypassing an attorney gate or an exact-source check. ProvenanceGuard v3 reinforces the distinction: the locked multi-source rerun reports reject/block F1 .846 but exact source-plus-relation accuracy .229. Its repair passing its own verifier is not independent legal correctness. R3-001 cites the retained GANDR artifact `7f66b928…` at lines281–293 and470–484, and ProvenanceGuard `d76b3363…` at1030–1320.

**High retrieval agreement depends on what is measured.** NormasTCU v1 reports functional rank equivalence for some nDCG/MRR judgments, while P@10 and R@10 rankings diverge materially. QUEST v1 finds experts stricter on whether an excerpt actually addresses credibility, as distinct from topic relevance. They concern Brazilian norms and Danish asylum material respectively. R3-006 proposes separate relevance, attribution and legal-construct labels, not an interchangeable judge or a transfer claim about U.S. prosecution. Evidence: `c93f0b68…`, lines1100–1462; `b703fb9c…`, lines460–875.

**Task utility cannot stand in for permission.** APEX v1 shows malicious selected actions alongside relatively preserved native task test-pass rates, and distinguishes the work-loop resource endpoint from discrete action success. Its monolithic-versus-chain comparison changes more than record sharing, so it does not isolate a universal causal effect. The useful transfer is an original-action-bound invocation check, including exact arguments and expiry, exercised across a skill handoff. Evidence: `c0295dd3…`, lines275–789, R3-003. PACE v1 distinguishes its certified design from evaluated fast paths, describes final-argument recheck and trusted hook premises, and places durable one-shot crash reconciliation outside the evaluated artifact. R3-004 uses those obligations as a comparison checklist against existing authority, not a second permission implementation. Evidence: `5a43f04d…`, lines360–526.

**The captured harness revision corrects the historical headline.** Scanning the Harness v3 reports 409/2,660 setups (15.4%) with selected dependency-selection or execution-preapproval exposures; the six-category union is475/2,660 (17.9%). These are corpus proportions, not exploit likelihood, incident prevalence or measured recall. Unflagged cases were not sufficiently evaluated to establish absence of defects. Versioned client semantics changed earlier counts, including a documented permission exception. R3-003 retains the original16% assertion as historical and the current primary scope separately. Evidence: `abcc1e92…`, lines303–656.

**End-to-end legal research is stricter than an average rubric score.** Legal Research Bench v1 reports 413 expert tasks across eight areas, none patent-specific, with mandatory criteria and citation URL validity. ## Mechanism transfer candidates, with bounds

The highest-value combination is a candidate record that binds an original source locator, an exact supporting span, a semantic support assessment, a matter and an authorized reviewer decision. R3-001 proposes 20 positive/negative consumer cases over one output type in two days. Wrong matter, wrong patient, topically similar support and nonexistent intrinsic-record locators must remain withheld. The dependency is the existing span substrate plus an attorney-authored rubric; a second model's approval adds no authority.

R3-008 proposes a two-day oversight experiment over ten agent runs using existing receipt and anchor fields. Evidence-Grounded Behavior Graphs builds deterministic visible-context behavior with original source locations; most, not all, model conditions improve. A legal oversight view should expose what action actually changed and where its evidence came from, while keeping direct source inspection available. This is an untested transfer, not a request for another memory service. Evidence: `32b2e945…`, lines630–1016.

R3-009 takes PrisMem's capability specialists and paired differential regression witnesses into evaluation of one reusable research skill. Its five memory components help locate the failing stage, but executable memory evolution must not auto-promote legal facts or change permission policy. An improved average cannot conceal a regression in citation, temporal attribution, matter isolation or abstention. Evidence: `d259b748…`, lines100–354.

R3-010 adapts evidence-responsive stopping to retrieval resource use. Judged Useless, Queried Anyway distinguishes judgment, belief and action in controlled HotpotQA/FEVER failures; its enforced five-result rule is not a universal threshold. A legal consumer should stop calling a persistently unhelpful route and return missing evidence, rather than answer from unsupported memory. Recoverable failures should continue. The proposed one-day fixture tests those two cases and budget doubling. Evidence: `cace6b8d…`, lines15–360.

R3-011 adapts local change sensors to the existing USPTO read driver. The pinned Sweden repository describes a five-item unbackfillable RSS window, observed-version-only supersession, unpinned unknown state, local client-sensitive ledgers and coverage-qualified empty search answers. The report's store-nothing shorthand does not mean no local state. A higher version or watch hit is a candidate for lawyer review, not proof of its significance. Evidence: pinned README `7211d774…`, lines1–239.

Closed-unmerged audit proposals do not erase useful local ledger guarantees; visible trajectory watermarking does not establish authorization (R3-019).

Incomplete X material cannot affirm ambient capture APIs, prompt-retention duties, competitor features or legal holdings. Historical model preferences are not current approved routing. R3-021 limits workstation/ambient transfers to consent, scoped authority, rollback and retained evidence, with no hardware or service purchase.
