(R2-005–009, R2-026, R2-032–033, R2-043–045, R2-057). Benchmark criterion-level
scores, all-criteria pass rates, retained lawyer edits, model answers and patent

SpecHarness governs only grounded monitorable obligations; subjective or
ambiguous requirements remain advisory. Its results include feedback and repair,
so they do not isolate commitment at matched compute. SWE-Gate independently
checks review constraints after functional repair. The recorded 221/644 hidden
failures concern its specific Python benchmark, not current Codex performance
(R2-024, R2-041, R2-070).

URI, content set and separate execution authority. The inspected SEP explicitly
states that unsigned hashes prove consistency with a server’s listing, not trust
in the server or a gateway. Changed resource sets revoke content-bound approval;
remote cached bytes do not graduate to filesystem-skill trust; nested activation
and cross-origin reads have separate consent requirements. The retained SEP also
includes optional directory-read semantics, so the reports’ shorthand of “three
methods” must not be used as a complete current host contract (R2-018, R2-059).
