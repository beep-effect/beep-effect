# Research corpus synthesis reflection — 2026-10-06

This reflection closes the exploration's intake and reasoning work. It does not
claim completion of the newly admitted goal, the parked child, or hosted PR
verification. Publication evidence belongs to the final closeout record.

## Summary

The durable result is a version-aware external source library and a fully routed
exploration: 461 findings produce one ready goal, one parked child exploration,
and amendments or re-entry evidence for existing owners. The original report
corpus was read completely; background external sources have explicit reading
limits. The strongest improvements came from separating facts that a tempting
summary would merge: retrieval versus reading, source existence versus support,
service acceptance versus delivery, and schema presence versus consumer behavior.

## Tooling experience

Immutable originals, exact hashes, caption provenance, ordinary pinned clones,
and separate GitHub discussion evidence made disputed readings recoverable.
Source/report navigation and an embedded-data HTML interface made a large corpus
usable without a server. Parallel readers were useful only because their assigned
units, exact source ranges, and final routing were reconciled centrally.

Real qualification mattered. Grok's socket-inspection failure needed an outer
read-only namespace while retaining its own protections. Workflow completion and
X Search invocation could be proven, but ACP events still omitted complete post
bodies. The X sources consequently remain incomplete. Firecrawl provider success
also required target-status and actual-body validation. Caption fallback needed
raw timedtext and track identity, not just plausible transcript prose.

A late human reading exposed a status defect: any historical readable capture
could mask a later incomplete disposition. The shared selection rule now follows
validated catalog append order per requested revision, while retaining historical
integrity checks. Regression tests cover review override, later reacquisition,
version isolation and corrupt evidence. This was a current-state defect, not a
reason to edit or discard captured originals.

## Implementation improvement opportunities

- Keep source identity, requested version, capture, artifact and occurrence
  separate in future consumers. A source-level URL cache loses historical intent.
- Reuse the effective-evidence selector across acquisition, verification, status
  and rendering. Independent policies drift even when each passes local tests.
- Keep session-owned provider imports strict and byte-bound. Provider execution
  metadata and generated interpretations are valuable, but have different uses
  from original source text.
- Preserve original denominators when discovery grows the collection. A growing
  catalog must not silently change what “all reports read” means.

Fresh-main integration exposed a second kind of drift: outbox implementation and
new decisions landed while its research amendment was being reviewed. Preserving
upstream lifecycle progress and historical v1 audit semantics was more important
than retaining the amendment's original decision number. D-28 now names the
remaining runtime work, with a commit-bound capability delta. Future corpus work
should schedule a final owner/source refresh before publication.

## Goal and prompt critique

The approved two-phase boundary prevented early recommendations from outrunning
acquisition. A future intake should budget reconciliation and hostile/partial
responses explicitly: they accounted for much more work than URL extraction.
Completeness should mean every finding has a reasoned disposition, not that every
interesting paper becomes a new implementation goal. Most historical findings
remain parked with an evidence or ownership trigger.

## Lessons

**High confidence:** preserve failed attempts and later corrections; bind every
claim to the evidence that actually exists; distinguish accountably unread from
readable; route to current owners before creating another goal.

**Medium confidence:** immutable snapshots plus small executable reconciliation
receipts scale better than a single narrative source list. Exact-location reading
records make independent review more useful than broad “reviewed source” labels.

**Low confidence:** domain analogies may improve agent harnesses and legal review,
but the proposed transfer still needs bounded experiments. The ADHD designs are
hypotheses, not validated product behavior.
