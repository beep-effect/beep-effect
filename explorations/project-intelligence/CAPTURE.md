## 2026-07-14 — Research distillation

### Product loop

Turn a bounded watchlist into current, evidence-backed landscape intelligence:
discover/admit sources, acquire immutable snapshots, extract non-authoritative
observations, ground candidate claims in exact evidence, deduplicate stable
identities, assess relevance, and publish a disposable daily brief. Every
rendered assertion must resolve to a claim, evidence span, source snapshot,
lifecycle state, and comparison window. “No supported change found” is a valid
result when its searched scope is explicit.

The smallest proof is offline and deterministic: a schema-versioned explicit
GitHub watchlist, fixed fixtures and clock, stable IDs/hashes/order, lifecycle-
bearing claims, and byte-equivalent rebuild of a Markdown brief. It is not a
crawler, scheduler, UI, autonomous code-change loop, or live vendor adoption.

### Findings to carry

- **Authority stays repo-owned.** Sources, immutable snapshots, claims,
  evidence, provenance, terms, and lifecycle are authoritative. Observations,
  assessments, briefs, indexes, graph/vector views, vaults, and MCP responses
  are rebuildable projections.
- **Relational first, projections later.** A repo-native relational authority
  best fits exact spans, lifecycle, deterministic fixtures, and reversible
  projections. Cognee, Graphiti/Zep, TrustGraph, and mem0 were useful donors or
  possible later projections, not acceptable first-proof authority.
- **Identity survives change.** Resolve provider coordinates to stable source
  identity; record aliases and terms revisions; content-address snapshots;
  reserve tombstoned identities; make license withdrawal, source removal,
  quarantine, purge, and dependent-claim support transitions explicit.
- **Rebuild is a contract.** Rebuild uses only surviving authoritative records,
  recorded versions, tombstones, and a fixed clock. It must not refetch or ask
  a model, reuse purged identities, or resurrect withdrawn evidence.
- **Untrusted input is data.** Source text never becomes an instruction, tool
  argument, executable markup, or secret-bearing log. Boundaries produce typed
  redact, quarantine, or render-safe outcomes; oversized, malformed,
  unattributed, secret-shaped, or scriptable content fails closed.
- **Admission is manual first.** A versioned watchlist file is decoded at the
  boundary, normalized, deduplicated after identity resolution, and recorded
  in run provenance. Discovery, source-growth automation, and scheduled
  reconciliation remain later approval-gated work.
- **Research themes were broader than the first proof.** High-signal areas were
  agent memory/context, knowledge graphs/ontologies, agent frameworks/MCP,
  Effect techniques, legal AI, and knowledge/research systems. A small public
  seed watchlist was preferable to pretending a sampled personal corpus was a
  complete taxonomy.
- **Mine prototypes selectively.** Promote deterministic IDs, typed plans,
  bounded concurrency, catalog/idempotency intent, and explicit dry-run/doctor
  patterns. Retire browser-history heuristics, operator clone scans, ad hoc
  Notion payload traversal, and source-URL-as-version assumptions. Defer
  Cognee/Graphiti emission and unattended scheduling until authority and
  provenance are proven.
